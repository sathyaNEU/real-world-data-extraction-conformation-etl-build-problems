## Tried and rejected

- Counting unit, weather-office episodes against the treaty's 72-hour occurrence: the occurrence is a filed definition, so counting on it is a definition read; building storm systems from episodes by space-time adjacency is either trivial (systems well apart) or forks the main call on the linkage gap, which is why `grain_or_unit` carries the guard's strategy-risk warning.
- The hours clause's choice of when each 72 hours begins: executing the clause to the reinsured's best advantage is rule application.
- Peril scope (convective damage tied to a tropical cyclone falls under the named-storm cover): a filed exclusion executed with the track file, rule application.
- As-if normalisation for exposure growth, national against county: once the estimand files losses at 2023 exposure and prices, county-level normalisation is a strong reader's default with county damage and county housing series in the pack.
- The large-loss count offset by total exposure (the client's frequency card 1) against per-loss as-if normalisation: the estimand that makes as-if right is a definition read, and worded per unit of exposure it forks.
- Exceedance frequency smoothed through a Pareto tail (the count above a reporting threshold times the fitted survival at USD 100 million) against the raw count above USD 100 million: both estimate the same expected frequency, so the choice is a variance trade the data cannot settle, an open family on Gate C.
- A research memo arguing the near-doubling: a memo written to carry the wrong answer is a decoy, banned as the decisive organ.
- A catalogue truncated at a nominal reporting threshold that as-if growth lifts above the attachment: the storm event records carry damage with no threshold, so the truncation never binds on the solver's source.
