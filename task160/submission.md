## 1. Final Recommendation

**The pool should block-book 600 agency RN shifts for the four weeks from Monday 4 January to Sunday 31 January 2027.** All of it is the eight North members' surge wave, which came five to six weeks after the Southeast's in every closed winter and this winter falls inside the block weeks, while the sixteen southern members' waves began in early December and end before them. Not the five closed Januaries' average draw of nearly 2,900 shifts, which reads January by the calendar when the Southeast's surge moved by five weeks from winter to winter; not none, the pool's own draw in the same weeks after each closed winter's peak, which never held a North wave because the North joined the pool on 1 November 2026.

1. Expected draw of the sixteen Southeast and West and Central members over the block weeks: **none**.
2. The North's wave falls **wholly inside** the block weeks, in mid-January.
3. Expected draw of the eight North members over the block weeks: **600** shifts.

6. Carried the closed winters' lag from this winter's Southeast spells: the North's wave falls in mid-January, wholly inside the four block weeks.
7. Averaged the North's five closed winters' waves at its members' staffing per level: 600 shifts to the nearest 50, the pool's expected draw over the block weeks (clause 5.2), so the block is 600 shifts.
