# task269 design note

Architecture 2, re-rooted 2026-10-19 (wave 4, Descriptive lane, sathay3012) after architecture 1 was spent at stage 4 on Gate F (`## Tried and rejected`). Callowfield University's School of Medicine runs its final-year objective structured clinical examination (OSCE) on a blueprint that draws the same number of stations from each of four skill domains every year (history taking, physical examination, communication, procedural skills). In 2026 the 84 final-year candidates rotated through 12 stations, three from each domain, in one circuit, and the same two examiners marked every candidate at each station. In 2027 Fennabeg Clinical School's first graduate-entry cohort joins the examination, which about doubles it, and the faculty cannot double-staff three circuits, so each station will have one examiner in each of three parallel circuits at three sites. The school's assessment policy requires the final OSCE's total mark to rank candidates with a reliability of at least 0.80, and the head of assessment notifies the accreditation board of the 2027 blueprint. The question is how many stations the 2027 sitting needs.

## Default path

1. Read the assessment policy: the final OSCE's total mark (the sum of the station marks, each out of 20) must rank candidates with a reliability of at least 0.80, the stations drawn from the case bank each year and the examiners from the faculty's register; the blueprint draws the same number of stations from each of the four domains.
2. Read the 2027 delivery plan: one examiner per station in each of three parallel circuits, examiners allocated by site and session, the same stations in every circuit, at most 18 stations at a site.
3. Build each candidate's 2026 station marks from the item-level export: the two days' codings reconciled, the checklist item the board withdrew after day 1 removed from every candidate's mark, items marked not applicable out of the denominator, each station on 20.
4. Fit the 2026 marks' variance components with the candidates crossed with the examiners nested in the stations, the stations taken as drawn from the bank (candidate 1.06, station 0.67, candidate by station 1.97, examiner within station 1.06, residual 1.08).
5. Decision study for 2027: one examiner a station, different candidates meeting different examiners, so the stringency is error with the candidate-by-station variance and the residual; the smallest number of stations reaching 0.80 is the blueprint.

Ends on: 16 stations, the textbook decision study (0.80 first reached at 16; 0.81 there), or, one step earlier, 12 stations on single-examiner alpha (0.80 on the lead examiners' marks, 0.81 on the second examiners') stepped by Spearman-Brown.
Golden: 13 stations (0.81; 12 stations fall short at 0.79), once each candidate's strength in a domain, which recurs in every form the blueprint draws, is counted as true score and the examiner's stringency as error.

## Stump sentence

The default path files 16 stations, because it decomposes the 2026 marks as if each year's stations were drawn from one undivided bank, so the part of a candidate's performance that belongs to a domain (strong communication, weaker procedures) lands in the candidate-by-station error; the blueprint draws the same number of stations from each of the four domains every year, so that domain strength recurs in every form and belongs to what the total mark measures, and only the decomposition with the domains as a fixed facet, the examiner's stringency still counted as error for single examining, puts the 2027 examination at 13.

## Three rules

Task type: psychometrics (Education & Learning Sciences), which serves Descriptive & Distribution Analysis: how the variance of a clinical examination's marks is distributed across candidates, domains, stations, examiners and their interactions, and what share of the total mark's variance is real difference between candidates under a given blueprint and examining arrangement.
Domain test: the deciding quantity is the reliability of the 2027 final OSCE's total mark, a generalizability study of the 2026 dual-examined marks with the blueprint's domains as a facet (candidate, candidate by domain, case specificity within domain, examiner stringency within station, residual) recombined in a decision study for one examiner a station on a blueprint that fixes the domains, the field's own analysis of a clinical examination's reliability; replacing it with the pooled decision study that treats the stations as one undivided bank files 16 stations where the blueprint needs 13.
Swap: "how many stations the examination needs on a blueprint fixing the stations per skill domain, for its total mark to rank candidates at 0.80 when each candidate meets one examiner a station" with another field's nouns (megawatt-hours for marks, meters for stations, a utility's daily reads for candidates) has no skill domain whose candidate-specific strength a fixed blueprint carries into every form, no case specificity and no examiner, so the decomposition and the task stop making sense.
Layer 1: the examination system's item-level export runs over both days in one file, but the system's overnight update between the days changed its coding: day 1 rows carry the station number, the examiner's badge and the mark as a label, day 2 rows the case code, the staff number and the mark as a digit, linked through the station sheet and the examiner allocation; the global grades come in their own export; the board of examiners withdrew one ambiguous checklist item after day 1, as its minutes record; items are marked not applicable when the simulated patient did not give the cue; checklists run from 14 to 22 items and each station is scaled to 20; each is how an OSCE system and an examinations office keep their records, nothing placed to be caught.
Layer 2: field_method, the generalizability study with the domains as a facet and its decision study on the fixed blueprint: the candidate-by-domain variance is true score when every form draws the same domains; the pooled study that treats the stations as one bank books it as error and flips the blueprint from 13 stations to 16.
Decisive step: estimating the 2026 marks' variance components with the stations nested in the blueprint's domains and, in the decision study for 2027, counting the candidate-by-domain variance (weighted by the domains' shares of the stations) as part of what the total mark measures, because policy 4.2 draws the same number of stations from each domain every year, while the examiner's stringency, the case specificity and the residual are error.
Not semantics: every field read correctly (the day codings reconciled, the withdrawn item removed, the not-applicable items handled, the stations' domains read off the station sheet, policy 4.2's equal numbers read and applied to the blueprint) still leaves the pooled decision study at 16 stations, because the error is in what the fixed blueprint does to the variance decomposition, which no field, code or definition states.
Not rule application: the policy defines what is measured (the total mark's reliability for ranking candidates, stations drawn from the case bank each year under the blueprint and examiners from the register, a standard of 0.80) and the delivery plan the arrangement; no rule or procedure says how reliability is estimated or which variance a fixed blueprint makes true score, and executing every stated rule with the textbook decision study files 16 stations.
Analyst test: a strong analyst who reads every file and the policy correctly fits the 2026 marks with the stations and the examiners random from one bank, counts the examiner's stringency as error for one examiner a station (the decision study architecture 1's reader ran) and files 16 stations; the blueprint draws the same number of stations from each domain every year, so a candidate's domain strengths recur in every form and are true score, and counted so the 2027 examination needs 13 (12 fall short at 0.79).
Estimand: the reliability of the 2027 final OSCE's total mark for ranking its candidates (the share of the variance among their total marks that reflects differences in their clinical performance, with the stations drawn from the case bank each year as policy 4.2 sets out and the examiners from the faculty's register), at each number of stations from 12 to 18 under the delivery plan's arrangement of one examiner a station in three parallel circuits, against the policy's standard of 0.80; filed in the assessment policy (4.2 the blueprint, 4.3 the standard and its definition) and the 2027 delivery plan (the arrangement and the room limit).
No decoy: nothing is planted; candidates' domain-specific strengths are how clinical skills vary (a good communicator is not always a deft proceduralist), no shipped document states a reliability figure, a station count it recommends or a method, and no memo, draft or stakeholder figure argues for a number of stations.
No certified replay: no published or certified figure reproduces the method; the pack carries no reliability for any past sitting and nothing is recovered by matching published figures.
Lesson: a reliability standard on a composite whose blueprint fixes the mix of content areas counts each candidate's area-specific strength as true score, so the decision study has to keep the areas as a fixed facet rather than treat the parts as drawn from one undivided pool, which overstates the length the blueprint needs; no build on file teaches a fixed facet in a decision study.

## Literal reader

Executed word for word: policy 4.3 asks for the share of the variance among candidates' total marks (the sum of twelve to eighteen station marks, policy 3.2) that reflects differences in their performance, with the stations drawn from the case bank each year, which policy 4.2 does with the same number from each of four domains, and the examiners from the register; the plan runs the 2027 sitting with one examiner a station in each of three circuits and the same stations everywhere, so a candidate's total carries their own examiners' leniency, which other candidates do not share, carries their strength in each domain in the same proportions in every form the blueprint can draw, and does not carry the stations' difficulty, which every candidate shares. The 2026 marks give the sources of variance (candidate 0.92, candidate by domain 0.76, case specificity within domain 1.36, examiner within station 1.06, residual 1.08); the literal reliability at n stations is (0.92 + S 0.76) / (0.92 + S 0.76 + (1.36 + 1.06 + 1.08) / n), S the sum of the squared domain shares of the n stations, which first reaches 0.80 at 13 stations (0.81; 12 stations 0.79). Every other reading in the fork table files the same number or violates a quoted sentence.
