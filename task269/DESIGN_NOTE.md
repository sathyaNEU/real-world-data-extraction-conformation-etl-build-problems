Architecture 5, drawn 2026-10-26 in a fresh world (wave 4, Descriptive lane, sathay3012), at the author's direction of 2026-10-26 to take every reopened number to ship with complete freedom on world and strategy. Architectures 1 to 4 and every screen under `## Tried and rejected` stay dead. Ardley Valley Hospital Foundation, the charity that funds nursing positions at Ardley Valley Hospital (a fictional regional public hospital in New South Wales), will fund a hand hygiene coaching nurse in 2027 for every ward whose staff performed hand hygiene at fewer than 80 per cent of the hand hygiene moments that arose on the ward's AM and PM shifts in the 2025-26 audit year. The hospital measures compliance through gold-auditor sessions: each ward's own trained nurses observe the five moments for about twenty minutes and record whether hand hygiene was performed. A ward's gold auditors can be released from patient care only when the shift's nursing hours cover the care its patients need, so their sessions sit almost entirely on well-staffed shifts, while staff perform hand hygiene less often the further a shift's worked nursing hours fall short of the care hours the acuity system says its patients require. The audits as they stand put every ward at or above the benchmark; carried across each ward's own shifts by how far each shift fell short, the moments weighted by the care that generates them, four wards fall below it.
