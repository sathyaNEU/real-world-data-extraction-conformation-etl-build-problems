Ardley Valley Hospital Foundation, the charity that funds nursing positions at Ardley Valley Hospital (a fictional regional public hospital in New South Wales), will fund a hand hygiene coaching nurse in 2027 for every ward whose staff performed hand hygiene at fewer than 80 per cent of the hand hygiene moments that arose on the ward's AM and PM shifts in the 2025-26 audit year. The hospital measures compliance through gold-auditor sessions: each ward's own trained nurses observe the five moments for about twenty minutes and record whether hand hygiene was performed. The roster follows each shift's acuity forecast, so a shift falls short when sick leave and casual-pool requests go unfilled rather than when its patients need more care. A ward's gold auditors can be released from patient care only when the shift's nursing hours cover the care its patients need, so their sessions sit almost entirely on covered shifts, which carry the same care hours as the ward's other shifts, while staff perform hand hygiene less often the further a shift's worked nursing hours fall short of the care hours the acuity system says its patients require. The audits as they stand put every ward but 9B (aged care, with low compliance on its covered and short shifts alike) at or above the benchmark; carried across each ward's own shifts by how far each shift fell short, the moments weighted by the care that generates them, AMU, ESSU, 4A and 7A fall below it beside 9B.

## Three rules

Task type: Healthcare Delivery & Health Economics, `provider-quality-risk-adjustment`: a provider process-quality measure (hand hygiene compliance against the five moments) read per ward from a direct-observation audit whose observed shifts are not the ward's shifts, adjusted for the workload mix the sample misses; it serves Descriptive & Distribution Analysis (each ward's compliance set against the 80 per cent benchmark).
Domain test: the deciding quantity is each ward's hand hygiene compliance over every moment on its day shifts, the field's direct-observation process measure; the field knows compliance falls as nursing workload outruns staffing (the moments rise with acuity while the hours to meet them do not), so gold-auditor sessions released mostly on well-staffed shifts are carried to the ward's own workload mix, each shift placed by its acuity system's required care hours against its roster's worked hours and the moments weighted by the care that generates them. With another field's nouns there are no moments generated by patient care, no auditors drawn from the ward's own nurses and no required care hours to set against worked hours.
Swap: with factories for wards and safety walk-rounds for hand hygiene the sentence still reads, but nothing there generates both the occasions and the workload from one patient's care, and no audit sample is released by its own unit's staffing against an acuity system's required hours; the identification needs nursing's two systems at the shift's grain.
Layer 1: the audit application's export with its own department names and the paper sessions of the March outage keyed afterwards under later session numbers; Ward 6A's move to the surgical block on 2 February 2026 as S1A in every system; escalation spaces in the acuity extract; the roster's supernumerary orientation, education and student shifts and the night shifts over the two daylight saving changes; night sessions and the other audited areas in the same export; the application's local name for personal care staff from 1 January 2026; each produced by the system named, none placed to be caught, each mishandled leaving the call where it is (R2 rows in `checks.py`).
Layer 2: `estimation_fit`, own:audited-moments-reweighted-to-each-shift's-care-hour-shortfall: the audit sample is selected on a covariate that drives the outcome (the shift's worked hours against its required care), so each ward's compliance is its audited compliance by shortfall carried to the shortfall mix of all its shifts; the weighting by shift and care hours the terms invite (R1) leaves the selection in place, and the audited shifts' care hours match every shift's, so a sample checked on its weights passes.
Decisive step: model whether hand hygiene was performed on the audited moments by ward and by the shift's required care hours over worked nursing hours, and carry each ward's model across all its AM and PM shifts weighted by required care hours.
Not semantics: every field, code and definition read correctly (the five moments, performed by rub or wash, the AM and PM shifts, the care-hour weights terms 2.2 files, S1A as 6A, supernumerary shifts left out) leaves the audits and their care-hour weighting on 9B alone; no definition says when auditors audit, and every audited moment carries its true outcome.
Not rule application: the terms file the estimand (every moment on every AM and PM shift, weighted by required care hours, funded below 80) and nothing on estimating it from a sample of sessions; executing the filed rules on the audits files 9B alone, and what rules that out is a comparison no document invites, the audited shifts' required against worked hours set against all shifts' (5.3 against 18.8 per cent more than 10 per cent short), reached only by building every shift from the acuity extract and the roster; checked on the care hours alone, the audited shifts match.
Analyst test: a strong analyst who cleans every record and applies every filed rule, then weights each ward's AM and PM audit compliance by its shifts' required care hours, or carries it across every shift by the care hours that weight it, funds 9B alone; executed on the generator's clean truth in `checks.py` (R3a ANALYST), where the decisive step alone files the golden five (R3b DECISIVE).
Estimand: terms 2.1 to 2.4 of hand_hygiene_coaching_grant_terms_2027.pdf: each ward's share of the hand hygiene moments that arose on its AM and PM shifts from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026 at which hand hygiene was performed by hand rub or hand wash, a shift's moments in proportion to its patients' required care hours, funded below 80 per cent, at most six wards.
No decoy: nothing is planted to be caught: no memo, earlier estimate, program report or stakeholder figure states a ward's compliance and no shipped file ranks the wards; the audits, the acuity extract and the roster are the systems' own exports.
No certified replay: no certified or published figure carries a ward's compliance, so the method cannot be recovered by reproducing one; the program summary states how auditing is done and no result.
Lesson: when an audit's sessions are released by the very workload that lowers what they measure, read each unit's sessions against its own workload and carry them to the unit's workload mix; no build on file teaches a sample selected on its own staffing.
