# task269 design note

Architecture 1, drawn 2026-10-19 (wave 4, Descriptive lane, sathay3012). Callowfield University's School of Medicine runs its final-year objective structured clinical examination (OSCE) in one circuit: in 2026 the 84 final-year candidates rotated through 12 stations over two days, and at each station the same two examiners marked every candidate. In 2027 Fennabeg Clinical School's final-year cohort joins the examination, which triples it, and the faculty cannot double-staff three circuits, so each station will have one examiner in each of three parallel circuits at three sites. The school's assessment policy requires the final OSCE's total mark to rank candidates with a reliability of at least 0.80, and the head of assessment files the 2027 blueprint, the number of stations, with the accreditation board as a material change. The question is how many stations the 2027 sitting needs.

## Default path

1. Read the assessment policy: the final OSCE's total mark (the sum of the station marks, each station marked out of 20) must rank candidates with a reliability of at least 0.80; stations are drawn from the case bank each year and examiners from the faculty pool.
2. Read the 2027 delivery plan: one examiner per station, three parallel circuits at three sites over two days, at most 18 stations at a site.
3. Build each candidate's 2026 station marks from the item-level export: the two day files reconciled, the checklist item the board of examiners withdrew after day 1 removed from every candidate's mark, items marked not applicable handled as the marking guide says, each station scaled to 20.
4. Estimate the reliability of single-examiner marks: Cronbach's alpha across the 12 stations on each examiner's marks (or a generalizability study with the examiners crossed with the candidates and one examiner per station in the decision study), and step it with Spearman-Brown to each number of stations.
5. The smallest number of stations that reaches 0.80 is the blueprint.

Ends on: 12 stations, the current number (single-examiner reliability about 0.81 at 12), so the 2027 sitting keeps its 12 stations.
Golden: 15 stations (reliability about 0.806; 14 stations fall short at about 0.795), once each station's examiner stringency, shared by every 2026 candidate, is counted as error for candidates who meet different examiners in 2027's circuits. Figures are the prototype's and are set by the generator at stage 3.

## Stump sentence

The default path keeps 12 stations, because it reads the reliability of single-examiner marks off the 2026 sitting, where the same two examiners marked every candidate at each station, so each examiner's leniency shifted every candidate alike and never moved one candidate against another; in 2027's three parallel circuits each candidate meets one examiner per station drawn from the faculty pool, so the leniency spread between examiners, visible in 2026 only as the gap between each station's two examiners' mean marks, becomes error in every candidate's total, and only the 2026 marks' variance components recombined for that arrangement put the 2027 examination at 15 stations.

## Three rules

Task type: psychometrics (Education & Learning Sciences), which serves Descriptive & Distribution Analysis: how the variance of a clinical examination's marks is distributed across candidates, stations, examiners and their interactions, and what share of the total mark's variance is real difference between candidates under a given examining arrangement.
Domain test: the deciding quantity is the reliability of the 2027 final OSCE's total mark, a generalizability study of the 2026 dual-examined marks (candidate, station, case specificity, examiner stringency within station, residual) recombined in a decision study for one examiner per station in parallel circuits, the field's own analysis of a clinical examination's reliability; replacing it with Cronbach's alpha on single-examiner marks stepped up by Spearman-Brown keeps 12 stations where the arrangement needs 15.
Swap: "how many stations the examination needs once each station is marked by one examiner in each of three circuits, for its total mark to rank candidates at 0.80" with another field's nouns (megawatt-hours for marks, meters for stations, a utility's daily reads for candidates) has no examiner whose leniency the new arrangement redistributes across the units and no case specificity that makes the number of stations the dial, so the decomposition and the task stop making sense.
Layer 1: the examination system's item-level export comes as two day files whose column names and mark labels changed with a software update between the days; examiner identifiers sit against the faculty roster's staff numbers and candidates' examination numbers against student numbers; the board of examiners withdrew one ambiguous checklist item after day 1, as its minutes record; items are marked not applicable when the simulated patient did not give the cue; checklists run from 14 to 22 items and each station is scaled to 20; each is how an OSCE system and an examinations office keep their records, nothing placed to be caught.
Layer 2: field_method, the generalizability study and its decision study: the examiners' stringency, a station-level shift in 2026's single circuit, is error for candidates under 2027's parallel circuits; reading reliability off the 2026 sitting's internal consistency (or a decomposition that keeps the examiners crossed with the candidates) flips the blueprint from 15 stations to 12.
Decisive step: estimating the 2026 marks' variance components with the examiners nested within stations and, in the decision study for 2027, counting the examiner-within-station variance as error alongside the case specificity and the residual, because in parallel circuits candidates at the same station meet different examiners.
Not semantics: every field read correctly (the day files reconciled, the withdrawn item removed, the not-applicable items handled, the examiners and candidates linked, every station on 20) still leaves alpha on single-examiner marks at 12 stations, because the error is in which sources of variance the 2027 arrangement turns into error, which no field, code or definition settles.
Not rule application: the policy defines what is measured (the total mark's reliability for ranking candidates, stations drawn from the case bank and examiners from the faculty pool, a standard of 0.80) and the delivery plan the 2027 arrangement; no rule or procedure says how reliability is estimated or which variance enters the error under a new arrangement, and executing every stated rule with alpha and Spearman-Brown files 12 stations.
Analyst test: a strong analyst who reads every file and the policy correctly computes Cronbach's alpha across the 12 stations on single-examiner marks (or a generalizability study with the examiners crossed with the candidates) and steps it with Spearman-Brown, filing the current 12 stations as enough at about 0.81; at each 2026 station the same two examiners marked every candidate, so their leniency never moved a ranking, but in 2027 each candidate meets one examiner per station drawn from the pool, and with that leniency spread counted as error the 2027 sitting needs 15 stations (14 fall short at about 0.795).
Estimand: the reliability of the 2027 final OSCE's total mark for ranking its candidates (the share of the variance of their total marks that reflects differences in their clinical performance, with stations drawn from the case bank and examiners from the faculty pool), at each number of stations from 12 to 18 under the delivery plan's arrangement of one examiner per station in three parallel circuits, against the policy's standard of 0.80; filed in the assessment policy (the standard and its definition) and the 2027 delivery plan (the arrangement and the room limit).
No decoy: nothing is planted; the two examiners' different leniency at each station is how real examiners mark (hawks and doves), no shipped document states a reliability figure, a station count it recommends or a method, and no memo, draft or stakeholder figure argues for a number of stations.
No certified replay: no published or certified figure reproduces the method; the pack carries no reliability for any past sitting and nothing is recovered by matching published figures.
Lesson: a facet that the measured sitting held constant for every candidate (the same examiners at each station) is invisible in that sitting's consistency and becomes error once the next arrangement varies it across candidates, so the next arrangement's reliability has to be rebuilt from the variance components with that facet's variance counted where the new arrangement puts it; no build on file teaches a generalizability study's decision study or examiner stringency.
