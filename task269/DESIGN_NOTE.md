# task269 design note

Architecture 4, drawn 2026-10-25 in a fresh world (wave 4, Descriptive lane, sathay3012). The number was reopened on 2026-10-25 at the author's direction (no number in the lane is retired); architectures 1 to 3 and every screen recorded under `## Tried and rejected` stay dead. Tillford County (a fictional county in an unnamed state) adopts its FY2027 budget in December 2026. The State Tax Commission's ratio study rule tests each county's single-family residential roll every year: the median of each parcel's assessed value over its market value on the assessment date has to sit between 0.900 and 1.100, and the coefficient of dispersion (COD) of those ratios has to be 15.0 or less; a roll that fails either is returned with an order to reappraise the class county-wide, at the county's cost, for the 1 January 2028 roll, so a county that expects the order budgets the reappraisal in FY2027. The County Assessor certified the 2026 roll (assessment date 1 January 2026) on 1 June 2026. The 2026 roll trended every single-family parcel from its 2025 value by its neighbourhood's factor, picked up the permits finaled in 2025 and carried in the Board of Review's 2025 reductions; and the Assessor's office reviewed every parcel that sold at arm's length in 2025 and moved its value toward its price. The County Administrator's budget and policy analyst has to tell the Board of Commissioners whether the 2026 roll meets the State's standard or the reappraisal goes into the FY2027 budget.

## Default path

1. Read the State Tax Commission's ratio study rule: the level is the median, over the county's single-family residential parcels on the certified roll, of each parcel's 2026 assessed value over its market value on 1 January 2026 (standard 0.900 to 1.100); the uniformity is the COD of those ratios (15.0 or less); market value is evidenced by the arm's-length sales recorded in 2025 (validation code Q), each price carried to 1 January 2026 with the county's monthly residential price index; a sale is set aside when the parcel changed physically between the sale and the assessment date; ratios are not trimmed.
2. Take the 2025 Q sales of single-family parcels, drop the sales of parcels whose permit work was finaled after the sale, and carry each price to 1 January 2026 by the index for January 2026 over the index for the sale's month.
3. Divide each sold parcel's certified 2026 total value by its indexed price: 1,698 ratios.
4. Median 0.952 and COD 8.0, both inside the standard.

Ends on: meets the standard at a median ratio of 0.95 and a COD of 8, so the reappraisal stays out of the FY2027 budget.

Golden: fails both tests at a median ratio of 0.87 and a COD of 18, so the Board funds the county-wide single-family reappraisal in FY2027.

## Stump sentence

The default path runs the State's ratio study on the 2025 arm's-length sales against the certified 2026 values, finds a median of 0.95 and a COD of 8 and files that the roll meets the standard, because it reads the sales as a sample of the roll; the Assessor's office reviewed every parcel sold at arm's length in 2025 and moved its value toward its price (the sold parcels' values rose a median 14.9 per cent from the 2025 roll, the unsold parcels' 3.4 per cent, each by its neighbourhood's factor), so the sales measure the reviewed parcels, each sold parcel's 2025 value carried forward by its neighbourhood's factor over its indexed price stands for the 93.3 per cent of the roll that did not sell, and with the reviewed parcels at their 6.7 per cent share the roll's median ratio is 0.87 and its COD 18: it fails both tests and the reappraisal goes into the FY2027 budget.

## Three rules

Task type: General Statistics & Data Science, `missing-data-censoring-measurement-error`: the roll's ratio distribution is the relative error of a valuation measured only on a validation subsample (the sold parcels) whose recorded values were refitted to the validation measurements, so the error is estimated out of sample from the subsample's values before the refit; it serves Descriptive & Distribution Analysis (the level and dispersion of that distribution against the State's standard).
Domain test: the deciding quantity is the median and the coefficient of dispersion of every single-family parcel's 2026 assessment ratio; the field's own analysis is the ratio study, with mass appraisal's own test of whether the sales sample the roll (the sold parcels' value change against the unsold parcels', the sales-chasing screen) and the sold parcels' prior values carried forward at the unsold parcels' change as the out-of-sample measure of the roll.
Swap: in General Statistics & Data Science the swap is of the method: replace the out-of-sample estimate (the sold parcels' 2025 values carried forward, mixed with the reviewed parcels at their share) by the sales' own median and COD and the call flips from failing both tests (0.87, 18) to meeting both (0.95, 8); replace the mixture by the carried-forward values alone and the figures move (0.86, 19) while the call stays; the deciding object is an error distribution estimated outside the sample the record was fitted to, and an in-sample summary changes the answer.
Layer 1: the 2025 roll exported by the county's retired appraisal system as a workbook (dashed parcel numbers, neighbourhood names) and the 2026 roll from its replacement as CSV (undashed numbers, neighbourhood codes); parcels split and combined between the rolls under new numbers, traced in the Assessor's parcel change log; the Recorder's sales extract with transfers coded U, deeds conveying more than one parcel and re-recorded corrective deeds; permits picked up in the 2026 values, some on parcels sold before the work was finaled; the Board of Review's 2025 reductions carried into the 2026 base; the Assessor's data corrections on a few per cent of parcels; the county index published monthly to one decimal on a January 2025 base; each written by the office named, none placed to be caught, and none moves the call.
Layer 2: `estimand_identification`, own:a-ratio-study-whose-sales-were-used-to-reset-the-values-it-measures: a sales ratio study measures the roll only if the sold parcels were valued like the unsold ones; the comparison of their value changes refutes that here, so the roll's ratios are estimated from the sold parcels' values before the review, carried forward at the unsold parcels' change; that choice flips the call from meets (0.95, 8) to fails (0.87, 18).
Decisive step: test whether the sales sample the roll by setting the sold parcels' 2025 to 2026 value change against the unsold parcels' in the same neighbourhoods, and, finding the sold parcels moved toward their prices, measure the roll from each sold parcel's 2025 value times its neighbourhood's factor (the unsold parcels' median change) over its indexed price, with the reviewed parcels' own ratios at their share of the roll.
Not semantics: every field, code, unit and definition read correctly (the validation codes, the index base, the physical-change rule, the median and COD definitions, both rolls' parcel numbers and neighbourhood names) still leaves the sales' own ratios standing for the roll, because no field marks a reviewed parcel and no definition says the sold parcels were valued differently; the difference lives only in the relation between the two rolls and the sales file.
Not rule application: the rule files the outcome (each single-family parcel's 2026 assessed value over its market value on 1 January 2026, median and COD over the county's single-family parcels on the certified roll) and the evidence (the 2025 arm's-length sales, indexed); nothing files how the sales stand for the parcels that did not sell. The enumerated family (the sales' own study, with or without the physical-change rule; the 2025 roll's own study on the same sales; the carried-forward values alone; the population mixture under nine constructions) spans a median of 0.857 to 0.952 (9.5 printed units at two decimals) and a COD of 8.0 to 18.7; the standing family (the nine mixture constructions consistent with the value-change evidence that measure the whole certified roll: the neighbourhood median, mean and value-weighted change, the mean without permit or appeal parcels, a county-wide change, post-stratified by neighbourhood, unrounded values, the post-sale-work sales kept, the share counted on the study sales) spans 0.8697 to 0.8712 and 17.97 to 18.01, one printed value each (0.87, every member at least 0.0038 inside its bin; 18, at least 0.47 inside); what rules the sales' own study out is a comparison nobody files (the sold parcels' value change against the unsold parcels', across two rolls in two systems' formats), and what separates the carried-forward values alone from the mixture is the filed population, a loud sentence that moves the figures and not the call.
Analyst test: a strong analyst who reads every rule and field correctly runs the State's ratio study on the 2025 arm's-length single-family sales, each price carried to 1 January 2026 by the county index and set against the parcel's certified 2026 value, finds a median of 0.95 and a COD of 8, and files that the roll meets the standard and the reappraisal stays out of the FY2027 budget; executed in `checks.py` on the generator's clean truth, where the out-of-sample measure alone files the golden.
Estimand: the State Tax Commission's residential ratio study rule (`ratio_study_standard_residential.pdf`, sections on the level, the uniformity, the evidence and the consequence): the median and the COD, over every single-family residential parcel on the certified 2026 roll, of the parcel's 2026 assessed value over its market value on 1 January 2026, against 0.900 to 1.100 and 15.0; market value evidenced by the 2025 arm's-length sales carried to the assessment date by the county index; a failing roll reappraised county-wide at the county's cost for 1 January 2028.
No decoy: nothing is planted to be caught: no memo, prior study, certification letter, workbook or stakeholder figure states a ratio, a median or a COD or argues that the roll meets the standard; the Assessor's review of sold parcels is the office's own practice, written into the values and nowhere else.
No certified replay: no certified or published ratio, median, COD or factor is in the pack, so the method cannot be recovered by reproducing a figure; the neighbourhood factors exist only as the unsold parcels' changes.
Lesson: an instrument's accuracy measured on the cases it was fitted to overstates its accuracy on the population, so a validation sample counts only after checking whether the measuring party treated it like the rest, and where it did not, the accuracy is measured from the sampled cases' records before the refit; no build on file evaluates a refitted record out of sample.
