## Three rules

Domain test: the call turns on maximum-day demand, the water-system engineering quantity a source capacity evaluation is scored on: the water the system's users and its leaks took in a day, production adjusted for what storage gave up or gained; with another field's nouns there is no well field rated with its largest well out, no elevated storage riding through a peak, no pressure zone fed through a booster, and the task stops reading the same.
Analyst test: an analyst who reads the daily log, the SCADA export, the well test records and the state's definitions correctly (local calendar days, the most recent tested capacities, the largest well out) and takes each day's production as that day's use files no shortfall; a storage-aware analyst who nets only the main zone's two tanks files 30 gpm, one who takes the days from the export's UTC midnights 117 gpm, one who differences daily mean storage none, and only production less the change in all four tanks between Eastern midnights gives 184 gpm. `stump_class: analytical`.
Layer 1: an operators' daily log kept as a workbook with a sheet per month (each well's cumulative totalizer at midnight, one register rolling over, run hours, chlorine, remarks); an hourly SCADA historian export in long format (237,168 rows: tag, UTC timestamp, value, quality code) with a tag list and a readme, instantaneous levels and flows beside cumulative totalizers, a radio outage with held values, the two daylight-saving days; pump-test records against nameplate, with W1's rehabilitation and retest; a facilities summary giving each tank's gallons per foot of level; on the ask path, month-end AMR meter reads by endpoint and meter (with a re-sent batch), a meter inventory with register multipliers, removals and change-outs, CMMS work orders carrying the vault meters' logged reads and the change-out reads, the facilities building inventory with a removed-buildings sheet, and the 2024 service line inventory; distractors (the campus weather station's daily record, the grounds irrigation controller schedule, the borough's consumer confidence report). None of it sits on the main call's rows in a way that can move the call.
Layer 2: the estimand is the most water the campus used in a calendar day, and the easy construction reads the wells' production as use, where use is production less the water the tanks gained; a water engineer recognises production as supply, which equals use only over periods in which storage ends where it started.

## Strongest-solver test

The strongest solver opens every file, sees four tank level tags in the export and a gallons-per-foot table in the facilities summary, and knows that a system's demand is production plus the water storage gave up. What keeps it on production is that nothing asks the question: the log is the operators' own daily record of water, the letter defines the day's use without saying how it is measured, the tanks appear only as hourly rows in feet in a long UTC export built for operations, and on 364 of the 365 days production and use differ by less than the day's ordinary refill (the tanks cycle overnight and end most days near where they began). A solver who does ask has three ways left to file another wrong figure (the main zone's tanks alone, UTC days, daily means), each a full analysis. The round tests whether a top solver asks.
