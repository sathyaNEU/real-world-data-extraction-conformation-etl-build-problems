I head the revenue research office. The finance secretary adopts the floors on our recommendation, and Manuel, our chief economist, reckons they should come straight off this year's returns. What I need from you is the 2025 floor at each of the top 10, 5 and 1 per cent marks, each to the nearest $1,000, in one sentence the secretary can adopt as written.

That schedule is what the forecast will run on, so build tier_schedule.xlsx around it. It opens on the schedule. Then, tier by tier for 2025, in whole dollars: estimated-tax receipts at each of the four instalments, withholding, and net capital gain, plus that gain's share of the tier's AGI as a percentage to one decimal place.
