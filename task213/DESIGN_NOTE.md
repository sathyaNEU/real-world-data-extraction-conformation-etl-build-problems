# task213 design note: Calabash Exchange's Q3 2026 rating-ring sweep

Seed AD36 (wave 8, Anomaly Detection & Diagnostics), read for its header and Situation only: a peer-to-peer
trading platform whose users rate each other after escrow settles, a quarterly sweep for coordinated rating rings
that suspends the accounts and expunges every rating they gave, and the count it expunges, committed to the nearest
hundred in the 1 December integrity notice. The world, the decision and the quantity are the seed's; the pitfall was
re-found from the data under the 10/10 rules. None of the seed's own devices carries the call: no second ring
definition reproduces a published share (no validated decoy), the playbook's cap is a filed fact the count passes
through on every route alike, and no document carries the head of trust's prior or any figure arguing for a count.

## Default path

The trust-and-safety field's own ring detection, run correctly on the shipped records (figures are the prototype's at
the draw and are replaced by the shipped seed's at stage 3):

1. Read the moderation playbook: a ring is two or more accounts run together that trade with one another so that
   the accounts can rate one another; the sweep suspends every account in a ring and expunges every rating each gave
   in the quarter, a rating once in its latest version; no account loses more than half (rounded down) of the
   ratings it received as the seller in the quarter's trades, the rest held for manual review; a customer's account
   at a Calabash Point is the customer's own.
2. Load the quarter's ratings (latest versions), the escrow trades and the sign-in log; resolve escrow handles and
   wallet numbers to accounts through the account register; conform the two fingerprint schemes.
3. Build the linkage graph every ring pipeline starts from: accounts joined through the phones and tablets they sign
   in on, the Calabash Point shop tablets set aside as the playbook says, and keep the linked groups whose members
   traded with and rated one another.
4. Confirm each group in the rating graph: mutual +10s on small trades, sellers rated almost only by the group,
   sign-ins switching between the group's accounts minutes apart. They are genuine rings, run from a few shared
   phones.
5. Suspend every account in those groups, expunge every rating they gave in the quarter, apply the cap seller by
   seller and total.

Ends on: 1,200 (the rings that share phones; prototype seed 1)
Golden: 5,800 (every ring, the three run from one phone per account included; prototype seed 1)

## Stump sentence

A competent analyst links accounts through the phones they sign in on, sets the agent-shop tablets aside, confirms the
linked groups as rings in the rating graph, applies the cap and files about 1,200 ratings, never following each
buyer's purchased USDT out of the escrow into the wallet ledger, where the purchases of the rings run from one phone
per account return within the hour to an account that funds the very sellers they bought from.

## Three rules

- Task type: banking-credit-financial-risk / fraud-aml-detection, which serves Anomaly Detection in the taxonomy; the
  anomaly is the quarter's coordinated rating activity, diagnosed account by account as ring or not.
- Domain test: the deciding quantity is the ratings given by accounts one person or group runs, established the way
  fraud and AML work establishes common control on a trading platform, by the economic substance of the accounts'
  trades (whether what a buyer pays for stays with the buyer), with the platform's identifier links as the field's
  first screen; with hospital or school nouns there are no escrowed coins, no wallet and no rating ring.
- Swap: "how many ratings will the sweep expunge" with patients and reviews of a clinic in place of traders and trades
  loses the escrow, the coins a seller must lock to earn a rating and the wallet they come back through, so the
  question stops parsing; with a goods marketplace it still parses but the decisive evidence (a buyer's purchase
  forwarded to whoever funds the seller) has no counterpart in a parcel.
- Layer 1: escrow export keyed by user handle (with handle renames) and the wallet ledger keyed by wallet number,
  resolved through the account register; escrow and ratings in UTC, the wallet ledger in Lagos local time without an
  offset, the sign-in log in epoch milliseconds; wallet amounts in integer micro-USDT against escrow amounts in USDT;
  the ratings service writing a row per version; two fingerprint schemes after the July app release; the escrow
  export split by month with a renamed column from August. Each is written by the system that holds it; nothing is
  placed to be caught and none of it moves the call.
- Layer 2: estimand_identification, the right construction of the population: whether accounts are run together is
  read from where each buyer's purchases go across the escrow and the wallet, because the identifier links the field
  starts from mark shared use (agent shops, households, cafes) and miss the rings that run one phone per account.
- Decisive step: following each buyer's purchased USDT from the escrow ledger into the wallet ledger and asking
  whether it reaches, within the quarter, an account that funds the seller it was bought from, then taking every
  account on such a loop as one ring's.
- Not semantics: every field, code and definition read correctly (handles, wallet numbers, versions, clocks, units,
  fingerprints, the playbook's terms) still leaves the linkage graph on the phone-sharing rings alone, because the
  loop is a relation among three accounts' rows in two ledgers that no field, code or flag records.
- Not rule application: no filed rule names a loop, a wallet or a forward; executing the playbook (its ring
  definition, the cap, the Calabash Point clause) on the platform's identifier links files the default path. The
  enumerated family (shared phones with or without the shop tablets, dense mutual +10 cores, wash-trade sets inside
  the escrow, small-ticket buyer heuristics, forwarders, collecting accounts and their neighbourhoods, transfer-funded
  sellers, pair round trips, the fairness algorithm) spreads from about 0.2 to about 15 times the golden over 20
  redraws; the standing family (every construction that closes the loop, at one loop or several, any forward window
  from an hour to the quarter) files the golden in 20 of 20, and the ruling-out is quiet: the data, not a sentence.
- Analyst test: a strong analyst who cleans every file and reads every rule links accounts through shared phones,
  sets the shop tablets aside, confirms the linked groups in the rating graph and files 1,200 ratings, the default
  path's Ends on, never reading where each buyer's coins went next.
- Estimand: the moderation playbook (the trust and safety team's filed standard) fixes the population (every account
  in a ring, a ring being two or more accounts run together that trade with one another so that they can rate one
  another), the outcome (the ratings those accounts gave in the quarter swept, each rating once in its latest
  version), the window (1 July to 30 September 2026 in UTC, by the time a rating was given), the unit (ratings) and
  the cap's holdback (half of an account's received-as-seller ratings, rounded down, the rest to manual review).
- No decoy: nothing is planted; the agent shops are the platform's real programme, the phone-sharing rings are
  genuine rings the default path rightly finds, the transparency report's prevalence is a true published figure that
  measures every inauthentic rating, and no memo, draft, briefing or stakeholder figure argues for any count.
- No certified replay: no certified or published figure reproduces under one method; the earlier sweeps' counts are
  totals for other quarters with no records shipped behind them, so nothing is recovered by replaying them.
- Lesson: on a platform where shared devices are ordinary, accounts run together are shown by where the value they
  trade ends up across every ledger the platform keeps, not by the identifiers they share; no build on file teaches
  reading common control from a value loop across two ledgers.

## Tried and rejected

- 2026-11-04, at the draw: the seed's own pairing, Product Analytics with Anomaly Detection, is undrawable (no
  product-analytics task type serves AD); the world moved to Banking, Credit & Financial Risk / fraud-aml-detection.
- 2026-11-04, at the draw, on paper: each trade's two sides attributed to the device that performed them (escrow
  actions joined to sessions by time) as the decisive step. Identity resolution has no hold on file (strategies F10),
  a per-record reader derives the device of each action with two joins, and same-device self-dealing is the
  textbook signature a judge names; kept as the default path's own evidence, never the step.
- 2026-11-04, at the draw, on paper: wash-trade closure inside the escrow ledger (strongly connected sets netting to
  zero) as the decisive step: the textbook wash-trading method, and on this platform the merchants who swap
  inventory are exactly such sets; kept as a reader's rung.
- 2026-11-04, at the draw, on paper: tracing coins lot by lot under a filed first-in-first-out or pro rata rule: the
  tracing-rule pincer task190's architecture 8 screen measured (filed it is rule application, open it forks the figure).
- 2026-11-04, proto1 and proto2: a single collecting account per ring with forwards running up to four hours; the
  60-minute forward-window variant moved the count (7,286 against 7,348), so forwards were bounded to 45 minutes and
  the window variants (one hour, one day, one week, any time) now agree in every redraw.
- 2026-11-04, proto2: trades inside households (2 per cent of shared-device pairs) put households into the device
  route with a few ratings each and left "run together" open to a literal judge; households now never trade with
  one another.
- 2026-11-04, proto3 to proto5: loyal small-ticket honest buyers made a per-record cell (small-ticket buyers who pass
  on everything, never withdraw and give only +10) land at 0.96 to 1.17 of the golden, then 1.15 to 1.38, two errors
  of opposite sign (it misses the sellers and takes in honest forwarders); the honest payment and savings-circle
  population was grown to its realistic share and the cell now sits at 2.1 to 2.8 times the golden.
- 2026-11-04, proto6: agent shops left informal: a literal judge could read an unregistered shop tablet with dozens
  of buyers as one person running the accounts (a Gate C fork on "run together"); the shops are now the platform's
  filed agent programme and the playbook says a customer's account at a shop is the customer's.
