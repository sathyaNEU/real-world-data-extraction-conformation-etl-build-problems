## 1. Final Recommendation

**Year 1 of the 2027-30 maintenance contract should be tendered at 1,750,000 m3 in situ.** Each reach and the turning basin gather sediment in proportion to how far their bed is held below the natural bed beside them, so holding them two metres deeper raises each one's 2016-17 to 2025-26 infill by two metres over that depth. Not 1,300,000 m3, the record carried at 12.3 m. Not 1,500,000 m3 from scaling by the held depths, 14.3 over 12.3, nor 1,400,000 m3 from a trench trapping efficiency: in the record each part's infill per square metre follows its depth below the natural bed, which neither reproduces.
