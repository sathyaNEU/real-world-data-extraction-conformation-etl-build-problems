## Stump sentence

## Three rules

**Domain test:** the deciding quantity is the flow of self-funding care home residents onto council funding as their capital runs down to the upper limit, forecast by months since admission against a means-test threshold, which is how long-term care funding forecasts are built; put another field's nouns in and there is no means test, no self-funder pool outside the funder's register and no depletion clock, so the task stops reading the same.

**Analyst test:** a strong analyst who links the placements register to the payment and assessment records, applies the budget definitions correctly and treats deaths as a competing exit still forecasts conversions as a trend of past conversions (740.3), or as a pooled conversion rate per self-funder times today's pool (729.1 to 746.0), and under-forecasts 31 March 2028 by 43 to 60 residents on the build (28 to 59 across the prototype's three seeds); only the conversion hazard by months since admission, applied to each self-funder's own admission date (788.96), sizes it. The easy way gets it wrong, so the stump is analytical.

**Layer 1:** a case system replaced in July 2023 (client numbers reissued, a crosswalk to the old numbers and to the finance system's debtor references, merged duplicates that keep two debtor references); placements split into episodes at every change of home or weekly fee; permanent, interim and respite placements in one register; the placement's locality is the arranging team's, not the home's; 4-weekly free personal and nursing care payment lines with April rate changes, hospital-absence reductions and retrospective adjustments; annual financial reviews completed up to five weeks before they take effect, and funding decisions backdated to when funding began; deaths linked from National Records of Scotland with a match status and a registration lag; homes re-registered on a change of owner with some placements left on the old service number; contract rates beside the draft 2027-28 offer, and fee supplements above the contract rate. Each is what these systems produce, and none decides the call.

**Layer 2:** the tempting shortcut is to forecast the conversion route at its run rate, or at a pooled rate per self-funder, because it has been steady for five years and back-tests well. An expert refuses it because conversions are generated by capital depletion, which runs on each resident's own clock from admission: the hazard is near zero in the first year and peaks between 18 and 36 months, so the 2027-28 flow is set by how many of today's self-funders are entering that window, and the 2025-26 surge put a pool half again its usual size just short of it. The rigorous method conditions the conversion hazard on months since admission, with death and discharge competing, and applies it to each self-funder's admission date.

**Gate G.** Litmus: no reported figure is overturned; the register's conversions, the payment lines and the definitions are all correct. Mechanism method_or_model_selection with forecasting support; surface_read_dependency no; sole_data_defect no. Clean-data test: with every Layer 1 device repaired in the generator, the run rate is still natural and still about 750, and the golden still about 800 (the generator asserts all three). Deletion test: delete every distractor and every Layer 1 device and the answer does not move. Lens swap: the default and the golden count the same population on the same dates; they differ in how a flow is generated. Identity test: residents on a date are today's survivors plus surviving direct admissions plus surviving conversions, and the identity closes over a quantity no file ships (the 2027-28 conversions), so no reconciliation reaches it. Corpus direction: every closed period confirms the run rate, so nothing in the pack refutes the naive path, and nothing files it as policy. No shipped artifact forecasts residents or conversions.
