## Three rules

- Task type: General Statistics & Data Science, data-integration-record-linkage-entity-resolution (integrating conflicting sources into one dataset and one count: the latent class model behind Fellegi-Sunter record linkage, per-source error rates estimated from agreement patterns with no reference), which serves Data Extraction & Conformation.
- Domain test: the quantity that decides the call is each region's share of full adult-ICU hospital-days, estimated the way data integration estimates a state that every source records with error: a two-class latent class model of the three feeds' flags (conditional independence given the day's state, each feed's miss and false-flag rate the same statewide, the share free in each region), fitted by maximum likelihood; replacing it with any record-level reconciliation rule changes which regions activate.
- Swap: in General Statistics the swap is of the method, and it breaks the task: with each region's share taken from a reconciliation rule (any feed shows full, two of three agree, the board counts only when the survey or a decline corroborates it, the model's own most likely class per day) the call becomes all eight regions, none, or one, never the four; with the hospital nouns replaced by another field's (three moderation queues flagging posts, the share of violating posts in each community) the latent class estimate still decides, as a general-statistics task requires.
- Layer 1: three feeds for one picture with their own keys and clocks (the census survey keyed by state licence number with resubmitted corrections and a column renamed when the form changed; the board's event log keyed by its own facility code, UTC timestamps, lines for every unit and statuses carried over from before the window; the transfer center's call log keyed by hospital names as typed, Central time for every hospital, reopened and cancelled requests and a decline-reason code list that changed), two time zones across the state, a facility crosswalk and name-alias list, and holiday gaps in the survey; each is produced by the system named and none is placed to be caught.
- Layer 2: estimation_fit, a measurement model for conflicting categorical sources: each region's share of full days is the latent class prevalence, and the feeds' errors are estimated from how the three agree.
- Decisive step: estimating each region's share of full days from a latent class model of the three feeds, their miss and false-flag rates identified by three-way agreement and the same statewide, the share free by region, instead of settling each hospital-day from its flags by any rule.
- Not semantics: with every feed, field, code, time zone and definition read correctly (Closed covers keeping the last beds, the census is taken at 07:00, a decline needs a request, the full day is every staffed bed occupied at some time) the analyst knows each feed is imperfect but not how many days were full; every rule built from that reading, any feed, two of three or the corroborated board, still files a wrong set.
- Not rule application: the compact files what is counted (full hospital-days, the 28 days, the region, one in three) and the annex says none of the three feeds is a record of full days; no filed rule or procedure says how the feeds are combined, and executing every stated rule leaves the analyst to choose a reconciliation rule, each of which misses the set.
- Analyst test: a strong analyst who conforms every feed correctly (latest accepted survey reports, the board replayed to local dates, the calls on each hospital's own date) and reads the full day literally counts a day full when any feed shows it and activates all eight regions (the default path's Ends on); requiring two feeds activates none, and the model's most likely class per day activates none or one.
- Estimand: the compact's article 2 and article 4 (`regional_icu_surge_compact_2025.pdf`): a region activates for the four weeks from the Monday after the review when at least one in three of its hospital-days in the 28 days to the Sunday before the review were full days; a hospital-day is one compact hospital with adult intensive care beds on one calendar day of its own local time, and a full day is one on which every staffed adult intensive care bed was occupied at some time, so that the unit could take no new patient.
- No decoy: nothing is planted: no memo, briefing, draft or stakeholder figure states a share, a rule or a set of regions; each feed's documentation describes how it is collected and is true; the distractors (the federal weekly capacity extract, last season's activation record) carry no hospital-day count.
- No certified replay: no certified or published figure reproduces only under one method; the method is left to the design (three independent feeds, no reference), and nothing in the pack scores a rule.
- Lesson: a model learns that when every source of a state misses some instances and flags some non-instances and none is a reference, the count is the prevalence of a measurement model whose source error rates are identified by how the sources agree, estimated group by group, not a tally of records settled by any rule, the model's own most likely class included; no build on file estimates categorical sources' errors from their agreement without an observed truth.
