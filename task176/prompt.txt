How many of the family child care homes licensed in Allerton on 31 December 2026 will close during 2027? Put the count in fcc_closures_2027.xlsx with the money the subsidy paid every family child care home open in 2026 in each quarter of the year by the date of the payment run, by the region of the home that gave the care, and the child-days of care the subsidy paid those homes for in each half of the year by the date of the care, across Allerton and counting a child once on a day.
