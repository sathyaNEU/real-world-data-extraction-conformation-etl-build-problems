### The architecture screened, and its default path

Niche: a university population-genomics institute's confirmation core splits one genotyping wave's fixed
capacity across seven disease programmes' nominated loci, with the trustees approving the appropriation
and the lab's capacity report fixing the total at 36,000 sample slots. Layer 2 is the client's own rigor
pattern 2 for this task type, `biomedical-clinical-research/genomics-gwas-high-dimensional-association/2`:
discovery effects carry winner's curse, the field's move is a replication estimate or a selection
correction, and the client's own named shortcut is discovery effects fed into replication sizing. The
filed estimand would have been each programme's share of the capacity, where a locus's requirement is the
number of 2027-wave samples at which its per-allele effect is measured with a standard error no greater
than a quarter of the effect **it is expected to show in that wave**, which is a future value and so
survives `stumping` 1.2a's observed-or-unobserved dichotomy. No significance level is filed anywhere: the
scan's own line is a historical fact about a selection already made, and nothing in the decision executes
a test.

Default path, as a competent analyst builds it from the files' grain:

1. Read the scan's per-variant table and keep each programme's nominated loci.
2. Read each locus's reported effect estimate, standard error, allele frequency and imputation quality.
3. Size each locus: the samples needed for a standard error of a quarter of the effect, using the effect
   the scan reports.
4. Sum the requirement over each programme's loci.
5. Split the capacity report's 36,000 slots in proportion to those seven sums, largest remainder to whole
   slots.

**Ends on:** CARDIO 16,188, METAB 13,712, RESPIR 4,081, NEURO 1,424, RENAL 391, IMMUNO 151, ONCO 53.
**Golden:** CARDIO 16,289, METAB 13,720, RESPIR 4,015, NEURO 1,400, RENAL 380, IMMUNO 144, ONCO 52.

Stump sentence: a competent solver sizes every nominated locus on the discovery estimate that nominated

### Why it is dead: the measurements

**1. The correction family's own spread is about six times everything the decisive step moves.** Five
routes a competent analyst would use (conditional maximum likelihood given the selection, the first-order
truncation-hazard correction, empirical Bayes with a normal prior fitted on the nominated set, Tweedie
shrinkage read off the whole scan's own z density, and a parametric bootstrap bias correction) give the
graded split a spread of **565 slots** at the worst programme, against the **101 slots** the decisive
route moves the split away from the naive route. Over 20 redraws the family spread is mean 472, minimum
263, maximum 1,590 slots, against a naive separation of mean 79, minimum 28, maximum 135. `stumping` 1.2
requires the family inside 0.4 of the printed unit, and the printed unit here is one slot.

**2. The dial fails at every setting, which is the single-dial law.** The lever is bought by pushing the
weaker programmes' nominated loci closer to the scan's line, and that same move destabilises the
conditional likelihood (whose estimate goes to zero at the line, so a requirement goes to infinity).
Sweeping the four weaker programmes' discovery sample size over a factor of nearly seven:

| scale | median z of weak programmes' hits | lever, slots | family spread, slots | lever / spread |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.45 | 6.09 | 64.0 | 9,282.0 | 0.007 |
| 0.60 | 6.31 | 66.0 | 438.8 | 0.150 |
| 0.80 | 6.44 | 69.0 | 9,345.4 | 0.007 |
| 1.00 | 6.52 | 73.4 | 485.6 | 0.151 |
| 1.40 | 6.73 | 74.0 | 455.2 | 0.163 |
| 2.00 | 7.09 | 64.5 | 406.1 | 0.159 |
| 3.00 | 7.52 | 52.9 | 275.4 | 0.192 |

The ratio never reaches 1 anywhere in the range, and its best value is 0.192 at the setting where the
lever is smallest. There is no setting between the two ends.

**3. The decisive step reorders nothing.** Over 20 redraws the decisive route and the naive route return
one identical ordering of the seven programmes. The step moves magnitudes by about 0.3 per cent of the
capacity and moves no programme past another, which is the one-live-degree-of-freedom law: a selection
correction is a move along one axis, monotone in the discovery statistic, and a share normalises most of
it away.

**4. `corr(decisive measure, unit size)` is +0.962** over 20 redraws (minimum +0.953, maximum +0.971)
against the discovery sample size of each programme, where +0.98 killed task57. The graded split is
96 per cent a restatement of how large each programme's scan was.

**5. The two halves of the designation screen fail in opposite directions, and that is the cleanest
statement of why the reserved decision type cannot work here.** Discretised to a designation, the answer
is determinate: the correction family names one largest-share programme in 20 of 20 redraws. But the
crude route (count the nominated loci) and the naive route name the same programme in 20 of 20, so the
discretised version has no lever at all. Graded as a split, the lever exists and the determinacy does
not. `stumping` 1.6a's rescue, that discretising makes a free weighting harmless, needs a threshold the
family's spread must not cross, and **an allocation to a fixed total has no threshold**: it grades the
number itself, so there is nowhere for the spread to hide. Worth recording for its own sake: the truth
names a different largest-share programme in some redraws (2 distinct leaders) while every estimator
family member names one, so on this ground the corrected route is not even reliably closer to the truth
on the graded object.

**6. The task246 collision is numerical as well as abstract.** The summed-squared-standard-errors
correction lands within **115 slots** of the selection correction on a 36,000-slot total, so the two moves
are not separable on this ground: a solver taking task246's move reaches the same split, which would make
the decisive step ambiguous even if it had separated. This is structural rather than incidental, because
for a selected locus the sampling-variance bias and the selection bias are both of order one over the
squared discovery statistic, so no tuning makes one large while the other is negligible.

### Pricing the one unspent non-level object, a rank verified

`stumping` Part 3 names exactly two non-level graded objects for selected-unit ground: an extremum judged
against a null extremum, which task191 holds, and a rank verified, which is unspent and was unpriced. The

estimator family itself puts on that same gap (`scratchpad/w6_200/screen2_rank_pricing.py`, 120 adjacent
pairs over 20 redraws).

**The determinacy half of that pincer does not close on this ground, and that is the one door this pass
leaves open.** Of 120 adjacent pairs, **73 sit more than ten family-spreads apart** (records-fixed, so

between one and ten** (the live middle), with the normalised gap running minimum -0.10, median 12.30,

determinate enough to grade, which is not what the pincer predicts. What still blocks the object is the

### The inverted draw: the file that does the forcing

Starting from the estimator closed four cells on this number already, so the draw started from the file.
The files a plant-scale measuring operation actually keeps are the gauge or instrument archive (one row per
item or per interval, the only file with values), the configuration or recipe register (what the instrument
was set to), the operations or production log (what ran, how much and in what mix), the asset register
(what the machine is), the sampling or inspection plan (what was looked at), the customer specification
(what the limit is) and the maintenance system (when the machine was not itself).

**The file taken, and what it pins that nothing else pins: the shift production log.** It is the only file
in the set that states how much of the measured output each of two sources produced, and it states it per
shift. The gauge archive cannot pin it, because the gauge sits after the merge and no reading carries a
source; the asset register pins only that there are two sources; the specification pins only the limit. The
quantity that file forces is therefore **one source's own distribution inside a blend no row attributes**,
and the field's method for it is to solve the blend identity across periods whose mix differs.

### The architecture screened, and its default path

2. Read the shift production logs, supersede the three revised shifts, fix the line crosswalk.
3. Put the published summary's tenths of a millimetre into millimetres and align the production week to the

### The general law this pass earns, and it strengthens contract screen 2

**A construction that is affine in the forcing file's own published column is reached with certainty by one
regression on that column, before any world exists.** Screen 1 kills a scalar correcting input by
arithmetic; this kills a per-unit correcting input whose relation to the graded quantity is linear, also by
arithmetic. So the inverted draw's step two should be run as **algebra first and a correlation second**:
write the construction as a function of the forcing column and ask whether it is affine in it. If it is, the
plainest column plus one regression is the decisive step, and no amount of mess, grain or Layer 1 between
the two files changes that, because the mess delays the join rather than breaking the identity. The
corollary for drawing: a surviving construction has to be **non-linear in the forcing quantity** (a power, a
reciprocal, an order statistic, a decay, a modulo), which is a one-line test to run on a candidate before it
is screened.

### The second death: why a record of extremes cannot carry a silent rung

The candidate: a high-rate gauge or historian stores one value per window and that value is the window's
**maximum** (a peak-hold instrument, a max-aggregating tag), with the window's underlying opportunity count
in an operations file at another grain, so the item-level distribution is recovered by inverting
`G(x) = F(x)^k` with `k` varying across lines and periods. Forced by arithmetic, varied inputs, no
attaining subset, and a genuine distributional object, so it passes the screens the wave was built around.

**It is dead on a pincer worth stating generally, because it closes a whole family.** Nothing in a record of
maxima measures that it is a record of maxima: a column of values carries no evidence of its own
extremisation, unlike two vintages measuring a coding lag or shared specimens measuring a dependence. So
either the pack documents the transformation, and the documenting sentence ("the logger holds the peak since
reset", "tag aggregation max over the interval") is a clause the solver cannot avoid reading, which sends it
looking and makes the decisive rung readable on `stumping` 2.1 test 1; or the pack does not, and the
construction is unavailable, which is `stumping` 1.2b case three. There is no third position. **This is the
1.2b pincer transposed from a break in the records to a transformation of them, and it closes every design
whose decisive step inverts a documented transformation of the recorded values.** It is the reason the
decimating-median candidate below would also have failed even had it been original.
