Where should the Wexcombe Foundation's 1,200 extra interviews go in the 2027 Provincial Household Medicines Survey? Our placement workbook, boost_region_2027.xlsx, should hold for every health region the share of its 2027 standard error the boost would remove if it went there, then the region that gets the boost, the runner-up and the smallest boost, in whole interviews per area, that would remove a larger share of the runner-up's error than the 1,200 remove in the region that gets them, and beside them each region's 2024 completed interviews, estimate and standard error on the regions as they have stood since the 2024 reorganisation. The foundation reads the case in boost_memo_2027.pdf, whose bar chart sets the regions' shares side by side. Shares are whole per cents, compared unrounded, the runner-up's boost a number of interviews per area, the 2024 estimates per cents to one decimal and the standard errors percentage points to two decimals.
