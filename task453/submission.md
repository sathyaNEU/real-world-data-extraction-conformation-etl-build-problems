## Tags

**Domain:** Physical & Environmental Sciences (emissions inventory: a provincial crematoria mercury fund's 2026 abatement share and designations, each cremation's mercury read from the dental amalgam of the person cremated).
**Analytical objective:** Data Extraction & Conformation (ETL).

## 1. Final Recommendation

**Yes: the Crematoria Mercury Fund must designate Ashgrove Cremation Centre and Tallis Creek Crematorium, because only about 45 per cent of the mercury released by cremation in 2026 was released in abated cremations, short of the Regulation's 55 per cent, and the share reaches it only with both counted (about 59 per cent; about 52 with either alone).**
Not Tallis Creek and Dunmore Falls, which the 2016 survey's amalgam applied at age at death designates at 41.8 per cent: at every age the 2026 dead belong to a cohort ten years younger than the survey's respondents of that age, so the age profile overstates those who died in their sixties and understates those who died in their late seventies and eighties. Not Corbett Narrows alone, which counting cremations designates.

## 2. Critical Components

1. 2026 cremations from the permits with a 2026 date of cremation: **29,294**, abated where the crematorium's plant was commissioned before the date of cremation (Wexley Regional from 4 July, Hollinsbrook from 15 September).
2. Each person's teeth restored with amalgam follow their birth cohort: the survey's 2016 mean for their sex ten years younger than their age at death, carried to 2026 by that cohort's own change since the 2006 survey (twenty years younger); nil under 18.
3. Share of 2026 cremation mercury released in abated cremations: **about 45 per cent**, below 55.
4. Unabated crematoria by 2026 mercury: **Ashgrove Cremation Centre** and **Tallis Creek Crematorium** the two largest; the share reaches 55 per cent only with both.

## 3. Step-by-Step Solution

1. Permits: kept the 2026 dates of cremation, keyed each facility number to its Fund code through the register's facility sheet (F1984 is Wexley Regional before 2 March 2026) and marked abated cremations by the register's commissioning dates: 29,294 cremations.
2. Amalgam: for a person aged a at death, twice the 2016 report's mean for their sex in the band holding a less ten, less the 2006 report's mean in the band holding a less twenty (each cohort's own change between the waves, repeated over the decade to 2026), floored at nil, nil under 18, at 0.55 grams a tooth (Regulation s.2).
3. Share: summed by crematorium, about 45 per cent of the 2026 mercury was released in abated cremations, short of 55 (s.3).
4. Designation: unabated crematoria in descending order of 2026 mercury until the share counting them as abated reaches 55 per cent (s.4(2)): Ashgrove Cremation Centre and Tallis Creek Crematorium, about 59 per cent with both.
5. Levy: fee lines with a 2026 service date for fees the scheme rules list (rule 2.2), net of sales tax, at 4.5 per cent for cremations not abated and 1.5 per cent for abated ones, Wexley Regional and Hollinsbrook split at commissioning; a credit on a booking takes the booking's service date; each crematorium's levy rounded once.
6. Account credits: a credit posted to a funeral director's account with no booking takes the service dates of the lines it reverses, the lines on that account in the preceding months whose amounts sum to it (each credit settles in one scheme year only), so December 2025 services credited in 2026 are out and December 2026 services credited in 2027 are in.
7. Bypass: a DP HIGH alarm not cleared within five minutes opens the bypass until the next PLANT RESET (the inlet falls to ambient while cremators fire), together with the MANUAL BYPASS ON to OFF spells; each CREMATION cycle under way in a bypass (controller times in Eastern time against the plant log's UTC) counted once in the quarter of its charge.
8. The Fund designates Ashgrove Cremation Centre and Tallis Creek Crematorium for the 2026 scheme year.
