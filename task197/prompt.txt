What product on our watch list, if any, should we refer to the Child Packaging Safety Office's 2027 Child-Resistant Packaging Review, and what was the effect of its packaging change? The referral goes in packaging_referral.docx with the product, its effect, the runner-up and the gap between their effects. In referral_tables.xlsx give every watched product whose packaging changed with its effect and whether it reaches 1.20, and for each of our network's children's hospitals and each half-year from January 2025 to June 2026 the acetylcysteine courses started, the deferoxamine courses started for an iron poisoning and the two together. effects_chart.png shows each changed product's effect against the 1.20 line. Effects and the gap are ratios to one decimal and courses are whole numbers.
