One number goes to the board on 23 October: the collective expected credit loss allowance on our PL36 and PL60 personal loans at 30 September 2026, in Australian dollars to the nearest thousand. Build it the way CRM-ECL-04 sets out, with this quarter's approved scenarios, on the loan book as it stood at close of business on the 30th.

The workbook is `ecl_allowance_2026Q3.xlsx`. Besides the allowance, it needs four lines, one for each stage and product: the number of loans, the gross carrying amount and the allowance, both to the nearest thousand dollars, and the coverage ratio in per cent to two decimals. Then the allowance for each product split by the year the loans were drawn down (2024, 2025 and 2026 each on its own line, 2023 and earlier together), to the nearest thousand dollars, because the board watches how each year's lending is carrying. Add the number of loans the collective model leaves out as stage 3.

Stephanie Manning chairs the Audit and Risk Committee and will take the board through it, so she wants two slides, `board_ecl_q3_2026.pptx`: the first states the allowance with the stage and product table, the second charts the allowance by drawdown year for each product.
