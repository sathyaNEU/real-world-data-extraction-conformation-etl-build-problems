How many log truck loads will the FY2028 harvest plan need from our forests to Port Kowhai once the route opens to 50MAX trucks on 1 July 2027? matiri_fy2028_log_cartage_loads.xlsx should hold that figure, the loads for each export grade and the per-load working behind them, along with the FY2026 cartage paid against each carrier and the tonnes delivered to each domestic mill in each quarter of FY2026. Beside it, fy2028_loads_by_grade.svg should chart each grade's FY2026 recorded loads against its FY2028 loads. Loads are rounded to the nearest 100 for the total and the nearest 50 for each grade; cartage is NZD thousand rounded to the nearest thousand; mill tonnes are rounded to the nearest 10 t.
