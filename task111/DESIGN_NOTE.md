## ENTITY, UNIT, DECISION

The Scotland field region (six patches, 84 day-roster engineers, a separate out-of-hours standby pool, a contractor
panel on winter call-off) books repair and annual-service visits into two-hour arrival windows. A visit is kept
when the on-site time falls inside its window; a miss earns the household a goodwill credit.

Two quantities read as "the cause": the family with the most July to September misses under FS-07's charging
(a closed partition of the quarter, which the v1 portal responses computed exactly), and the family that will
cost the most over January to March if nothing changes (what the decision buys). They part because the quarter's
leader, Rota & cover, is generated only when the out-of-hours desk sends evening calls to day-roster engineers,
which it does in summer and does not do from October to May, when the contractor panel takes the calls standby
cannot reach.

Decision: exactly one cause family, the one that will be behind the most missed appointments over January to March
if nothing changes, graded with its July to September count; the change before January follows from it. The
committed call faces forward. The tag stays Root-Cause Analysis: the work is attributing every miss to its cause,
and the call names the cause to fix.
