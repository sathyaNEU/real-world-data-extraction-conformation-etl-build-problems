Not all of our FY2022 filings have a final decision yet, and the annual performance report still has to print their median pendency. It goes to the Minister for Enterprise on 31 January. I head performance statistics, so the headline section is mine to draft. Thomas Kennedy, our deputy commissioner, holds that our dockets are our applications and twenty years of docket figures already give us the answer. What I need from you is the median pendency of our FY2022 filings, in months to one decimal, as the one figure the headline prints.

That figure opens `fy2022_pendency_headline.docx`, written as the sentence the report will carry. Under it goes each of our eight technology groups with its lower-quartile pendency, its median pendency and the share of its FY2022 filings decided within 36 months, then the share of all FY2022 filings still undecided when the September extract was cut. Everything there is to one decimal, months for pendency and percentages for shares.

The same figure titles `fy2022_time_to_decision.svg`, which the report prints beside the section: the share of FY2022 filings decided by each month after filing as a step curve, a line at 50 per cent, the median marked and labelled with its value, and the undecided share annotated where the curve stops. No ranges.
