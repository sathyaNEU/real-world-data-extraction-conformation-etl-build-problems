Where did the Umzimela catchment's resource units carry more sulfate past their lower boundaries in the 2025/26 dry season than the agency's allocation schedule allows, so that the mines discharging into them are directed in 2027? The directives paper, sulfate_load_directives_2027.pdf, should mark every resource unit over or within its allocation and chart each unit's dry-season load against its allocation. With it, umzimela_sulfate_loads_2025_26.xlsx should hold each unit's load, the sulfate as SO4 each licensed mine discharge sent out in each quarter of the hydrological year, and, for each continuous EC logger, the hours in each month from November 2025 to April 2026 that the river's EC stood above the logger's trigger level in the network register. Loads are in tonnes a day to the nearest tonne, discharges in tonnes to the nearest ten and hours in whole hours.
