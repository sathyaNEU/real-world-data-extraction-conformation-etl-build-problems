Order too few examinations and every payment our digit filter routes past the order goes to Fernhollow at their premium rate. Order too many and we still pay for part of what sits unused. The 2027/28 call-off goes to them on Friday 12 March, and as head of exchequer services the quantity is mine to set. Douglas Harris at Fernhollow expects us to order whatever last year routed.

What I am ordering is the number of payments the filter will route for examination from April 2027 to March 2028, to the nearest hundred.

That number opens `examination_calloff_2027-28.docx`. Next to it, the department carrying the largest share and its own count, then the figure Fernhollow would size on from the latest closed year and how far we sit from it, all to the nearest hundred. One chart in the note: monthly routed payments from April 2025 to March 2028 stacked by department, a line at the monthly average of Fernhollow's closed-year figure, the plan year shaded, the busiest plan-year month annotated with its count, and our order in the title.

The order is only as good as its working, so `examination_calloff_2027-28.xlsx` carries it in three sheets. First, each plan-year month's routed payments. Second, 2025/26 month by month: the payments the filter routed and how many of those Fernhollow examined. Third, each quarter of 2025/26: the examinations they charged at the premium rate and the unused-volume charge.

Counts are whole payments and money is whole pounds.
