## Why it survives the solver

The pilot's plain solver reads every filed document and executes its rules, reproduces every published table, finds every join whose keys line up, replays records at the finest grain against closed periods, runs event studies on staggered cohorts and applies every stated clause, and the note's own decisive move fails that test (the 2021 intake's full rise and run-off sits in Housing Support's flagged-cell series, and a per-household replay recovers the 30-instalment term unprompted), so it is the rung the solver is expected to reach. The new decisive rung meets each habit with a stated defence. No document states it: the closure notice says the step-down places close and the guests leave by 31 March 2027, and no sentence says how a carer is paid or what a carer is paid afterwards. No join hands it over: no guest-level register ships and no payment line names a guest, band or arrangement, so a carer's composition is recoverable only by decomposing each amount into the scheme's weekly rates, an arithmetic identity rather than a key (RC01 v2 died on a two-hop join found from the schema alone). No reproduction check sees it: in all three statement years every step-down guest was in place, so the dual carers sat in cell 30 throughout and the statements reproduce identically under either reading. The replay's natural default is wrong only forward: a carer-level replay reproduces every closed period exactly at each carer's current amount, which is FC01's position (every charge replayed from the minute it started). No cohort or event study sees it: the closure is a future date with no step in any closed series. And the stated clause is executed and returns nothing: the first-order check the notice invites, whether step-down payments sit in a flagged cell, finds none (step-down-only carers are paid in cell 15, dual carers in cells 29 and 30), which is OS01's position, a natural reading the files support and nothing contradicts. What could still crack it is a solver that follows the closure to each affected carer's future amount; the design keeps that a construction by naming no carer in the notice and giving no step-down guest a payment line of its own.
