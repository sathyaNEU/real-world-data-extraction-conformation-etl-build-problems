Which of our four lending books should the Financial Support Team work in 2027, how many possessions would it avoid there, and how many would it avoid in the next best book? The answer goes to the Credit Committee as a paper, support_team_2027_placement_paper.pdf, with a chart of the possessions each book would avoid. In support_team_2027_workbook.xlsx, for each owner-occupied book: the possession orders the courts made or varied at hearings from October 2025 to September 2026, how many of those were suspended on terms, and the possession claims open at 30 September 2026; then the possessions sold with completion from October 2025 to September 2026, how many of them were sold at a loss, and the average loss per possession sold. Possessions avoided to the nearest ten, average losses to the nearest hundred pounds.
