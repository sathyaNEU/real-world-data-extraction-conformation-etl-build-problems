Which junction on Ardleshire's 2026 screening list should take the roundabout in the county's 2027 Junction Safety Fund bid, and how many injury collisions a year would a roundabout prevent at each listed junction? The bid document, junction_bid_2027.docx, should name the junction and give every listed junction's benefit as the fund's guidance defines it, with a bar chart of those benefits marking the junction chosen. In bid_annex_2026.xlsx put the two supporting tables the guidance asks for: the county's injury collisions in each quarter of 2026 up to September by severity, and their casualties in each of those quarters by road user. Benefits to one decimal place; collision and casualty counts whole.
