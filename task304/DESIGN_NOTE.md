## Three rules

- **Task type:** screening-diagnostic-policy-evaluation (Epidemiology & Public Health), serving Opportunity Sizing &
  Decision Support: a screening programme placed and sized by its yield, pool to realisable (people met, tested,
  found, started).
- **Domain test:** the deciding quantity is a screening programme's forward case-finding yield in a defined
  population, which the field estimates from the prevalence of undiagnosed infection among the people a programme will
  meet, distinguishing a first (prevalent) round from later (incident) rounds and allowing for provision elsewhere;
  swapping it for a count, a score or a sort loses the call.
- **Swap:** "where would our van start the most people on hepatitis C treatment in 2027" with "where would our van
  sell the most coffee" keeps a ranking but loses the call: a coffee buyer is not cured by last year's sale, so a
  population a first round has already screened and treated has no analogue, and neither has a pharmacist's test that
  removes the infections the van would find.
