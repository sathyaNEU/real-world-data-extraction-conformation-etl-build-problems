Wave 8 of the comms firmware rollout goes out on Tuesday night, and the rollout desk moves nothing onto a build the read-health alert fires on. I own that alert for metering data services. Eight builds are in the field: EM3.10.9, EM3.11.4, EM3.12.0, EM3.12.1, EM3.12.2, PX5.2.7, PX5.3.0 and PX5.3.1. Which of them does the alert fire on this week, and which hold?

That call goes to the alert router as `wave8_read_health_alerts.json`. Fire and hold lists first, then one record per build, ordered from furthest below its lower limit to furthest above, with the number of installations it was assessed on, its read health and its lower limit. Read health and limits are percentages to two decimals; counts are whole numbers. Two more things go in it. One is how many installations the Wave 8 schedule would move onto EM3.12.2 and onto PX5.3.1 if it goes ahead. The other is, for each build Wave 7 moved installations onto this week, how many it moved and how many interval reads those installations were still missing at the delivery cut-off on the market day they moved.

The go/no-go call runs off `read_health_by_build_wk41.png`, titled with the builds that fire: one row per build in that same order and labelled with its assessed installations, read health as a point, its lower limit as a tick beside it, and the reference drawn as a line with its value on it.
