# task280 design note

Architecture 1, drawn 2026-10-24. Wave 6, Descriptive lane. Seed DA30's world is kept at the author's choice (a broadband regulator's measurement panel on one cable provider, households judged on delivered download speed against their plan at the evening peak); the seed's mechanism rows and pilot log are decoy-era design and are set aside (the rows on node-specific busy hours and the pilot log were read in error past the header `status.py` prints, and nothing here uses them). The Orlenford Valley Cable Commission, the joint cable franchising authority of eight towns, holds Tollard Cable to the Network Performance Schedule of its franchise agreement: each December the Commission designates the hubs whose panel households' connections spent more than 20 per cent of the autumn window's weekday evening peak below 80 per cent of their plan's advertised download speed, and Tollard must add downstream capacity in every designated hub before the next autumn window. The evidence is the Commission's own measurement panel (1,140 Tollard subscriber households with a measurement unit on the line), Tollard's node register and its 15-minute node utilisation filing.

## Three rules

Task type: General Statistics & Data Science, `missing-data-censoring-measurement-error`: a share over time estimated from measurements whose missingness depends on a state recorded in another file; it serves Descriptive & Distribution Analysis (each hub's share of peak time below the standard and its distribution across the hubs against the line).
Domain test: the deciding quantity is each hub's share of weekday peak time below 80 per cent of plan, a missing-data estimate in which the completed tests are missing at random only given the node's utilisation in the quarter-hour; the field conditions on that auxiliary record (each household's tests re-weighted to its node's full peak load distribution, or the load at which its connection drops under 80 per cent located and the node's peak quarter-hours above it counted), where the share of tests is the complete-case estimate and the hour weighting conditions on the wrong variable.
Swap: with a fixed sensor reading every quarter-hour in place of the panel unit (a water meter, a ward's call-bell log) the sentence still parses but nothing is missing and the share of readings is the answer; with the share of completed tests in place of the missing-data estimate, the generic summary any field would compute, the call changes. The task is the missingness mechanism, so swapping either the probe or the estimate empties it.
Layer 1: Tollard's utilisation filing stamps intervals in UTC and the panel's export in local time with its offset, across the 1 November clock change; plan changes are effective-dated in the subscription history; a failed measurement unit is replaced under a new unit id at the same address; nodes split during the window are renamed in the node register with effective dates; the panel's collector re-uploads a unit's buffered batch after an outage, so some tests arrive twice; the panel export gives throughput in kbit/s and the plan catalogue advertised speeds in Mbit/s. Each is written by the system that holds it; none is placed to be caught, and each is asserted at stage 3 to leave the call where it is when mishandled.
Layer 2: `estimation_fit`, own:missing at random given an auxiliary utilisation record: the share of completed tests, overall, by hour or by night, describes a lighter load than the peak holds; conditioning on the node's utilisation flips the call.
Decisive step: condition each household's weekday peak tests on its node's utilisation in the tested quarter-hour and carry that to every quarter-hour of the peak, either by re-weighting the tests to the node's full peak load distribution or by locating the load at which the household's connection falls under 80 per cent of plan and counting the node's peak quarter-hours above it.
Not semantics: the methods note's skip rule, read correctly, says when a test is skipped, not whether skipping is related to speed; every field, code, unit and definition read correctly (throughput, plan speeds, the peak's hours, the hub of each node, the plan in force) leaves the share of tests, overall or hour by hour, on the default set, and the relation between skipping and congestion appears only when the node loads at the completed tests are set against the node's full peak record, which no field states.
Not rule application: the Schedule files the estimand (every quarter-hour of every weekday evening peak in the window, every panel household served from the hub counted equally, the plan in force on the day) and nothing on how to estimate the speed in the quarter-hours no test reached. The enumerated family (the share of tests; weighted by hour; weighted by night; every skipped test counted below the standard; load-bin re-weighting at 10 and 20 bins; the household's threshold load applied to its node's quarter-hours) names 3 to 4 distinct designated sets on each of prototype seeds 1 to 12 (`scratchpad t280/p6.py`, line at 20 per cent); the standing family, the constructions conditioned on the node's utilisation, names one set on each seed, its members within 0.35 to 0.77 points of each other on every hub. What rules the others out is the comparison of the node loads at the completed tests with the node's full peak record, which no filed clause makes and no group-by on the test file performs. Measured again on the generator at stage 3.
Analyst test: a strong analyst who reads every field correctly, keeps the weekday 7 to 11 pm tests of the window and takes each hub's share of those tests below 80 per cent of the household's plan speed (or weights each evening hour equally) files the default set, because the units skip a test while the household's own line is busy and household use rises on the same nights the hub's nodes congest; executed on the generator's clean truth in `checks.py` (R3a ANALYST), where the decisive step alone files the golden (R3b DECISIVE).
Estimand: the Network Performance Schedule of the franchise agreement (stage 2 fixes its section numbers): a hub is designated when, over the autumn measurement window, the connections of the panel households served from it delivered less than 80 per cent of the advertised download speed of the plan in force for more than 20 per cent of the weekday evening peak, 7 pm to 11 pm local time Monday to Friday, taken over every quarter-hour of the peak for every such household, each household counted equally.
No decoy: nothing is planted to be caught: no memo, earlier estimate, provider report or draft states a hub's share or ranks the hubs; the tests, the unit log, the register, the utilisation filing and the plan catalogue are the panel's and Tollard's own records, and the 2025 close-out summary answers another question (last autumn's designations and the capacity Tollard added).
No certified replay: no certified or published figure carries a hub's 2026 share, so no method is recovered by reproducing one.
Lesson: when a probe is silenced by activity that rises with the load that degrades the outcome, the completed probes describe a lighter load than the window holds, and the share of the window is estimated by conditioning on the load's own record rather than on the clock. task273 architecture 1 taught the closed-loop form, where the outcome itself silences the probe; here the probe is silenced by a correlate, and the clock correction the field reaches for first is the one that fails.

## Tried and rejected

- The seed's decision as filed (one national household share at one decimal): a single figure whose admissible estimators spread by more than its rounding; moved to the hubs designated against a line, which the estimator family can agree on with margin.
- Each hub's share of households meeting the standard (the household's median speed at peak at least 80 per cent of plan), prototype `p3.py`: the household's median sits on a knife edge at its threshold load, so the load-conditioned estimators disagreed by up to 6 points on a hub (seed 2, hub A); moved to the share of peak time below the standard, whose household terms are fractions and average out (`p4.py`, the family within 0.3 to 0.8 points).
- Household activity drawn independently quarter by quarter (`p1.py`, `p2.py`): the deferral bias was too weak to move a hub (13 of 1,140 households flipped); sessions that persist across quarter-hours, event nights shared by the households and the nodes, and heavier use on the faster plans give the bias its realistic size (`p5.py`, about 18 per cent of scheduled weekday peak tests skipped).
