It joins one desk in January for the year, and the planning meeting on 4 November wants one name from the shortlist: Politics·national, Sport·metro, Business·national, Sport·national, Local·metro or Culture·national. Kayla Torres, our head of audience, says it belongs wherever the readers are. As managing editor, the call is mine. Tell me which desk the squad should join and the extra article clicks it would add there in 2027, to the nearest 50,000.

That desk and that number open squad_placement_2027.docx, the paper the meeting reads, with the runner-up and how far behind it sits. Its one chart walks every desk from its 2027 clicks to the extra clicks the squad would add, in order of those extra clicks, with the chosen desk and the runner-up marked, the gap between them labelled and a title naming the desk.

Behind that number, squad_placement_2027.xlsx gives each desk a row. The row holds its extra clicks for 2027 and, over the twelve months to September, its average monthly readers to the nearest thousand, the extra clicks per reader to one decimal place, its headline corrections and their rate per 1,000 articles to one decimal place, and the median minutes from an article going live to its first headline correction. Click totals are to the nearest 50,000 throughout, and counts and minutes are whole numbers.
