Which of the Clay Street storage tank alternatives should the authority carry into design so that Outfall 014 meets the consent decree's limit of four untreated overflow events in an average year? The working belongs in clay_street_storage_evaluation.xlsx with the events a year each alternative would leave, and beside it two things the annual report needs from the same pull: the rainfall each of the authority's rain gauges recorded in each month of 2025, and the volume the Northside tank returned to the Northside interceptor in each year from 2021 to 2025. The board will see the selection and the events by alternative as a chart in clay_street_tank_selection_board.pptx. Events a year are to one decimal, rainfall to the hundredth of an inch and returned volumes to the nearest million gallons.
