Where does the 2027 zero-emission controller conversion go under our methane reduction plan? The conversion order rests on our 2026 source-level inventory, built on the MRS-03 Annex B template as larchmere_source_inventory_2026.xlsx with one row per intermittent-vent controller and the methane it vented, each pad's intermittent-vent methane on the pads sheet, a sheet giving each plunger lift well's 2026 cycles that ended without the plunger arriving, and a table of the sales gas pressure controllers' 2026 methane for each maker in each of our Kakwa and Elmworth areas. For the order itself, controller_conversion_2027.pptx should list any pads that go in with each one's 2026 intermittent-vent methane on one slide and chart every pad against the plan's line on a second. Methane per controller and per maker goes in kilograms to the nearest kilogram, and per pad in tonnes to two decimals.
