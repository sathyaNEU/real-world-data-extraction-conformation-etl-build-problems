## Three rules

Task type: accessibility-agglomeration-location-analysis (Urban & Regional Science), tagged in the taxonomy for Opportunity Sizing & Decision Support: a new grocery store's trade from a spatial-interaction model of every origin's grocery spend within reach, sized as its addition to the chain's annual contribution, pool to realisable.
Domain test: The deciding quantity is the new store's trade from the retail geographer's spatial-interaction model, each origin's grocery spend shared among the stores in reach by floor area and distance deterrence, and an origin is wherever spend starts: a holiday park whose guests live outside the region is an origin the census never counts, priced per pitch from the out-of-region cardholders at the chain's stores beside other parks, which moves the pick from Ashby Moor to Pellow.
Swap: "the trade a new store wins once the guests on a holiday park's pitches are carried as an origin the census never counts" put into a hospital's patients or a utility's megawatt-hours loses its mechanism: no hospital catchment is fed by a register of pitches and a feed of where card holders live, no load forecast shares demand among sites by floor area over distance, and neither has a census that counts its users where they sleep.
Layer 1: Census 2021 output areas with population-weighted centroids on the British National Grid; an ONSPD extract with terminated postcodes and their months; the VOA rating list in its own descriptions with the summary valuation's main measure in m2, parking spaces or pitches; a payment acquirer's merchant-insight feed by month and cardholder home postcode sector, out-of-region sectors included, with year-round visitors of residents in every store's rows; modelled grocery spend per household by output area; the chain's April to March year against calendar-month feeds; nothing is placed to be caught.
Layer 2: field_method: the spatial-interaction model's demand side is every origin of grocery spend within reach, not the census's residents alone; a holiday park is an origin with no census count, its guests' in-store spend per pitch measured where the chain already trades beside parks (the out-of-region cardholders' spend above their year-round level, grossed up by the store's card share, over the pitches whose spend the model gives that store), and carried at the new store's share of the park's spend.
Decisive step: carrying the holiday parks as origins of grocery spend, each at its pitches times the season's in-store spend per pitch that the card feed measures at Brindley and Stoneacre, rather than taking the census output areas as every origin of the spend the six sites compete for.
Not semantics: every field reads as what it is (usual residents and households, weekly spend per household, the rating list's description and measure, each cardholder's home sector, each store's card share of sales), the model's parameters are read as filed, and the census is complete for its residents; no field, code or definition says that anyone shops in the region who does not live in it, and the analyst who reads all of it correctly still shares the census households' spend.
Not rule application: the model sheet files the WHAT (each origin's grocery spend shared among the stores of 280 m2 or more by area over deterrence, 88 per cent in store, the appraisal's 26 per cent less what the store takes from ours less its running costs) and says nothing about which origins there are; executing every filed rule over the census, which is what both probes did, files S1 Ashby Moor.
Analyst test: A strong analyst who reads every file correctly runs the filed model over the census households' spend, confirms it on the card feed and approves S1 Ashby Moor at about 0.6 million, where the golden is S3 Pellow at about 0.9 million; checks.py will run both on the generator's clean truth and on the pack.
Estimand: the addition to Wenlow Foodstores' annual contribution of the one store the committee approves, once it is trading: 26 per cent of its sales, less 26 per cent of the sales it takes from the chain's ten existing stores, less its running costs, its sales being its share of the grocery spend of every origin within reach (`spend_share_model_2025`, appraisal paragraph).
No decoy: nothing is planted: the census is ONS's count of residents, the rating list the VOA's, the feed the acquirer's record of card spend; no memo, draft or figure argues for a site, no file ranks the six, no past appraisal or forecast of a site is in the pack, and no stakeholder figure is set up to be overturned.
No certified replay: no certified or published figure sits in the pack to be reproduced; the estate's sales are actuals the model is never fitted to, and the method is not recovered from any figure.
Lesson: a spatial-interaction model is only as complete as its origin set; where the census is the origin set, any lodging whose occupants live elsewhere is demand the model cannot see, and the chain's own card feed, read by where cardholders live and when they come, is what prices it; no build on file prices an origin of demand that its demand data never counts.
