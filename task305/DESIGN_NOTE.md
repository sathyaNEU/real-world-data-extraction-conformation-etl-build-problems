## Three rules

- Task type: product-analytics, churn-propensity-uplift-modeling (serves Opportunity Sizing & Decision Support): the
  revenue a three-project limit on the free plan adds in calendar 2027 in each of five apps, pool (the free accounts the
  limit reaches) to realisable (what they pay less the free-plan revenue of those who go), the call the app where it adds
  most.
- Domain test: the deciding quantity is the limit's uplift on an app's revenue, which product analytics estimates from
  accounts exposed to a change of the same kind set against comparable accounts not exposed (a forced choice between
  paying, trimming and leaving), and it turns on each app's freemium economics: the Plus price the payers bring against the
  free-plan revenue (marketplace commission, advertising, partner referrals) the leavers take away.
- Swap: "a hospital trust choosing which clinic takes a three-appointment cap on free physiotherapy" keeps a cap and a
  choice of site, but no clinic has a paid plan an over-cap patient moves to, a free-plan revenue a patient who leaves
  takes away, or an existing allowance whose stopped users show the response, so the reasoning that decides has no
  counterpart.
- Layer 1: Notewell carried as Leafnote before April 2025 in the panel, the register and the monthly count; the monthly
  count's layout and project bands changing between the 2024 and 2025 sheets; storage in MiB against the price list's

by a counter-based hash of account, month and slot, so the run with the limit and the run without it share every draw.
