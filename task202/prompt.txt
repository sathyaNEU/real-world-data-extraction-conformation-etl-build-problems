What should the 2027/28 referral ceiling be for a four-person household under the Household Relief Grant? Clause 9 caps the share of its awards that would be referred at 6.0 per cent, each band's ceiling generated from the reference-size one by schedule B's scale. Partners claim monthly in aggregate, so a return gives a total and an award count per partner and band; only the two pilot provinces claim award by award on the portal. Put each band's ceiling, referred shares of awards and value and scale factor in relief_referral_ceilings_2027-28.xlsx and plot the distribution against them in referral_ceiling_board_paper.pdf. Add claims_annex.xlsx for claim months to December 2025: by the claim month's scheme quarter the pesos the runs transferred, each instruction once, and the pesos the checks held back, to the nearest peso, and the partner claim-months the check recorded a hold in, by reason. Round ceilings up to PHP 100, shares to one decimal and factors to three decimals.
