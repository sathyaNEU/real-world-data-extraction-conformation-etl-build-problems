What should the 2027/28 referral ceiling be for a four-person household under the Household Relief Grant? Clause 9 commits the scheme to ceilings that would refer no more than 6.0 per cent of its awards, each band's ceiling generated from the reference-size one by schedule B's equivalence scale. Partners claim monthly in aggregate, so a return gives a total and an award count per partner per household-size band; only the two pilot provinces claim award by award on the portal. Put each band's ceiling, referred shares of awards and value and scale factor in relief_referral_ceilings_2027-28.xlsx, and plot the distribution against the ceilings in referral_ceiling_board_paper.pdf. Add claims_annex.xlsx for claim months to December 2025: by scheme quarter the pesos the payment runs transferred and the claim checks held back, to the nearest peso, and the claim-months with a hold by reason. Round ceilings up to the nearest PHP 100, shares to one decimal place and factors to three decimals.
