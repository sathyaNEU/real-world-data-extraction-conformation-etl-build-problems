Which shortlisted programme should the Selwith Recovery Fund back for the 2027 bloom season, and by how much does it beat the runner-up? Its decision paper, selwith_fund_recommendation_2027.pdf, names the programme with its figure and the runner-up's and charts every programme's figure for the 2024, 2025 and 2026 seasons as bars; selwith_phosphorus_workings.xlsx holds those figures, the River Lenning's inflow to the lake and its phosphorus load from October 2023 to April 2024, the north basin's June-to-September mean surface-water total phosphorus and chlorophyll a in 2025 and in 2026, and how many dwellings Connect Right found draining foul water to the surface-water drains, how many a surveyor confirmed fixed by 30 September 2026 and how many were not. Phosphorus is in whole kilograms except the winter load, to the nearest 100 kg; the inflow is in million cubic metres and concentrations in micrograms per litre, both to one decimal.
