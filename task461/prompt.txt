Did Pellow House's base building meet the 2026 emissions target in our sustainability-linked loan, and by how much did it miss or beat it? For the verifier, pellow_house_2026_kpi_report.xlsx needs the base building's electricity in each month of 2026 and over the year, its Scope 2 emissions and its intensity per square metre of net lettable area as the loan's KPI schedule defines them, against the target, and beside them the building's Scope 1 lines: the gas oil the standby generator burned in each quarter and the refrigerant each chiller released over the year, each with its emissions. Send pellow_house_2026_kpi_letter.pdf to the bank with it, stating the result against the target, with a chart of the base building's monthly electricity. Electricity in whole megawatt-hours, gas oil in whole litres, refrigerant in kilograms to one decimal, Scope 1 emissions in whole kilograms of CO2e, Scope 2 emissions in tonnes and intensity in kilograms per square metre to one decimal.
