What induction dose of dovikizumab, 300, 600 or 900 mg IV at weeks 0, 4 and 8, should Corvane carry into phase 3 under its dose-selection charter, when the phase 2b study tested only 600 mg against placebo? In phase2b_induction_tables.xlsx give, for each arm, the patients with at least one treatment-emergent serious infection, the patients with at least one infusion-related reaction, serious infections per 100 patient-years, the treatment-emergent anti-drug antibody incidence at weeks 4, 8 and 12, and the patients in symptomatic remission on the e-diary at weeks 6, 8 and 10.
