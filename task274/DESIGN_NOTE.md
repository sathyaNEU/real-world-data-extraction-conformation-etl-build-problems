The Merrivane Health District's Center for Health Statistics runs the district's Health and Nutrition Examination Survey. Its 2025 wave examined adults as one mobile examination centre moved through the district's eight counties a county at a time, and the contracted laboratory measured serum 25-hydroxyvitamin D on two immunoassay analyzers through six reagent lots and four calibrator lots, recalibrating at every lot change, every 28 days and after maintenance. The district's 2027 vitamin D programme (a supplementation and fall-prevention offer) opens first in the counties where more than 30 per cent of adults were below 20 ng/mL on the scale of the reference measurement procedure, the scale the cut-off was set on, and the board of supervisors votes on those counties. The laboratory's one accuracy-based check, in October 2025, measured the assay under one calibration of one analyzer; at the wave's close the survey re-assayed a random 8 per cent of its stored duplicate aliquots in one December run, the only link from that calibration to the others.
