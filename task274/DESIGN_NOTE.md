# task274 design note

Architecture 3, drawn 2026-11-02 after the author re-tasked the slot from Descriptive & Distribution Analysis to Experiment & Causal Analysis (the author's decision of 2026-11-01, retirement not taken) and the descriptive draw had screened about 170 candidates dead (Tried and rejected). The world, the domain and the dataset are new. Larkmoor County's prosecuting attorney runs a pre-plea diversion programme for first-time defendants charged with eligible misdemeanours and low-level felonies, delivered under contract by Nethercote Community Justice Services. The provider had places for about half of the eligible referrals, so from June 2025 the office offered the programme by monthly lottery among eligible defendants. A defendant who signs the diversion agreement spends twelve months in the programme; completion ends in a dismissal, and termination (a new arrest or sustained non-compliance) sends the case back to the trial calendar, where it is disposed months later like any other case. Nethercote's contract ends on 31 December 2026, and the Board of Commissioners' pilot resolution ties the renewal to how far being offered the programme lowers the share of eligible defendants convicted in the referred case against defendants not offered it: renewal at full scale (every eligible defendant offered) on a cut of at least 25 percentage points, at the pilot's scale on at least 15, and a lapse below 15. The data run to the 31 August 2026 cut, fifteen months after the first lottery.

## Default path

1. Read Board Resolution 2025-31: the report gives the share of listed defendants convicted in the referred case at its final disposition, not offered less offered, in points; 25 or more renews at full scale, 15 or more at the pilot's scale, less lets the agreement lapse.
2. Take each draw's list and result from `diversion_lottery_draws_2025_2026.xlsx` (3,608 listed, 1,801 offered) and join the docket's events from both extracts on the case number, normalised across the legacy and the new system's formats; drop clerk re-entries and the migrated copies of pre-cutover events; put the new system's UTC stamps on Central dates.
3. Mark a case convicted on its first disposition event if that is a guilty plea, a guilty verdict or a guilty finding on the stipulated record, not convicted on a dismissal, a nolle prosequi, an acquittal or a dismissal on completion, open otherwise (1,236 offered cases and 89 not-offered cases are open on 31 August 2026).
4. Analyse as randomised (the field's method): every listed defendant in the arm the draw put them in, decliners kept in the offered arm. Cases are still open, so estimate each arm's cumulative incidence of conviction by days since the draw, the other endings competing and open cases censored at 31 August 2026 (Aalen-Johansen), and read it at the longest follow-up, 452 days: 56.0 per cent not offered, 25.3 offered.
5. Check the read: the not-offered curve settles by month 9 at the share the five fully disposed lotteries show, and the offered curve adds about three points between month 9 and month 14, so both look settled.

Ends on: renew at full scale, a cut of 31 percentage points.

Golden: renew at the pilot's scale, a cut of 18 percentage points.

## Stump sentence

A competent analyst analyses the lottery as randomised, reads each arm's cumulative incidence of conviction at the longest follow-up and renews at full scale on a cut of 31 points (the recorded shares give 36); the offer defers its own outcome, since a participant is convicted only after the program terminates them and a terminated case is then tried on the stipulated record months later, and the program terminates almost a quarter of those who reach its final review, so only completing every open offered case through the program's month-by-month termination chance, the final review included, and the conviction rate terminated cases reach files a cut of 18 points and renewal at the pilot's scale.

## Three rules

- Task type: Criminology & Justice, intervention-policing-policy-evaluation, the client's task type for evaluating a justice intervention's effect, which serves Experiment & Causal Analysis.
- Domain test: the deciding quantity is the diversion lottery's intention-to-treat effect on conviction in the referred case at final disposition, everyone offered a place against everyone not offered as the draw assigned them, the estimate criminal justice evaluation makes of a randomised diversion offer; the field's own reading of such an offer is the randomised contrast, and the call rests on that contrast's value.
- Swap: rewritten for a hospital ("how far being offered a care-management place lowers the share of patients readmitted"), the call loses what decides it: no prosecution is deferred, no failed participant returns to a trial calendar and no stipulated record turns a failure into a conviction, so the completion through a deferral stage and a post-failure conviction rate has nothing to run on.
- Layer 1: two docket extracts either side of the court's 2 March 2026 cutover (legacy codes and MM/DD/YYYY dates against descriptive event types and UTC stamps, case numbers in two formats, open cases' earlier events migrated into both), clerk re-entries in the legacy extract, sealed cases' gaps in the number series, the provider's workbooks (header blocks, court references typed five ways, a new system from 16 January 2026 with its own columns), the penal code renumbered on 1 January 2026 and the county's July fiscal year; each is written by the system named, none is placed to be caught, and none moves the call (`checks.py` R2 rows).
- Layer 2: estimation_fit: the offered arm's outcome is completed through the stages the offer itself creates (the program's month-by-month termination chance through its final review, read from every participant's months, and the conviction rate terminated cases reach), not through each arm's cumulative incidence by time since the draw, whose longest follow-up ends before most offered cases can be convicted.
- Decisive step: recognising that the offer defers its own outcome and completing every open offered case through the program's stages: a participant still in the program fails in the months left on the term at the rate each program month terminates participants, the final review included, which a life table over every participant's months on the term gives, and a terminated case convicts at the rate terminated cases already disposed reach; decliners and not-offered defendants end at their own arms' disposed rates.
- Not semantics: every field read correctly (each agreement's term in its docket entry, every disposition code, both extracts, the UTC stamps, the resolution's estimand) still leaves the arms' cumulative incidence at 31 points and the recorded shares at 36; no code or definition marks which participant will fail at the final review, and the 18 comes only from the structure of the process.
- Not rule application: no filed rule completes an open case; the resolution fixes the estimand and the tiers and leaves the construction to the analysis. Enumerated family (every construction a competent analyst reaches for) spans minus 1.8 to 35.6 points: cases already ended minus 0.3, a product limit timed from the agreement filing minus 1.8, a constant monthly termination rate 22.6, participants only 20.6, the cumulative incidence at one year, fourteen months and the longest follow-up 34.0, 33.0 and 30.7, the share terminated so far 31.2, terminated cases convicting like the not-offered 30.2, the term without its final review 32.9, the three earliest lotteries at the cut 32.5, the recorded shares 35.6. - Analyst test: a strong analyst who reads every field and the resolution correctly analyses the lottery as randomised and reads each arm's cumulative incidence of conviction at the longest follow-up, a cut of 31 points that renews at full scale (the recorded shares, 36 points, file the same tier); open offered cases convict only after terminations the program has not yet made, so only completing them through the program's month-by-month termination chance and the terminated cases' own conviction rate files 18 points and the pilot's scale.

- Lesson: a model that gets it right learns that a randomised offer which defers its own outcome cannot be read off the arms' outcome curves before the deferral has run its course, and that the arm is completed through the stages the offer creates, each estimated where the record observes it in full; no build on file teaches it (task172 v2 reads an effect that changes with time since assignment, task191 completes undecided cases by outcome-specific decision times inside one population, task133 conditions a filed curve on survival to date).

## Survivor anatomy

- Actor: the deciding property, each open offered case's chance of ending in conviction, has no column: it is a relation between the case's term start, the evaluation date and every other participant's months on the program, and between terminations and the dispositions that follow them; no file stores a final outcome, a remaining risk or a program month, and the roster carries only the draw.
- Link: the signal is read through borrowing the field's method never asks for: the randomised contrast and the cumulative incidence by time since the draw never condition on a post-randomisation stage, while the completion takes the final review's rate from the earliest cohorts alone (only participants from the June, July and August 2025 draws have reached it) and the conviction rate from terminated cases, a subgroup the randomised contrast never forms; no key or clause joins either to the open cases.
- Reflex: the field's method sits at rung 0 and rung 1: analyse as randomised (recorded shares, 36 points) and, for open cases, each arm's cumulative incidence of conviction with competing endings and censoring at the cut, read at the longest follow-up, 31 points; both renew at full scale.
- Back-test: the textbook route validates: the not-offered curve settles by month 9 at 56 per cent, the share the five fully disposed lotteries show, and the offered curve adds about three points from month 9 to month 14, so a solver checking for a plateau finds one; the final review's convictions fall after 452 days for every cohort.
- Second use: every file the decisive step reads has its own reason: the roster is the draw's record of every defendant's arm, the docket extracts are the court's record of every criminal case (and carry the eligible-pool ask), and the term in each agreement's docket entry is how the court records a case continued for diversion; deleting the decisive step from the design drops no file.
- Shock: what the completion recovers reads as ordinary: a low recorded conviction share is what a working diversion program is expected to show, the month-12 spike in the life table is a program's end-of-term review, and no lottery, division or month is the pack's largest anomaly.
- Pins: the WHAT is filed in the Board's own resolution (population, outcome at final disposition with open cases counted by how they will end, comparison, unit, the records' date, tiers); no filed sentence names the program clock, the final review, a termination rate, a conviction rate after termination or any completion; the services agreement's term clause states the term as any such agreement does, and deleting it leaves the term dates in every agreement's docket entry, so nothing forced hangs on it.
