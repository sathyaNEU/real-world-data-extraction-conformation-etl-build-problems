# task274 design note

Architecture 3, drawn 2026-11-02 after the author re-tasked the slot from Descriptive & Distribution Analysis to Experiment & Causal Analysis (the author's decision of 2026-11-01, retirement not taken) and the descriptive draw had screened about 170 candidates dead (Tried and rejected). The world, the domain and the dataset are new. Larkmoor County's prosecuting attorney runs a pre-plea diversion programme for first-time defendants charged with eligible misdemeanours and low-level felonies, delivered under contract by Nethercote Community Justice Services. The provider had places for about half of the eligible referrals, so from June 2025 the office offered the programme by monthly lottery among eligible defendants. A defendant who signs the diversion agreement spends twelve months in the programme; completion ends in a dismissal, and termination (a new arrest or sustained non-compliance) sends the case back to the trial calendar, where it is disposed months later like any other case. Nethercote's contract ends on 31 December 2026, and the Board of Commissioners' pilot resolution ties the renewal to how far being offered the programme lowers the share of eligible defendants convicted in the referred case against defendants not offered it: renewal at full scale (every eligible defendant offered) on a cut of at least 25 percentage points, at the pilot's scale on at least 15, and a lapse below 15. The data run to the 31 August 2026 cut, fifteen months after the first lottery.
