## Stump power
High. A strong model that cleans the data and reads every document correctly still has to discover from the offer history that acceptance depends on carrying on from the casual's own shift and then forecast per store; the natural routes (rank by listed open hours, last year's fill rate, or availability-only supply) land on Werribee. Without the carry-on effect Werribee (626) beats Camberwell (555), so the stump is not softened by the availability story. The 10 to 16 weekday window at Camberwell means Camberwell stays near the top even for a partial solver, so a solver who finds only the availability gap can still land on the right store; the ladder is held by Werribee/Box Hill, not by every wrong route. Two-thirds of asks are heavy-reconciliation, which should depress the top-two average.
