Twelve groups is what one watch-list analyst carries, and on 4 November the executive risk committee sets 2027 headcount from the caseload I put to it. How many of the obligor groups in our monitored book at 30 September will reach the watch list by 30 September 2027, as one whole number?

That number heads `watchlist_inflow_2027.xlsx`. Under it, one row per exposure band we report caseload in, smallest first: expected arrivals from groups the September early-warning run flagged, expected arrivals from groups it did not, and the committed exposure all of them are expected to bring, with a total row. Expected arrivals to one decimal, exposure in £m to one decimal.

The committee will look at `watchlist_inflow_2027.svg`: a stacked bar per band in that order, flagged and not flagged, the caseload written on the chart and a title the room can quote. I head credit risk monitoring.
