How many of the seven commercial vehicle uses we license should the Commercial Fleet Safety Programme cover from 1 July 2027, and which, under the coverage rule the Commission adopted in October? The operators of each covered use have to be told by 15 January to fit their vehicles in time, so put every use and whether the rule covers it in fleet_safety_programme_2027.xlsx, and chart each use's rate per mile of causing crashes against the rule's line in fleet_safety_programme_2027.pptx. The workbook also needs, for each of the seven uses, the people killed or seriously injured in all the crashes its drivers caused from 2021 to 2025, not only those between moving vehicles, and what those crashes cost at the Department of Transportation's crash unit costs. Give the people as whole numbers and the costs in millions of dollars to one decimal.
