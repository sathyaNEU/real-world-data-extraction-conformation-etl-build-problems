## Three rules

Task type: General Statistics & Data Science, `missing-data-censoring-measurement-error`: a rate whose denominator no record holds, the missing exposure recovered from a sample inside the records and scaled by an external measured total; it serves Descriptive & Distribution Analysis (each use's rate set against a reference rate and a line).
Domain test: the deciding quantity is each use's rate of causing crashes per mile driven, whose miles are unobserved; the field estimates exposure by induced exposure (the not-at-fault parties of crashes as a draw from the traffic) and the estimate holds only where crash intensity is the same for every vehicle in the sample, so the field's own construction is the stratified share scaled by measured vehicle miles.
Swap: with hospital wards for uses, patient falls for crashes and bed-days for miles the sentence still reads, but no ward's bed-days are missing and no fall leaves a second party drawn from the beds in proportion to their days, so the task loses both its missing denominator and the sample that recovers it; it only works where a crash's other party is a draw from the traffic.
Layer 1: two editions of the state crash report (2016 and 2023: different codes, fault recorded per driver or per crash, one code for every passenger service for hire); State Patrol dates and times in UTC across daylight saving, three agencies' local time formats; 2016 crashes located only by legacy route number and milepost, joined through the road inventory for their class; 2016 vehicles for hire and delivery resolved to a use through the operator register's names on reports; VMT tables inside a PDF in millions and thousands; count station hours in a wide file by station, day and direction with missing days; each produced by the system named, none placed to be caught.
Layer 2: `estimation_fit`: the struck vehicles are a sample selected by crash occurrence (outcome-dependent sampling), so their shares estimate miles only within strata of one crash intensity and must be re-weighted by each stratum's measured miles; pooled against stratified-and-scaled flips the call.
Decisive step: induced exposure re-weighted to miles: each use's share of the struck vehicles within each road class, day type and hour, times the Department's miles for that stratum (its class's VMT spread over the week by its count stations), summed over the 288 strata.
Not semantics: every field, code, unit and definition read correctly (both report editions, UTC and local clocks, legacy routes, operators' names, the rule's crashes, causes and private drivers) leaves the pooled struck-vehicle share, the field's standard exposure measure, on airport shuttles and paratransit; no definition says where the struck vehicles are a draw from the traffic.
Not rule application: the rule files the rate per mile and nothing about estimating miles, and no filed procedure, table or formula gives a use's miles, so executing every stated rule leaves the miles open and the field's default files the wrong pair.
Analyst test: a strong analyst who cleans every record and applies every rule, then takes the pooled not-at-fault share as each use's share of the miles (induced exposure), files two uses, airport shuttles and paratransit; executed on the generator's clean truth in `checks.py` (ANALYST), where the decisive step alone files the golden four.
Estimand: rule 3.1 and 3.2 of cfsp_coverage_rule_2026.pdf: the crashes caused (4.1 crashes between two or more moving vehicles, 4.2 each driver recorded at fault, fled drivers no use) by a use's drivers (4.3) in 2021 to 2025, divided by the vehicle miles its vehicles drove on public roads in Stonereach County (4.5) in the same years, against the same rate for private drivers (4.4), covered at 1.5 or more.
No decoy: nothing is planted to be caught: no memo, earlier estimate, filed share, stakeholder figure or draft states a use's miles or rate; the vehicle counts are the operators' and platforms' own returns, the VMT and count hours are the Department's, and no shipped file ranks the uses.
No certified replay: no certified or published figure carries a use's miles, share of traffic or rate, so the method cannot be recovered by reproducing one; the Department's VMT and station volumes are inputs every route reads the same way.
Lesson: a sample drawn by events weights every unit by the event intensity where it was drawn, so to turn its shares into a population's exposure take the shares where the intensity is constant and weight each by the measured exposure there; this lane's other builds teach inclusion weights (task264) and an older rule's flag (task267), none an event-weighted exposure sample.
