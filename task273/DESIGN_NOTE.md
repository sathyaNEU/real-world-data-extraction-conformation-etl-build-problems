# task273 design note

Architecture 2, drawn 2026-10-22 (wave 5, Descriptive lane, sathay3012), re-rooted after architecture 1 (the closed-loop load test, `## Architecture 1 record`) was spent at stage 4. Seed DA23's world stays set aside and the slot draws a new world in the same objective. The Halloway Valley Regional Transportation Commission licenses the region's commercial passenger and delivery operators, and from 1 July 2027 its Commercial Fleet Safety Programme (telematics with driver coaching, paid by the operators) covers each commercial vehicle use whose drivers caused crashes at a rate per mile driven of at least 1.5 times that of private drivers over the region's police-reported crashes of 2021 to 2025. The commission votes on the covered uses on 9 December 2026. No record counts the miles each use drives: the region's crash reports record every vehicle and which driver the officer found at fault, the state's traffic report gives the miles driven on each class of road and how they spread over the hours of the week, and the registrations and permits count vehicles, not miles.
