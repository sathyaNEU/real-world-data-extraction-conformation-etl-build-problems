How many of the road sections we counted in 2025, if any, carried more than three million vehicles in the year, and which are they? That list fixes the scope of the noise mapping contract. In noise_round_sections_2025.csv give every section we counted in 2025 with its count point, its road, the junctions at each end, its length and its 2025 traffic, marking those over three million, and in section_traffic_2025.png chart each section's traffic against the three million line. For the noise model's other inputs, traffic_network_2025.xlsx should hold each permanent counter's average daily heavy goods vehicles in 2025 and the vehicles the detectors recorded in 2025 on each approach to the Western Avenue / Fenwick Road and Ring Road / Tanyard Lane signals. Section traffic is in millions of vehicles to one decimal, heavy goods vehicles are to the nearest 10 a day and approach totals are in thousands of vehicles to the nearest thousand.
