Which of our eight acute trusts should the region's external review of deaths after delayed escalation to critical care sit in? The board funds one twelve-month engagement and the reviewers start on 1 April 2027. It is placed once. Norman Scott, who chairs the board, would send them wherever the most patients die waiting. I lead quality surveillance for the regional health board and the recommendation is mine. Name the one trust the review should sit in.

That trust opens external_review_placement_2027-28.docx, followed by the number of deaths a year of review there could confirm, the trust that comes closest and the gap between the two in deaths.

Behind it, review_placement_workings.xlsx carries the record: one row per trust across the whole of the network's record, giving the patients whose wait put them inside the review's remit, the deaths among them that the remit covers, and the deaths the reviewers could have confirmed, with a total for each column.

The board meets the call first in review_placement_by_trust.png. It wants one bar per trust for the deaths inside the remit over the period the placement rests on, the confirmable part shaded within each bar, trusts ordered by that part, the gap to the runner-up marked, and a title naming the trust.

Every figure counts people, so whole numbers throughout.
