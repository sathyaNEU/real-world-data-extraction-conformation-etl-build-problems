## Default path

1. Read the call log and the account register; take the accounts that reached 30 days past due in 2025 and 2026 at each centre, the ones its team called and the ones it did not.
2. Mark each account's outcome from the delinquency ledger: rolled to 90 days past due within 120 days of listing, or not.
3. Compare the roll rates of called and uncalled accounts by centre, adjusted for the behaviour score.
4. Take each centre's difference as the rolls its calls prevent per 100 accounts called, and fund the centre with the largest, which clears the funding rule.

Ends on: fund one centre at about 4 rolls prevented per 100 accounts called (prototype figure; the generator fixes it at design).
Golden: no centre; the best prevents about 2 per 100 on the same-day comparison (prototype figure).

## Stump sentence

The default path funds the centre whose called accounts roll least, at about 4 rolls prevented per 100, because step 3 compares called with uncalled accounts drawn from the register's arrears flag and pooled over the year, when the uncalled include accounts with no valid phone that the team could never call and accounts listed on the big billing cycles' days, when the team never reaches the bottom of the list, both of which roll more for reasons a call does not change; on the same-day comparison of the dialler list's over-capacity days the best centre prevents about 2 per 100, and no centre gets the team.
