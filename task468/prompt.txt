Our annual return for the regulator's March audit has to list every customer over a limit. Put the review in trade_waste_cod_review_2026.pdf: the customers that exceeded their COD limit and those that did not, each customer's 2026 COD mass against its limit, and a chart of each customer's mass as a share of its limit with those over it marked. In trade_waste_figures_2026.xlsx give each customer's volume discharged in each quarter of 2026 and the mass of suspended solids it discharged in the year.
