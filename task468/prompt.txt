Did any of our trade waste customers discharge more chemical oxygen demand in 2026 than their agreements allow, and if so which? Our annual return for the regulator's March audit has to list every customer over a limit. Put the review in trade_waste_cod_review_2026.pdf: the customers that exceeded their COD limit and those that did not, each customer's 2026 COD mass against its limit, and a chart of each customer's mass as a share of its limit with those over it marked. The return also reports the tankered liquid waste the treatment plant received and each customer's suspended solids, so in trade_waste_figures_2026.xlsx give the volume of each kind of tankered waste received in each quarter of 2026 and the mass of suspended solids each customer discharged in the year. Volumes are in whole kilolitres and suspended solids in tonnes to one decimal.
