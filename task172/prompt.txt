Should the TALON-2 trial stop for futility at its interim analysis, go on to its final analysis as planned, or have that analysis extended under Schedule 2 to the Ashgrove funding agreement? The DMC charter's conditional power decides it, and talon2_interim_recommendation.pdf should give the committee the call and that conditional power, while talon2_interim_tables.csv gives for each arm at the data cut the patients with a serious adverse event, the serious adverse events in all and in infections and infestations, hepatobiliary, gastrointestinal, and blood and lymphatic system disorders, the patients whose ALT or AST rose above five times or whose bilirubin rose above three times the upper limit of normal after the day of their first dose, the patients who received study treatment, and the ravolimab or placebo doses given. The conditional power is to the nearest 10 per cent and every count is a whole number.
