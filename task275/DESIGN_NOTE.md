# task275 design note

Architecture 1, drawn 2026-10-23 from seed DA25's decision shape only (which of eight units join a programme, judged on a share against a fixed line); the seed's road-policing world was screened and redrawn (`## Tried and rejected`). The Wrayburn Coast Fish Producers' Organisation manages the quota of its members' vessels, landing at Kestlemouth, Penmarrow, Brindlesea, Cauldwick and Gullsby, in eight fleet segments by gear and length. Since 2019 a discard ban has required quota species to be landed, and quota fish below the minimum conservation reference size (BMS) to be landed apart and sold for reduction; non-quota species and the exempted Nephrops may still be returned. The PO's 2027 selectivity programme (square-mesh panels, separator grids and a camera on each vessel) takes every segment that discarded more than 10 per cent of its 2025 catch by weight, and the board fixes the segments in December 2026. The only measurement of discards is the at-sea observer scheme, which puts an observer on a random draw of about one notified trip in twelve.

## Three rules

Task type: Survey Research & Official Statistics, `nonresponse-calibration-measurement-error-adjustment`: a probability sample of fishing trips whose measurement changes what it measures (the observer effect), the part the measurement cannot change estimated from the sample and the part it changes taken from the census of landing declarations; it serves Descriptive & Distribution Analysis (each segment's discard share set against a line).
Domain test: the deciding quantity is each segment's discards on the trips no observer saw; the field raises observer-sampled discards to the fleet's effort by stratum and tests the observer and deployment effects by comparing observed with unobserved trips' landings and grounds, and where the observed trips land what the unobserved trips throw back it raises the catch process an observer does not change (the unwanted catch a haul brings aboard per hour on a ground) and takes the catch's fate from the census of what was declared.
Swap: with hospital wards for segments, patient falls for discards and audited shifts for observed trips the sentence still reads, but a ward has no catch that is the same whether or not an auditor is present and no census of what was kept to subtract from it, so the identification (an invariant catch per hour on a ground and a mass balance of catch against landings) has nothing to stand on; it only works where every haul brings aboard a catch whose fate the skipper chooses.
Layer 1: the deployment log typed from the skippers' notifications (port letters and numbers with and without the space or with a hyphen, notified departures in UK time up to 90 minutes off the logbook's UTC departures, draws cancelled before sailing); landing declarations in live weight by species, presentation and size status; the logbook's discards field filled only above 50 kg; observer weights raised to the haul in UK time, with the hauls an observer could not attend (52 trips) left out of the records; sales notes of eight buyers dated at sale, after landing; VMS reports in UTC from terminals swapped between vessels; each produced by the system named, none placed to be caught.
Layer 2: `estimand_identification`, own:observer effect in discard raising: the observed trips are a random draw of trips but not of behaviour, so their discards measure what a watched vessel returns; raising the observers' discards (rung R2) against raising the catch process (unwanted catch per hour by ground) and taking the fate from the declarations flips the call.
Decisive step: estimate the unobserved trips' discards as the unwanted catch (BMS and lawful discards) per hour fished that the same segment's observed trips show on the same ground, times the unobserved trips' hours there, less the BMS the unobserved trips declared.
Not semantics: every field, code, unit and definition read correctly (the fractions RET, BMS and DIS, Above MCRS and BMS on the declarations, live weight, the terms' catch and discards) leaves the observer-raised discards on NEP alone; no definition says a trip lands its BMS only when observed, and the observers' DIS is exactly what the observed trips returned.
Not rule application: the terms file the outcome (the catch returned to the sea on every 2025 trip) and nothing on estimating it for the trips no one watched; the enumerated family (the logbook's declared discards, the observed trips' share, the field's raising by hours or by landings, the observed unwanted share, pooled grounds, the unobserved BMS counted whole, and the golden's 20 constructions) files six distinct designated sets; the standing family (the constructions that take the unobserved trips' declared BMS as what they kept, stratified by ground) files one set with every segment at the same whole per cent across all 20 (spread at most 0.68 points, TR1 12.62 to 13.30); what rules out the easy route is one comparison no document invites, BMS declared per hour by observed against unobserved trips on the same ground (TR1 25.28 against 2.10 kg), reached only by joining the deployment log to the landing declarations.
Analyst test: a strong analyst who cleans every record and applies every rule, then raises the observers' discards by the hours fished on each ground (the field's discard raising), files one segment, NEP; executed on the generator's clean truth in `checks.py` (R3a ANALYST), where the decisive step alone files the golden four (R3b DECISIVE).
Estimand: terms 2.1 and 3.1 to 3.4 of selectivity_programme_terms_2027.pdf: each segment's catch returned to the sea (3.2) over its catch, landed and returned (3.1), on every fishing trip its member vessels began in 2025 (3.3), in live weight (3.4), designated above 10 per cent (2.1).
No decoy: nothing is planted to be caught: no memo, earlier estimate, scheme report, stakeholder figure or draft states a segment's discards; the observer records, the deployment log and the declarations are the scheme's and the logbooks' own, and no shipped file ranks the segments or computes a discard share.
No certified replay: no certified or published figure carries a segment's discards, catch or share, so the method cannot be recovered by reproducing one; the quota uptake report sums landings, which every route reads the same way.
Lesson: when a sample's measurement changes what it measures, raise the part the measurement cannot change (the catch a haul brings aboard per hour on a ground) and take the part it changes (what was kept) from the census of every unit; no build on file teaches an observer effect.

## Literal reader

Executed word for word, the terms file each segment's catch returned to the sea over its catch, landed and returned, on every trip its vessels began in 2025, in live weight, designated above 10 per cent. The declarations give every trip's landings exactly; only the observed trips have their returns weighed, and the literal estimand still asks for the returns of the other 11,144. The observers' DIS measures what a watched trip returned, which the records refute as a measure of the others: the observed trips declared BMS at 25.28 kg an hour on TR1's grounds and the unobserved 2.10, at 4.66 against 0.06 on BT's, at 6.82 against 0.05 on SN's (EVIDENCE in `checks.py`), while the catch a haul brings aboard per hour on a ground does not depend on who is watching. Raising the observed trips' unwanted catch per hour by ground to the unobserved hours, less the BMS the unobserved trips declared, is the estimate the records support, and every member of its family (any raising variable, ground or ground by half-year, either source of the observed trips' kept catch) names TR1, TR2, NEP and BT at the same whole per cent; the generator's world names them too.
