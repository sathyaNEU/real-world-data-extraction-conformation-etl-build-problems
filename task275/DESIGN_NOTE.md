# task275 design note

Architecture 1, drawn 2026-10-23 from seed DA25's decision shape only (which of eight units join a programme, judged on a share against a fixed line); the seed's road-policing world was screened and redrawn (`## Tried and rejected`). The Wrayburn Coast Fish Producers' Organisation manages the quota of its members' vessels, landing at Kestlemouth, Penmarrow, Brindlesea, Cauldwick and Gullsby, in eight fleet segments by gear and length. Since 2019 a discard ban has required quota species to be landed, and quota fish below the minimum conservation reference size (BMS) to be landed apart and sold for reduction; non-quota species and the exempted Nephrops may still be returned. The PO's 2027 selectivity programme (square-mesh panels, separator grids and a camera on each vessel) takes every segment that discarded more than 10 per cent of its 2025 catch by weight, and the board fixes the segments in December 2026. The only measurement of discards is the at-sea observer scheme, which puts an observer on a random draw of about one notified trip in twelve.
