# task271 design note

Architecture 3, re-rooted 2026-10-20 (wave 4, Descriptive lane, sathay3012) after architecture 2's stage-4 readers both landed the call; the slot's last architecture. A nonprofit integrated health plan in Colorado (its name drawn and screened against the H21 register at stage 3) launches a Family Outreach Programme through its Inherited Cancer Risk Service on 1 April 2027: for each recurrent pathogenic variant the programme takes on, the service writes again to the untested first-degree relatives of its probands and offers the women who carry the variant annual breast MRI from 30. The service's criteria take a variant on when the women in its families who carry it face at least a 30 per cent chance of breast cancer by their 70th birthday. The plan's Clinical Programmes Committee adopts the variants in February 2027, on the service's pedigree records and the laboratory results of the families referred to it from 2016 to September 2026.

## Default path

1. Read the programme's criteria: a variant qualifies when the women who carry it face at least a 30 per cent chance of breast cancer by their 70th birthday, measured on the first-degree female relatives of the service's probands who carry the family's variant.
2. Conform the service's files: the pedigree export (one row a person, relationships coded to the proband, birth years or ages, vital status, cancer histories with ages at diagnosis) and the laboratory results (linked to the pedigree by person key, the variant written in legacy protein names before 2020 and HGVS after).
3. Leave the probands out: their cancer brought each family to the service.
4. For each variant, run Kaplan-Meier on the female first-degree relatives whose laboratory results show the family's variant: age at a first breast cancer, censored at the age at last contact or at death; read the risk by 70.
5. Name the variants at or above 30 per cent.

Ends on: the two PALB2 truncating variants and the ATM missense variant, together with the PALB2 missense, BARD1 and RAD51D variants reclassified in 2024 (six variants; the world is fixed at stage 3).
Golden: the two PALB2 truncating variants and the ATM missense variant (three).

A competent analyst who conforms the pedigree export and the laboratory results, sets the probands aside and runs Kaplan-Meier on the relatives tested and found to carry each family's variant names six variants for the 2027 Family Outreach Programme, among them the PALB2 missense, BARD1 and RAD51D variants reclassified in 2024, because their relatives were tested mostly for a breast cancer of their own while few of their unaffected carriers have taken up the letters yet; only every first-degree relative's history, each relative a carrier with probability one half unless a test settles it (the kin-cohort or the Mendelian likelihood), puts those three under 30 per cent and names the two PALB2 truncating variants and the ATM missense variant.
