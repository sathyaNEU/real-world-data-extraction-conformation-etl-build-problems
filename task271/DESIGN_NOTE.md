# task271 design note

Architecture 2, re-rooted 2026-10-20 (wave 4, Descriptive lane, sathay3012) after architecture 1's stage-4 readers both landed the call. Pellmont Community Health Plan, a nonprofit Medicaid managed care plan in Washington, launches a Community Engagement Support track on 1 January 2027, when the state's community engagement requirement for Apple Health expansion adults begins: navigator teams in four of its ten Apple Health regions, the four where the smallest share of its expansion members aged 19 to 64 already work 80 hours a month. Its board adopts the four regions in December 2026, on the plan's spring 2026 Member Work and Income Survey, telephoned to a random sample of each region's expansion members by the plan's own member services centre and a survey vendor, Larchfield Survey Research.
