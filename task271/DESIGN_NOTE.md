Fenmarsh Investment Management runs a central dealing desk in London that executes its funds' UK equity orders through two brokers' algorithms on eight execution venues: the Wexcombe Stock Exchange's order book, the lit and midpoint books of Arden MTF and Corvel MTF, Lowther MTF's midpoint book, and two systematic internalisers, Bramwell Securities and Kestrel Markets. Its wealth arm, Fenmarsh Wealth Limited, runs a platform for retail clients (ISAs, SIPPs and general accounts) whose dealing in UK shares has been executed by a third-party execution-only broker; from Monday 4 January 2027 the central desk deals for the platform. The platform nets its clients' orders at three dealing points a day into one order per stock and side, each small and sent whole to one venue by the desk's router. The order execution policy keeps a venue off a client flow's venue list when the fills that flow's orders would receive there meet an average adverse move above 1.5 basis points in the 60 seconds after the fill, and the wealth board approves the platform's venue list on 9 December 2026.

## Three rules

Task type: market-microstructure-execution-cost (Quantitative Finance), which serves Descriptive & Distribution Analysis in the taxonomy.
Domain test: the quantity that decides the call is each venue's adverse selection, the average move of the mid against the order in the 60 seconds after a fill (its markout), which execution analysis measures fill by fill and separates from the trader's own price impact by reading the move against the trader's following volume; the platform's orders, sent whole, carry the counterparty's part alone.
Swap: "which of eight hospital wards should single admissions avoid, by how far a patient's early warning score rises in the hour after admission, read from a year of admissions that came in batches" has no order whose later child fills push the measured price the trader's way, so the move that decides the call (netting the desk's own following trades out of each fill's markout) has nothing to act on.
Layer 1: two brokers' fill files in their own layouts (pence and UTC against pounds and London local time across the 29 March and 25 October clock changes), venue codes against venue names with Lowther's code changed mid-year, busted and corrected executions as reversal rows, drop copies of one broker's midpoint fills in the other broker's file, the cost provider's references against the brokers' execution IDs, closing auction fills with no continuous mid after them, and a share consolidation in one stock in May; each is produced by its system, nothing is placed to be caught, and on the call's path each mishandled keeps the call.
Layer 2: estimand_identification: the adverse move the platform's lone orders will meet is the counterparty's part of each fill's markout, and the 2026 fills' markouts carry beside it the desk's own following trades in the same order, which the platform's orders will not have.
Decisive step: reading each venue's move on the fills the desk did not follow with its own trades in the stock within the minute, or net of the desk's following volume, not over every 2026 fill.
Not semantics: every field read correctly (both brokers' units, times and venue codes, the busts and drop copies, the cost provider's mids, the auction flag) and the policy's test applied as written still leave the raw venue averages naming two venues.
Not rule application: the policy fixes the horizon, the reference mid, the sign and the 1.5 basis point line and says nothing of the desk's own trades; the enumerated family (every fill, the median, the 1 and 5 second markouts, the 2026 single orders, parent fixed effects, the fills with no desk trade in the next minute, the regression on following volume, the fills outside each order's opening burst, each order's last fill) spans two designated sets, and the standing family (the four constructions that leave the desk's following trades out of the move) files one set in 20 of 20 prototype worlds (each order's last fill 18 of 20); what rules the rest out is the record itself, the move falling about 6 basis points for each 1 per cent of daily volume the desk trades in the stock in the following minute, not a filed clause.
Analyst test: a strong analyst who joins both brokers' fills to the cost provider's mids, nets the busted and duplicated executions and averages each venue's 60-second adverse move over its 2026 continuous-trading fills names Corvel lit and Kestrel SI, because the raw averages of Arden dark and Lowther dark, the books the algorithms ping in each order's opening seconds, sit under 1.5 basis points; those fills were followed within the minute by the same order's next child fills, which pushed the mid the desk's way, and the platform's orders carry no such following trades, so read on fills with no desk trade in the stock in the next minute, or net of the desk's following volume, the two midpoint books fail the test too.
Estimand: the policy's test ("a venue is kept off a client flow's venue list when the fills that flow's orders would receive there meet an average adverse move above 1.5 basis points in the 60 seconds after the fill"), its definition of the move (the mid 60 seconds after the fill against the mid at the fill, in basis points of the mid at the fill, positive when the price has moved against the order, continuous trading only), and the dealing plan's platform orders (one order per stock and side at each dealing point, sent whole to one venue).
No decoy: no memo, report, draft or stakeholder figure states or ranks any venue's adverse move; the desk's 2025 venue report gives volume shares only (a distractor named in metadata.json), and the router configuration and the dealing plan state operational facts.
No certified replay: no published or certified venue figure ships to be reproduced; the policy's test is run on the desk's own 2026 fills and blesses no construction.
Lesson: a markout measured inside a sliced order carries the order's own later trades, so a venue the algorithms use at the start of orders looks benign on the raw average; carried to orders that go whole to one venue, a venue's move has to be read net of the trader's own following trades. No build on file separates a measured move from the measuring actor's own continuing activity.
