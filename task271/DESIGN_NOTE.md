## Three rules

Task type: nonresponse-calibration-measurement-error-adjustment (Survey Research & Official Statistics), which serves Descriptive & Distribution Analysis in the taxonomy.
Domain test: the quantity that decides the call is each region's share of members in work estimated over the whole sample when whether a member answers depends on that very outcome; survey methodology estimates it from the fieldwork's own paradata, here a callback (level-of-effort) latent class model of each class's chance that a call in each calling window reaches the member, the field's answer to nonresponse that is not missing at random.
Swap: "which four warehouses hold the smallest share of pallets over a tonne, read from the scale's log": a pallet is weighed whether or not it is heavy, no record of failed weighings carries information about the pallets never weighed, and the call histories that decide the call stop making sense.
Layer 1: two call centres' records in two systems (the vendor's dialer export by case number with ISO times and their UTC offset; the plan's CRM workbook by member ID with dates, AM and PM times and its other outreach mixed in); the vendor's February pilot and April test calls in the dialer and six test interviews in the interview file; cases passed from the plan's centre to the vendor worked in their own queue; declines recorded in words in one system and as codes in the other; the key question's answers in the interview file under their own status. Each is produced by the systems, nothing is placed to be caught, and each mishandled keeps the call (R2, asserted).
Layer 2: estimation_fit: nonignorable nonresponse identified from the contact histories, a latent class callback model against weighting-class adjustment of the respondents.
Decisive step: model each work class's chance that a call reaches the member by calling window from every sampled member's calls, reached or not, and estimate each region's share over all its sampled members from that model, rather than weighting the respondents, which assumes the members never reached are like the respondents of their cell.
Not semantics: every field read correctly (each call's time and window, the dispositions in both systems, the key answers, the March hours, the region) and the criteria's definitions applied still leave the share among the members never reached to be estimated: no field records it, and the field's default adjustment on every field read correctly files Pierce, North Sound, King and Spokane.
Not rule application: the criteria fix the population, the outcome, the month, the unit and the comparison, and the methods report describes the fieldwork; no filed rule or procedure says how the members never reached are to be estimated, and executing every stated rule with the field's default adjustment files Pierce, North Sound, King and Spokane.
Analyst test: a strong analyst who conforms both centres' calls, reads every disposition, the key answers and the criteria correctly and weights the respondents to each region's age band and sex (or carries the members reached late to the members never reached) files Pierce, North Sound, King and Spokane; only the callback model of every sampled member's calls names Thurston-Mason in North Sound's place.
Estimand: the track's criteria (a filed PDF), 2.1 "the four of the plan's ten Apple Health regions where the smallest share of the plan's expansion members worked for pay 80 hours or more in March 2026", 2.2 "for the plan's expansion members aged 19 to 64 enrolled in the region on 1 March 2026, every one of them: the members the 2026 Member Work and Income Survey reached and the members it did not. It is estimated from the survey, which drew 1,500 of those members in each region", 2.3 the outcome (all jobs together, 80 or more), 2.4 compared unrounded and reported to the whole percent.
No decoy: no memo, briefing, draft or stakeholder figure argues for a region; the methods report gives each region's fieldwork counts (sampled, first called by the plan's centre, passed to the vendor, completed, declined, not reached) and no share in work; the CAHPS results and the pilot memo answer other questions.
No certified replay: no published or certified share is shipped to be reproduced, and the method is not recovered from controls.
Lesson: a survey whose response depends on the outcome is not mended by weighting the respondents to who they are; the record of the calls that failed carries the evidence of who the missing are, and a callback model reads it (members in work are out when the daytime calls come). task233 carried a departure from missing at random as an odds ratio measured on a follow-up; no build on file models nonresponse from contact histories.
