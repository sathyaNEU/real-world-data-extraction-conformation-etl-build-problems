# task271 design note

Architecture 1, drawn 2026-10-19 (wave 4, Descriptive lane, sathay3012). A consumer-governed nonprofit health plan in Wisconsin will name three hospitals as its 2027 Pancreatic Surgery Centres: members who have a pancreatic resection there pay no cost-sharing and get travel support. Its board votes the three at its November 2026 meeting, on each eligible hospital's deaths within 90 days of the operation in 2023 to 2025 against the deaths the same patients' condition before surgery would have produced at the eight eligible hospitals together. The evidence is the state's hospital data consortium's linked discharge files and death index.

Decisive generator G14 (pattern E, a conditioned yield: the pooled risk model's expectation does not fit the referral centres' patients until it is conditioned on the earlier operation), then G16 (the risk model the data entitle). `composition_mix` describes the same move from the pool's side and is barred in the window by task468, whose card was not flagged `architecture_spent` when that build retired on 2026-10-19; the attribution label names what the decisive step does to the call, so it is the label kept, not a passing one.

10. Screens, run as code before any pack is cut (scratch `t271/sim2.py`, `sim3.py`, `scan1.py`), then asserted on the built world (`generator/checks.py`):

    - 1.2a: the filed quantity is unobserved: the deaths the same patients would have had at the eight hospitals together is a counterfactual, estimated by a risk model, and a model on the index admission's record and one that adds the earlier operation are two estimators of it; the second is the one the patients' condition entitles because the earlier operation is pre-operative, strong and unevenly spread.
    - Seed scan (`scan1.py`, eight hospitals, 1,710 operations, 120 to 170 deaths): 14 of 300 worlds keep the golden trio identical across four model forms (logit, probit, with interactions and an age term, and an empirical Bayes ratio), differ from the raw ranking, from the index-only model and from the three largest hospitals, with at least 0.06 between the third and fourth ratios and 0.04 between the referral centre's index-only ratio and the third; the generator's own scan repeats this on the built records.
    - To be measured at stage 2 before the ladder is written: the common-exposure subset (first operations only, the referral centres' ratio on patients with no earlier operation), the broad definitions of an earlier operation (any upper abdominal operation, gallbladder removal, endoscopic stenting of the bile duct), the volume heuristic and a shrinkage route, each against the golden trio.

## Tried and rejected

- The seed DA21's design (a nonprofit health plan's 2027 intensive care-management track by rate cell), set aside at the draw on 2026-10-19: its own stump split one Medicaid line into two rate cells by aid code, which is data semantics as the decisive step; the cell's top-5% share of 2026 spending completed for claims not yet received moved by 0.3 to 1.0 points in simulation, under any threshold's margin; the concentration measure against the board's stated threshold is a stated score deciding the call. The world (a nonprofit health plan, its board, its 2027 programme) is kept.
- Screens run at the draw on 2026-10-19 before this pairing, each set aside with its record: each hospital's mortality shrunk toward the mortality of hospitals of its volume rather than the overall mean (Dimick and Staiger's reliability adjustment; the overall-mean shrinkage of the public measures is a defensible fork the judge would hold open, and a reader who tabulates mortality by volume meets the gradient first); a landfill's methane generated in 2026 by first-order decay with the decay rate read off the flared sites' metered decline (the decay model is the field's reflex and the metered sites invite its calibration; set aside once before, task253's note); a health plan's incurred cost per member month with claims adjudication faster after a platform migration (completion is the spent family, and which date to develop on reads as time semantics); a patient-experience score adjusted for a web-and-phone mode change through a randomised parallel run (the experiment names the correction it exists for); hospitals' risk-standardised mortality with coding intensity carried in the index admission's diagnoses (set aside at task270's third draw: present-on-admission adjustment is a defensible fork); a new user's twelve-month persistence on a drug class with prevalent users carried in by plan switching (the new-user washout is a population definition, a WHAT); the dissimilarity index's small-unit bias (the realised index is records-fixed); a period fertility rate depressed by postponement (the tempo-adjusted and cohort routes disagree).
