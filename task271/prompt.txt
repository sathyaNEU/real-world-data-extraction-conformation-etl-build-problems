Where does each of our lakes that take catchables fall against the high-use line for 2027, on the angler-hours it drew over the 2026 open-water season? The tiers go in stocking_tiers_2027.xlsx, a row for every such lake, with a sheet of how many catchable rainbow trout went into each lake alive in 2026 and a sheet giving, for each lake with a temperature logger at 1 m, on how many days of July and August its water there averaged above 20 °C over the Pacific-time day. stocking_board_note_2027.docx is a one-page note on the tiers with a bar chart of every lake's angler-hours per hectare against the high-use line. Release counts are to the nearest 100 fish and warm days are whole days.
