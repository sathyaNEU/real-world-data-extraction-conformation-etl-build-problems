## 1. Situation

A city's annual PM2.5 fell 25% in the three years of its clean-air plan, from 80 to 60 µg/m³. The plan financed five measures with a
results-based loan: residential coal-to-gas heating, closures of the city's steel and cement plants, a diesel truck scrappage scheme,
construction dust control, and a contribution to a joint regional fund shared with the neighbouring province. The lender's next tranche
scales up one measure, the one with the largest verified share of the decline once weather is taken out. The city's progress report credits
the heating conversion.

## 2. Gate G: why this is legal

* **Litmus.** Every reported number is correct: the monitors' hourly readings as their instruments recorded them, the gravimetric samples,
  the speciation filters, the meteorology, the boundary monitors and the ledger. The progress report's inventory figures are right, and the
  city's own closures did cut its industrial emissions. No one's reading of their own figures is overturned. The question is whose
  emissions the fall came from.
* **Flags.** surface_read_dependency: no · stumping_family: analytical_non_defect · sole_data_defect: no.
* **Deletion test.** Delete the progress report and every voice. The verifier's construction, homogenised and reproducing all 14 settled
  tranches, still credits the city's closures first, and nothing in it reads the boundary monitors.
* **Instrument repair.** Suspect: six sites switched to beta-attenuation monitors in the second year and read high in humid winter months,
  so their series change meaning mid-window. Repair: one instrument throughout, as their gravimetric samplers give. Rung 0 then still names
  the heating conversion (9.0), and rungs 1 and 2 both name the city's closures (4.6); none names the regional fund. The speciation filters
  record chemistry, which says what kind of source a particle came from, not whose; they are daily and complete, as are the boundary
  monitors and the meteorology. Separating the neighbour's closures from the city's is a sector-conditioned difference against a
  background no row records, so the decisive rung is still needed.
* **Lens swap.** The naive population of sources is the city's own emitters, the one every settled tranche assumed. The answer's includes
  the neighbouring province, upwind in winter, a different population of emissions.

## 3. The driving force

A strong solver does not take the progress report's inventory shares. It deweathers each monitor with the verifier's random-forest
normalisation, because the second and third winters were windier, and apportions the deweathered decline by the source factors in the
speciation filters. Truck scrappage leads. Then it reads the network's QA log: six sites changed instruments in the second year, and their
co-located gravimetric samplers show the new instruments reading 9% high in humid winter months. Homogenised, the industrial district's
decline deepens and the city's closures lead, 4.6 against 3.8. The settled ledger reproduces this construction in all 14 past tranches,
and the industrial district's fall looks like the ledger's 2019 cement tranche. But in every settled tranche the neighbouring province's
output sat at its national quota, so the regional background was flat and a city's factor decline was all its own. The quota went two
years ago, and the joint fund paid for the province's steel closures, north-west of the city. At the boundary monitors, in hours the wind
comes from the province, the deweathered background fell 9 µg/m³. Carried into the city, it is 6.0 of the 16.0 decline, and most of it is
the metals and sulfate the factors had booked to the city's closures, which keep 1.4.

* **Position table.** The joint fund ranks 5th on rungs 0, 1 and 2 (1.5 each, the inventory's figure) and leads only rung 3. Rung margins are
  2.00, 1.24, 1.21 and 1.67.
* **Discriminator dominance.** The city's closures carry a 3.1 µg/m³ lead over the joint fund into rung 3 (4.6 against 1.5). The regional
  construction moves 3.2 off the closures and 4.5 onto the fund, a 7.7 swing, 2.48× the carried lead. The floor is 1.2×, so the edge has
  2.07× headroom.
* **Partial correction priced (L3).** A solver who measures the regional background without homogenising the two boundary sites finds only
  2.5 and names scrappage (4.15, 1.32× heating). One who takes the network's designated background site, in the south-east, finds 0.8 and
  names the city's closures (5.1, 1.33×). One who subtracts the regional fall as nobody's names scrappage (3.6, 1.44×). One who uses the
  raw, undeweathered background finds 0.5 and names the closures (5.3, 1.38×). No half names the joint fund.
* **Grid.** Weather (raw, deweathered) × instruments (as read, homogenised) × background (none, south-east site, north-west sector) gives
  12 cells. Raw cells name the heating conversion, deweathered as-read cells name scrappage, and homogenised cells without the north-west
  sector name the city's closures. Only the full construction names the joint fund.

## 5. Why the decisive rung survives the opponent

1. **Written nowhere.** The loan agreement credits each measure with its verified share of the deweathered decline. The joint fund's
   agreement lists the closures it paid for. No document says the province's closures reach the city's monitors or that the background
   moved.
2. **Corpus blind for a computable reason.** *In every settled tranche the deweathered background at the boundary monitors was flat, because
   the neighbouring province's industrial output was held at its national quota throughout those six years, so a city's factor decline was
   all its own.* The ledger reproduces rung 2's construction in 14 of 14 tranches.
3. **No arithmetic symptom.** The homogenised series reconcile to the gravimetric samples, the factors reconstruct the filters' mass, and
   the decline is partitioned exactly on rungs 2 and 3.
4. **Not a row predicate.** The regional share needs the boundary monitors' hours selected by wind sector, deweathered and homogenised, then
   carried into each city monitor and set against its factor changes.
5. **The enumeration is arithmetic.** No filter or reading is labelled regional. The 6.0 is computed from the sector difference.
6. **No cutover date.** The province closed its plants one by one over two years, and the background fell with them.
7. **Survives deletion.** No wrong number exists to delete.

## 6. The calibration corpus

* **Form.** The lender's ledger of 14 settled results-based tranches in this city and two others over six years. Each holds the claimed
  reduction, the verifier's deweathered reduction and the amount disbursed.
* **What it certifies.** Rung 2's construction: homogenised, deweathered factor changes reproduce every verified reduction to 0.1 µg/m³, and
  the settled amounts follow from them. The inventory reproduces 3 of 14.
* **What it is blind to.** A moving regional background (property 2).
* **Twin pair.** City monitors NW-3 and SE-7 are identical on deweathered, homogenised decline (16.0), factor profile and land use. In
  north-westerly hours NW-3's decline runs 9.0 deeper than in other hours and SE-7's 2.0, so their local declines are 7.0 and 14.0 (2.0×).
  Only the sector-conditioned background separates them.
* **Resemblance points at the decoy.** The industrial district's fall most resembles the ledger's 2019 cement-closure tranche, verified in
  full at its factor change.

## 7. Pins, voices and the licensed wrong basis

* **Filed pins.** The loan agreement: "The scale-up tranche funds the measure with the largest verified share of the weather-normalised
  decline." The verification protocol fixes the random-forest normalisation and the source factors. The network's QA manual: "Where an
  instrument changes, its co-located gravimetric sampler is the reference."
* **Empirical pins.** Homogenisation factors come from the gravimetric samplers. The deweathering model comes from the protocol.
* **Voices.** Mayor's office: "Taking coal out of a million homes is what cleaned our air." Environment bureau director: "Our plant closures
  did the heavy lifting." Transport commissioner: "The trucks were always the problem." Lender's task manager: "The ledger has never been
  wrong about which measure delivered."
* **Licensed wrong basis.** The loan agreement records that the city reports progress on its emission inventory and that the lender's board
  sees the city's annual report on that basis.

## 8. Determinism by construction

* **Sectors.** North-westerly hours are winds from 270° to 360° over 2 m/s at the boundary mast. Any sector from 280°–350° to 260°–10° gives
  the fund between 5.6 and 6.4.
* **Background reach.** The boundary monitors sit 40 km beyond the city and its plume never reaches them, so all-hours and sector readings
  agree within 0.2 when the wind is from the province.
* **Homogenisation.** Each site's factor comes from at least 120 paired gravimetric days per season, and no factor moves a leader.
* **Normalisation.** The protocol fixes the forest, the seed and the resampling window. Any seed moves no credit by more than 0.05.
* **Maturity.** Every filter of the third year was analysed and validated before the extract.

## 9. Prompt sketch and deliverables

> The lender scales up one of our five clean-air measures with the next tranche, and the city says the heating conversion earned it. Tell me
> which measure gets it, in a sentence the lender can minute, with the µg/m³ of the decline you credit to each measure, to one decimal. Send `measure_credits.xlsx`, a chart `decline_bridge.png`, and a one-page `tranche_note.pdf`.

* `measure_credits.xlsx` — the five measures on every construction, the ozone sheet (ask A), the scrappage sheet (ask B) and the ledger
  back-test (ask C).
* `decline_bridge.png` — a bridge from 80 to 60 µg/m³ with bars for weather, instruments and each measure, an inset of the boundary
  background by wind sector across the three years, and a title naming the measure.
* `tranche_note.pdf` — the measure and why each other measure delivered less.

## 10. The ask layer

* **Ask A (device-carried, decoupled).** For each of the 12 city monitors, the days last year on which ozone's 8-hour mean exceeded the
  standard. *Device:* the network stores each 8-hour mean against the hour it ends, while the standard's daily maximum takes windows that
  start in the day, as the QA manual states. Assigning by end hour moves about one exceedance day in ten. Ozone never enters PM2.5.
* **Ask B (device-carried).** For each of the 12 quarters, the diesel trucks scrapped under the scheme. *Device:* the scheme's ledger books
  each truck at claim and again at payment under one certificate number. Counting rows nearly doubles the count. The main call never reads
  the scheme's ledger.
* **Ask C (validity).** For each of the 14 settled tranches, the verified reduction beside the one your construction gives for it.
* **Decoupling.** Clearing the regional construction or the homogenisation changes no figure in asks A or B. Ask C holds no moving
  background, by property 2.

## 11. Rubric arithmetic

12 monitors (ask A) + 12 quarters (ask B) + 14 tranches (ask C) + the named measure, the five credits and the winning margin + 5 named chart
parts + 3 files ≈ 53 criteria.

## 12. World-building constraints

* PM2.5 falls from 80 to 60 µg/m³. Weather accounts for 5.5, the instrument change hides 1.5, and the homogenised deweathered decline is
  16.0, of which 6.0 is regional.
* Credits by rung: inventory 9.0 / 4.5 / 3.0 / 2.0 / 1.5 (heating, closures, trucks, dust, fund); deweathered as read 3.4 / 3.0 / 4.2 /
  2.4 / 1.5; homogenised 3.6 / 4.6 / 3.8 / 2.5 / 1.5; decisive 2.5 / 1.4 / 3.6 / 2.5 / 6.0.
* The regional 6.0 is metals and sulfate in the ratio of 3.2 to 1.1, with 0.2 in the vehicle factor. As read, the boundary sites show 2.5.
* Six sites change instruments in the second year: the four industrial-district monitors and the two north-west boundary monitors. Their
  gravimetric samplers run throughout.
* The ledger holds 14 tranches, all with a flat background. NW-3 and SE-7 are identical on every column but the sector difference.
* Ozone windows and scrappage rows never touch PM2.5 readings, filters or the ledger.
