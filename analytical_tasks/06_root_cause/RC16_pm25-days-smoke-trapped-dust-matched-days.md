## 1. Situation

A county recorded eleven days above the 24-hour fine-particle standard this summer, against two last summer, after a large aggregate quarry opened
nearby. Residents have petitioned for enforcement; the quarry points to a severe wildfire season. The air district must file a classification of the
eleven days: smoke days go into its exceptional-events demonstration and are excluded from the county's attainment record, and three or more days
classed as local refer the quarry for an enforcement inspection. The district's procedure sets how a day is classed, and last summer's demonstration
was concurred with in full.

## 2. Gate G: why this is legal

* **Litmus.** Every figure is correct: the monitors, the smoke-plume maps, the speciation analyses, the soundings, last summer's certified
  profiles and mass balances, and the quarry's source test and shift log. The wildfire season was severe, and the quarry's dust did reach the
  monitors. Nothing reported is overturned; the certified method meets two sources that interact, which it never met.
* **Flags.** surface_read_dependency: no · stumping_family: analytical_non_defect · sole_data_defect: no.
* **Deletion test.** Delete the petition, the operator's statement, the residents' belief and the licensed basis. The balance with the quarry's
  profile still files five local days and refers the quarry.
* **Instrument repair.** Suspect file: the certified profile set, which has no crustal source for this summer. Repaired to every source
  operating this summer, the quarry's source test included, and at the deepest to a perfect apportionment of each day's mass by source, rung 2
  files 4 smoke, 2 mixed and 5 local, as rung 3 does, and refers the quarry; rungs 0 and 1 read no speciation and still file 2 local days.
  None files 4, 6 and 1. The answer still needs each day read against matched non-smoke days: an apportionment, however exact, measures what
  the quarry added under a smoke layer that held its dust down, not what the day would have read without the smoke.
* **Lens swap.** The naive classes subtract each source's measured mass from the day it was measured; the answer reads each day against other
  days without the smoke or without the quarry, a different population of days behind each class.

## 3. The driving force

A strong solver distrusts the plume map and the before-and-after comparison, applies the district's multi-line rule, which reproduces every day
accepted last summer, and goes further, because the procedure classes a day by what it would have read but for each source. It solves the
chemical mass balance over the certified profiles, finds the silica the quarry sends downwind parked in the residual, adds the quarry's
source-test profile from its permit file, and subtracts each source's mass to test the standard: four smoke days, two mixed and five local,
enough to refer the quarry. Subtraction assumes the sources add. On the five local days the smoke held the mixing height at a third of its
non-smoke median, and the quarry's dust, trapped beneath it, reached the monitor at two to three times its usual concentration; without the smoke
it would have mixed out. On non-smoke days this summer with the same wind sector and wind speed and the quarry on the same shifts, the county
stayed below the standard on four of the five. Read from those matched days, with the without-quarry concentration read from the quarry's idle
days under the same smoke, those four days needed both sources: four smoke, six mixed and one local, and no referral.

## 5. Why the decisive rung survives the opponent

1. **Written nowhere.** The procedure says "would not have exceeded but for"; the close-out lists each accepted day's without-smoke
   concentration but not how it was read. No document says smoke traps local dust or that a day without the smoke is read from other days.
2. **Corpus blind for a computable reason.** *In every closed summer no crustal source operated within 30 km and the local contribution on every
   exceedance day was under 3%, so subtracting the smoke's mass and reading matched non-smoke days give the same without-smoke concentration on
   every accepted day.* The close-out certifies the multi-line rule and the balance (14 of 14) and cannot see the trapped dust.
3. **No arithmetic symptom.** Every balance closes with a residual inside the close-out's range once the quarry's profile is in, and every
   subtracted concentration is a physically possible day.
4. **Not a row predicate.** A day's without-smoke concentration comes from other days matched on wind sector, wind speed and the quarry's shifts,
   and its without-quarry concentration from the quarry's idle days under matching smoke.
5. **The enumeration is arithmetic.** Which days are mixed is the output of eleven pairs of counterfactual readings; no column holds them.
6. **No cutover date.** The quarry's opening steps nothing that alignment can use: it fell in the same weeks the smoke season began, so the
   petition's before-and-after comparison credits the smoke to the quarry and is a decoy in the other direction (nine extra days).
7. **Survives deletion.** With every voice and the petition gone, the balance with the quarry's profile still files five local days.

## 6. The calibration corpus

* **Form.** The federal agency's concurrence on last summer's exceptional-events demonstration: the 14 exceedance days accepted, and for each
  the district's mass balance (species, profiles, contributions), its without-smoke concentration and its evidence lines.
* **What it certifies.** The multi-line rule (14 of 14), the certified profiles and the balance (every accepted day's smoke share and
  without-smoke concentration reproduced within 0.5 µg/m³), and the but-for classes.
* **What it is blind to.** Trapped local dust (above).
* **Twin pair.** 14 July and 2 August had the same maximum (41 µg/m³), medium smoke overhead, uniformity 0.31, wind from the quarry's sector,
  the quarry on double shifts and the same balance (smoke 18, quarry 15 µg/m³ above background). Read from matched non-smoke days, their
  concentrations without the smoke were 37.2 and 18.4 µg/m³ (2.02×): 14 July's light westerly carries the quarry's dust over the standard on
  its own, 2 August's fresh one does not. 14 July is local and 2 August mixed; subtraction gives both 23 µg/m³ and one class.
* **Resemblance points at the decoy.** Every one of this summer's eleven days matches an accepted smoke day of last summer on plume density,
  fire activity and uniformity.

## 7. Pins, voices and the licensed wrong basis

* **Filed pins.** The district's procedure: each exceedance day is classed smoke when it would not have exceeded the standard but for the
  smoke, local when it would not have exceeded but for local sources, and mixed when it needed both, read at the day's maximum monitor. The
  referral rule: three or more local days refer the source for inspection.
* **Empirical pins.** The balance and the certified profiles, from the close-out; the quarry's profile, from its source test.
* **Voices.** The quarry's environmental manager: "Our dust stays on site; whatever the monitors caught came off the county road." The
  district's monitoring lead: "Our rule got every single day right last year."
* **Licensed wrong basis.** The procedure records that the quarry's consultant classes days by the smoke-plume map and will present that
  classification at the hearing.

## 8. Determinism by construction

* **Matching.** A matched day is a non-smoke day this summer with the same wind sector, the same wind-speed band and the quarry on the same
  shifts (idle, single, double); every one of the eleven days has at least four, and the reading is their median.
* **Idle days.** The without-quarry reading is the median of the quarry's idle days (Sundays and its two shutdown weeks) in the same smoke
  density band; every day has at least three.
* **Balance.** Effective-variance solution over the filed species; every day's balance converges under every profile set.
* **Standard.** No counterfactual reading sits within 1.5 µg/m³ of the standard under any construction.
* **Days.** The eleven days are fixed by the standard; every monitor reports at least 18 valid hours on each.
* **Rounding.** Day counts are whole; concentrations to 0.1 µg/m³.

## 9. Prompt sketch and deliverables

> The district has to file how it classifies this summer's eleven bad-air days, and whatever we file decides whether the quarry is referred.
> The residents' group is sure the quarry caused every one of them. Tell me how many of the eleven days we class as smoke, mixed and local, and
> whether that refers the quarry, as the paragraph for the hearing brief. Send `exceedance_days.csv`, a chart `days_without_each_source.png`
> and `hearing_brief.docx`.

* `exceedance_days.csv` — one row per day: evidence lines, species, the balance, the subtracted and the matched readings, and the class.
* `days_without_each_source.png` — paired bars of each day's maximum against its without-smoke and without-quarry readings under subtraction
  and under matched days, the standard as a line, each day's class labelled, and 14 July and 2 August marked.
* `hearing_brief.docx` — the classification, the referral, and what subtraction and last year's rule miss.

## 10. The ask layer

* **Ask A (device-carried, decoupled).** For each of the four ozone monitors, the summer's fourth-highest daily maximum 8-hour average.
  *Device:* 8-hour averages are labelled by their starting hour and the daily maximum takes only windows starting 07:00 to 23:00, as the
  data-handling appendix documents; labelling by ending hour or using all 24 windows moves the fourth-highest value at two monitors.
* **Ask B (device-carried).** For each month of operation, the quarry's reported production and operating hours. *Device:* its quarterly
  reports switch from short tons to metric tonnes in the second quarter, with a units field, as the permit's reporting form documents; summing
  across the switch overstates June by about 10%.
* **Ask C (validity).** For each of last summer's 14 accepted days, the class and without-smoke concentration each of the five constructions
  returns; and this summer's eleven classes under each construction.
* **Decoupling.** Clearing the matched reading changes no figure in asks A or B.

## 11. Rubric arithmetic

4 monitors (ask A) + 4 months × 2 figures (ask B) + 14 days × 5 constructions + 11 days × 5 constructions (ask C) + the three class counts and
the referral + 5 named chart parts + 3 files ≈ 150 criteria.
