## 1. Situation

An open mapping foundation signs a two-year grant with a humanitarian fund on 1 March to grow the volunteer mapping community in Kessa.
The fund pays a bonus if Kessa's volunteer stickiness rises a quarter above the baseline, and the baseline is Q4 2026. The grant's
verification clause requires the baseline and every later reading to come from the platform's public statistics service. The service
publishes monthly tables by country in two views, activity in a country and contributors by home country, a regional summary for each
volunteer chapter, and an organised-editing view of the accounts on the platform's organised-editing registry. Cells under its privacy
thresholds are shown as "<50" for MAU and "<25" for DAU, except in the organised view, which its disclosure rules publish in full. The
pack also holds the 14 existing grants with their filed and audited baselines and the Organised Editing Guidelines.

## 2. Gate G: why this is legal

* **Litmus.** Every figure is correct: both views, the regional summaries, the organised view and the existing grants. The dashboard's
  headline is a true statement about activity in Kessa, and the home view is true about everyone homed there. Nobody's reading of their
  own numbers is overturned. The difficulty is who the grant counts.
* **Flags.** surface_read_dependency: no · stumping_family: analytical_non_defect · sole_data_defect: no.
* **Deletion test.** Delete the partnerships lead's view, the chapter coordinator's view and the fund's licensed basis. The home view is
  still the grain all 14 existing baselines reproduce on, and nothing on it separates a paid editor from a volunteer.
* **Instrument repair.** Clean-data test. The suspect file is the home view, whose November and December daily cells are withheld. Fill
  them (publish 19.0 and 15.0): rung 1's fallback and rung 2's recovery both become a direct read of 24.2%, rung 0 stays at 15.1%, the
  answer is unchanged, and subtracting the organised editors is still needed. No other file is suspect: the organised view and the
  regional summaries are published in full, and the home view's count of everyone homed in Kessa is correct for what it counts.
* **Lens swap.** The two reads cover different populations. In October 498 contributors were active in Kessa, 250 were homed there, and
  238 of those were volunteers; in December 79 were homed there and 67 were volunteers.

## 3. The driving force

A strong solver moves from the dashboard's activity view to the home view the annex names, which the existing grants reproduce exactly.
It meets "<25" in November and December and, rather than drop the months or borrow the region's rate, recovers each withheld cell as the
chapter region's daily total less its eight published countries: 19.0 and 15.0. Every number is now published or exactly recovered, and
the baseline is 24.2%. But the annex counts volunteers, and the Organised Editing Guidelines say editing done as a job is not
volunteering. A mapping company opened a Kessa office in 2026, and its twelve editors are homed in Kessa and edit every working day.
They are a small share of the monthly contributors and a large share of the daily ones, more than half in December, so they hold the
ratio up. The organised view publishes their counts, and the volunteer figure is the home view less that view, month by month: 29.8%,
12.9% and 9.9%.

* **Figure shape.** The corrections walk the figure up and the decisive move reverses them. A baseline filed at rung 2 sets the bonus
  line 38% above where the volunteer community stands, so the grant could deliver its purpose and never pay.
* **Partial correction priced (L3).** A solver who subtracts the organised editors but keeps the region-rate fill lands at 14.0% (−20.0%),
  and one who subtracts them but fills the band midpoint at 13.0% (−25.7%). Subtracting them from the activity view gives 13.1%
  (−25.1%), and subtracting them but dropping the withheld months gives 29.8% (+70.3%).
* **Grid.** View (activity, or home with the withheld cells dropped, filled at the region rate, the band midpoint or its upper bound, or
  recovered) × organised editors (in or out) gives 12 cells. The nearest wrong cell is the activity view at 15.1% (−13.7%), and every
  other cell is at least 18% away.

## 5. Why the decisive rung survives the opponent

1. **Written nowhere.** The annex says "volunteer contributors homed in the country". The guidelines define organised editing for the
   registry. The service's methodology note says organised accounts appear in every view. No document connects the three, or says the
   home view includes anyone the grant excludes.
2. **Corpus blind to organised editing.** The existing book certifies the home grain exactly (14 of 14, against 2 of 14 on the activity
   view). *In every existing grant the organised view was empty for the country, because no organisation on the registry had editors
   homed in any past programme country.* Run over the existing book, the home view and the volunteer construction give the same 14
   baselines.
3. **No arithmetic symptom.** Every published cell is exact, the views tie to their regional summaries, and the organised accounts are a
   subset of the home view in every month.
4. **Not a row predicate.** The volunteer count is a difference of two published views for the same country-month. No row in either
   view carries it.
5. **The enumeration is arithmetic.** Six cells are subtracted, and two of the home cells are first recovered by subtraction.
6. **No cutover date.** The team has been homed in Kessa all quarter, and no series steps on its arrival.
7. **Survives deletion.** No wrong number exists to delete. Without any voice, the home view is still the certified grain.

## 6. The calibration corpus

* **Form.** The 14 existing country grants (2021–2025): each with the baseline filed from the public service and the value the
  foundation's audit later recomputed from raw logs, volunteers only.
* **What it certifies (E07).** The home grain: all 14 filed baselines reproduce exactly from the home view and match their audits. The
  activity view reproduces 2 of 14, and every miss is low, because remote mappers' bursty activity drags it down.
* **What it is blind to.** Organised editing (above), and withheld cells: every past baseline month was published.
* **Twin pair.** Kessa and Doru in December are identical on every home-view column: 79 MAU and a withheld DAU recovered as 15.0. Kessa's
  15.0 include 8.4 organised editors a day and Doru's none. Their volunteer stickiness is 9.9% and 19.0% (1.9×), and every construction
  on the home view gives them the same 19.0%.
* **Resemblance points at the decoy.** Kessa's home profile most resembles Velmar's, an existing grant whose filed baseline equals its
  home-view figure because no organised team was homed there.

## 7. Pins, voices and the licensed wrong basis

* **Filed pins.** The grant's KPI annex: "Contributors are the volunteer contributors homed in the country as the statistics service
  assigns it each month; a quarter's stickiness is the mean of its three monthly ratios of average daily to monthly active contributors,
  stated to one decimal of a per cent." The verification clause: "The baseline and every KPI reading are taken from the platform's public
  statistics service." The guidelines: "Editing done as part of employment or a contract is organised editing, not volunteer editing."
* **Empirical pins.** The home grain, from the existing book.
* **Voices.** The partnerships lead: "The fund will check the dashboard's headline for Kessa; that's the number to sign." The chapter
  coordinator for the Lakes region: "Kessa behaves like the rest of the Lakes chapter. When their figures are hidden, ours are the best
  guide."
* **Licensed wrong basis.** The verification clause records that the fund's monitoring team reads the activity view and will report its
  figure beside the foundation's.

## 8. Determinism by construction

* **Partition.** Home countries are assigned per contributor-month, every Lakes contributor has one home country a month, and Kessa holds
  the only withheld daily cells in the region in Q4, so each recovery has one unknown.
* **The organised view.** It uses the home view's assignment and is a subset of it in every month; DAU are monthly averages of daily
  counts, to one decimal, in every view, so subtraction is exact.
* **Averaging.** The annex fixes the mean of monthly ratios; the ratio of summed DAU to summed MAU is a hazard the annex refutes.
* **Rounding.** No construction lands within 0.05 points of a rounding boundary.

## 9. Prompt sketch and deliverables

> The grant with the humanitarian fund is signed on 1 March, and it needs Kessa's baseline: volunteer stickiness for last quarter, the
> number every future reading is measured against. Our partnerships lead believes the dashboard's headline for Kessa is what the fund
> will check. Give me the baseline as a percentage to one decimal, as the line that goes in the agreement, and send
> `baseline_build.xlsx` with the build and the sheets below, plus `stickiness_months.png`.

* `baseline_build.xlsx` — the recovery, the organised subtraction and the baseline, the four rung constructions (ask C), the
  new-contributor sheet (ask A) and the building-edits sheet (ask B).
* `stickiness_months.png` — Kessa's monthly stickiness for October to December under the four rung constructions, the organised editors'
  share of daily contributors as bars, the recovered months marked, and the Kessa–Doru December twin annotated.

## 10. The ask layer

* **Ask A (device-carried, decoupled).** New contributors (first-ever edit) homed in each of the nine Lakes countries in each month of Q4
  2026. *Device:* accounts created in bulk through the humanitarian tasking tool often wait months before a first edit, and the service's
  definitions page dates a new contributor by first changeset, not account creation. Counting by creation date misplaces 22% to 61% of
  new contributors in six countries.
* **Ask B (device-carried).** Net buildings added in each of Kessa's three priority districts in each month from July to December 2026.
  *Device:* bulk imports carry an import tag and are excluded from mapped totals, as the import guidelines document, and a reverted
  changeset is listed in the revert log against its original id. Ignoring either overstates 13 of the 18 cells.
* **Ask C (validity).** The baseline under each of the four rung constructions, the two recovered DAU values and Kessa's organised cells.
* **Decoupling.** The new-contributor export and the changeset statistics share no row with the DAU and MAU tables. Clearing the
  organised subtraction changes no figure in asks A or B.

## 11. Rubric arithmetic

9 countries × 3 months (ask A) + 3 districts × 6 months (ask B) + 4 constructions, 2 recovered cells and 6 organised cells (ask C) + the
committed baseline, the twin values and the grain gap in October + 5 named chart parts + 2 files ≈ 69 criteria.
