What did each of the changes Strathcorrie Council made to S1 to S3 maths for session 2025-26 cost its pupils in the June 2026 assessment, and which cost them the most marks? Its 2027-28 budget, set in February, will reverse that one change. A committee report (maths_change_review.docx) should name it and the runner-up, give the marks each change cost the pupils it reached with a bar chart of those costs, and say why each other change cost fewer marks; with it, a table (school_year_marks.csv) with one row per school, year group and session giving the pupils who sat the June paper, their mean mark and how many reached 36 of 60. Marks are to one decimal place and pupil counts whole.
