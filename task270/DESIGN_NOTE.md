The Fitness Professional Certification Board, a United States nonprofit that certifies personal trainers, redesigns its candidate score report for the January 2027 testing window: every report carries the total scaled score and the pass or fail outcome, and the Board's score reporting policy (2027 revision) prints a content-area score only for a content area whose score tells the candidate more about their standing in that area than the total score does.
