# task270 design note

Architecture 5, drawn 2026-10-26 in a fresh world (wave 4, Descriptive lane, sathay3012), after architecture 4 died at its draw probes the same day. Architectures 1 to 4 and every screen under `## Tried and rejected` stay dead. Kleerwissel, a Dutch second-hand fashion marketplace app, reads every onboarding experiment on a two-week metric, because six-month retention arrives half a year after a change ships. Its 2027 proxy metric standard adopts the candidate whose effects across past experiments best track the same changes' effects on six-month retention, and the growth team adopts one of the five candidates in the metric catalogue for every 2027 onboarding experiment.
