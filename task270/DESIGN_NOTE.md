# task270 design note

Architecture 2, re-rooted 2026-10-19 (wave 4, Descriptive lane, sathay3012) after architecture 1's two stage-4 readers both landed the call. A member-owned motor insurer in England and Wales makes protection of the no-claims steps standard on every private car policy from the 1 January 2027 renewals, so an at-fault claim will no longer move a policy down its steps. Its pricing committee places three of the eight rating regions in the top frequency band of the 2027 rating basis, by the claim frequency each region's policies show over the 2024 and 2025 accident years on the 2027 wording.

## Stump sentence

A competent solver ranks the regions by their reported 2024 and 2025 claim frequency, or lifts steps 6 to 9 by the Step Protection holders' frequency, and files band C as South East, West Midlands and Yorkshire and the Humber, South East in place of North West, whose policyholders stand mostly on the middle steps with the book's highest premiums, because there an at-fault claim costs the most premium over the climb back and those policyholders pay small damage themselves, so North West reports the smallest share of its accidents while South East, on the top steps and under Step Protection, reports nearly all of its own; only recovering the absorbed claims, from the claims no policyholder absorbs and the size distribution of the policyholders a claim costs nothing, places North West in band C.

## Three rules

Task type: claims-frequency-severity (Actuarial Science & Insurance), which serves Descriptive & Distribution Analysis in the taxonomy.
Domain test: the quantity that decides the call is each region's claim frequency on a wording where an at-fault claim costs no no-claims steps, the actuary's frequency of accidents above the excess recovered through the truncation a step scale imposes on reported claims: the claims every policyholder makes, scaled by the ground-up size distribution of those for whom a claim already costs nothing.
Swap: "which three regions' patients will report the most symptoms once a report no longer raises their premium": there is no scale whose cost of a report depends on the reporter's own position, no excess and no size through which absorbed events could be recovered, so the step-by-step truncation and its recovery stop making sense.
Layer 1: the 2025 move from the legacy policy system to the new platform (policies renumbered at their first renewal there, joined through a crosswalk, and claims notified after that renewal registered under the new number even when the accident fell in the legacy year); mid-term adjustments that move a car to another rating region or change its cover (exposure split by days, a district on the legacy system and a full postcode on the platform); the legacy expiry date the last day on cover against the platform's renewal date; fault settled, split or pending, not-at-fault claims recovered from the other insurer, nil notifications and rejected third-party claims; amounts in pence on the legacy claims system and pounds on the platform; two postcode districts reassigned between regions from 1 April 2025; notification times in UTC on the platform. Each is produced by the mutual's own systems, and nothing is placed to be caught.
Layer 2: estimation_fit: a frequency under a changed reporting incentive, recovered through the cut in claim size that the old incentive imposed step by step.
Decisive step: recover each region's absorbed at-fault claims step by step, counting the at-fault claims above the size no policyholder absorbs and scaling them by the share of claims that large among the policyholders a claim costs nothing (step 0 and Step Protection), with the not-at-fault claims everyone already makes added as they stand.
Not semantics: every field read correctly (the step at inception, fault, the excess, the add-on, windscreen, the rating basis note's wording) still leaves the reported frequency as the natural estimate of a region's frequency; no file says that a claim was absorbed, by whom or how many.
Not rule application: the rating basis note fixes what counts (the claims made on the 2027 wording) and no filed rule or procedure estimates the claims the old wording kept out of the record; executing every stated rule files the reported frequency.
Analyst test: a strong analyst who builds each region's 2024 and 2025 vehicle-years and accident claims correctly ranks the regions by their reported claim frequency, or lifts steps 6 to 9 by the Step Protection holders' frequency against other policyholders on those steps, and files band C as South East, West Midlands and Yorkshire and the Humber (the same three on the lift, and on a lift carried to every insured step); only the size-based recovery places North West, whose policyholders stand mostly on the middle steps, in band C.
Estimand: the 2027 rating basis note (a filed PDF), section 2, fixes the population (each region's policies over the 2024 and 2025 accident years, by garaging region on the day), the outcome (the accident claims with a cost to the mutual, at fault, split or not at fault, that those policies would have made had every policy already carried step protection; windscreen, theft and fire claims outside), the window, the unit (claims per 100 vehicle-years earned) and the comparison (the three highest regions form band C); the sentence is quoted under `## Literal reader`.
No decoy: no memo, draft, briefing or stakeholder figure argues for a band; Step Protection is an add-on the mutual sells, its holders self-selected as any add-on's buyers are, and nothing in the pack ranks the regions on the 2027 basis.
No certified replay: no published or certified frequency is shipped to be reproduced, and the method is not recovered from controls.
Lesson: under a no-claims scale the claims record is cut below a cost that each policyholder's step fixes, deepest on the middle steps; a frequency for a wording that removes the cost is recovered from the claims every policyholder makes and the size distribution of those for whom a claim costs nothing, and a group that bought out the cost gives the size of claims, not their rate. task255 teaches a severity fit through deductibles written on every claim; no build on file teaches the incentive cut of a frequency.

## Literal reader

Executed word for word, the basis note files each region's frequency as the accident claims the mutual paid or reserved for that the region's policies would have made over the 2024 and 2025 accident years had every policy already carried step protection, per 100 vehicle-years. The literal quantity needs, for the policies without protection, the claims their holders would have made had a claim cost no steps, which no file records; the claims as made fill it with the accidents each policyholder chose to claim under the old wording. The step-0 policies and the Step Protection holders already claimed under a wording where a claim cost nothing, and claims above the largest amount any policyholder absorbs are complete on every step, so the literal execution is the size-based recovery: each step's claims above that amount, scaled by the share of claims that large among the policyholders a claim costs nothing, the golden.
