# task270 design note

Architecture 8, drawn 2026-10-27 in a fresh world (wave 4, Descriptive lane, sathay3012), after architecture 7 was spent at stage 4 on 2026-10-26. Architectures 1 to 7 and every screen under the tried-and-rejected section stay dead. NHS Iversford Vale Integrated Care Board commissions a statin adherence service from its community pharmacies for 2027-28: a pharmacist contacts the patients aged 65 and over who collect and take their own statins and held statin for under 80 per cent of their 2026 days, and the board pays an enhanced tier (more funded consultations and a weekly pharmacist session with each practice) in the primary care networks where 30 per cent or more of those patients fell under 80 per cent. The board's medicines optimisation committee fixes the tier schedule from the 2026 dispensing before the service contract issues to pharmacies on 1 March 2027. Care home residents, and patients whose medicines a pharmacy packs into a compliance aid, are outside the service: their medicines are reviewed under the structured medication review service. The twelve networks' practices run on two clinical systems, Quillon and Tessaly; only Quillon's year-end extract carries the compliance-aid code, and its practices code about two thirds of the patients who use one.

## Three rules

Task type: medication-adherence-persistence (Pharmacology, Pharmacoepidemiology & Safety), which serves Descriptive & Distribution Analysis in the taxonomy.
Domain test: the deciding quantity is each network's share of its eligible patients whose 2026 proportion of days covered fell under 80 per cent, pharmacoepidemiology's refill-adherence measure built as the specification files it; the measure reads a patient's taking only where the patient's own requests time the refills, so the field computes it over the patients who collect their own medicines, and the patients a pharmacy supplies on its own compliance-aid cycle have to be found and left out.
Swap: "the gyms where 30 per cent of members who book their own classes attended under 80 per cent of weeks" reads alike, but a gym's booking is the attendance it records and carries nothing forward between bookings, so there are no days covered by a supply and no supplier booking members on its own cycle into the measure; the task needs a supply measure whose refills most patients' own taking times.
Layer 1: the two practice systems' year-end extracts in different layouts (Quillon's CSV with SNOMED codes and day-first dates, Tessaly's workbook with its own code labels), the board's data service re-keying every patient pseudonym on 1 July 2026 (a crosswalk file), dosage instructions as prescribers typed them (ONE AT NIGHT, 1 OD, TWO TABLETS AT NIGHT, AS DIRECTED) and statins by brand or generic name, two practices that moved network on 1 April 2026, pharmacies that changed owner and ODS code in 2026, patients who died or left during 2026; each written by the system named, none placed to be caught.
Layer 2: estimand_identification, own:cycle-dispensed supply left out of a refill measure's population where no code marks it: the right population for a refill measure is the patients whose refills their own taking times, and those a pharmacy supplies on its own cycle are found from the supply record's structure where the extract does not code them.
Decisive step: taking every patient whose statin a pharmacy dispensed on a compliance-aid cycle (every item on the patient's set weekday, exactly 7 or 28 days after the last) out of each network's count, coded or not, so each share is taken over the patients who collect their own statins; Quillon's coded aid patients all show the cycle and no self-managing patient does.
Not semantics: every field, code and definition read correctly (both extracts' care home and compliance-aid codes, the specification's scope and its days-covered definition, the dosage text, the pseudonym crosswalk) leaves the uncoded aid patients in the count, because Tessaly's extract has no aid code at all and Quillon's codes about two thirds of its aid patients; reading the codes as the scope names four networks, and no field marks the patients the scope leaves out.
Not rule application: the specification files the scope (patients who collect and take their own statins; not care home residents, not patients whose medicines a pharmacy packs into a compliance aid) and the measure, and nothing on how to find an aid patient no code marks; executing every filed rule on the coded patients names four networks. The family the rule leaves open, measured on the prototype: enumerated (the cycle read exactly, within one day, within two days, within three days at 80 per cent of intervals; weekly 7-day items alone; full cover alone; the coded patients alone) spreads three networks across its members; standing (the readings Quillon's coded aid patients validate, catching every coded patient and no self-managing patient: exact, within one day, within two days) spreads none, five networks each; the ruling-out is quiet, done only by checking a reading against the coded patients, which none of the cheap routes does.
Analyst test: a strong analyst who reads the specification and both extracts correctly takes out care home residents and the coded aid patients, computes each network's 2026 share under 80 per cent of days covered over the rest and names four networks (prototype), leaving out Briskdale; executed on the generator's clean truth in `checks.py` (R3a ANALYST), where the decisive step alone names the five (R3b DECISIVE).
Estimand: the statin adherence service specification 2027-28, 2.1 (the population: patients registered with the board's practices on 31 December 2026, aged 65 or over then, who had a statin dispensed in 2026 and collect and take their own statins; care home residents and patients whose medicines a pharmacy packs into a compliance aid are outside it), 2.2 (the proportion of days covered in 2026 as defined there) and 3.1 (the enhanced tier: the networks where 30 per cent or more of the population fell under 80 per cent).
No decoy: no memo, dashboard, report or stakeholder figure states a network's share or ranks the networks, and no document argues for the coded patients as the scope; the community pharmacy contract's note that aids are dispensed weekly is a true operational fact every route reads the same way.
No certified replay: no certified or published adherence figure ships; nothing is reproduced to recover a method.
Lesson: a refill measure reads a patient's taking only where the patient times the refills; where a pharmacy dispenses on its own cycle the record reads as full cover whatever is taken, so those patients leave the measure's population, and where no code marks them the supply's own regularity does; no build on file fixes a measure's population by who times the record it is read from.
