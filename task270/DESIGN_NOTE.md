Iversford Vale GP Federation, the provider company its member practices own, runs a statin adherence service for the twelve primary care networks those practices form, subcontracting the community pharmacies, for 2027-28: a pharmacist contacts the patients aged 65 and over who collect and take their own statins and held statin for under 80 per cent of their 2026 days, and the federation funds an enhanced tier (a weekly pharmacist session with each practice) in the networks where 30 per cent or more of those patients fell under 80 per cent. The federation's board fixes the tier schedule from the 2026 dispensing before the service launches on 1 April 2027; NHS Iversford Vale Integrated Care Board's health data service supplies the dispensing and the practice lists, and the board's guidance governs compliance-aid supply. Care home residents, and patients whose medicines a pharmacy packs into a compliance aid, are outside the service: their medicines are reviewed under the structured medication review service. The member practices run on two clinical systems, Quarvis and Synaris; only Quarvis's year-end list records the compliance aid, and its practices record about two thirds of the patients who use one.
