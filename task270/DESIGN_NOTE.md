## Three rules

- Task type: network-analysis in urban-regional-science, which serves descriptive-distribution: the distribution of 2026's fire cover over a county's homes on its road network, cut by district.
- Domain test: the deciding quantity is each district's time-averaged fire cover, the share of its homes a free engine can reach within the attendance standard over the road network with its crew's turnout added, the measure fire services model for their community risk management plans by replaying appliance availability over drive times; the field's estimate is that replay.
- Swap: "which districts kept a free fire engine within ten minutes of fewer than three quarters of their homes" with porters for engines and wards for homes: porters have no road network, no duty system's turnout and no station to stand by at, so the drive times, turnouts and standby moves the measure runs on stop making sense.
- Layer 1: the mobilising system re-sending an unchanged status every four hours; status rows and location rows interleaved in one log; one-way town links and missing farm-track links in the routing network; residential against commercial address classes; the on-call bookings in local clock time against the log's UTC; incident records on 2011 output areas before April 2023 and on a new classification from April 2024. Each is how the service's own systems record, and none is placed to be caught or carries the call (location times read an hour out move no district across the line).
- Layer 2: field_method, the network cover replay read from where each free engine stood rather than from its station, since the record's free engines were away from their stations on training and prevention work or standing by at other stations.
- Decisive step: placing each free engine, minute by minute, where the log's location rows show it before taking its ten-minute reach, rather than at the station the fleet list attaches it to.
- Not semantics: every status code, time zone, key, class and unit read correctly still leaves the station replay, because no field says a free engine is away: a status row marks it free wherever it stands, the fleet list gives its station, and a location row carries no status, so only reading a free engine's reach from where the log places it moves the call.
- Not rule application: the standard files what cover is (a home a free engine could reach within ten minutes, turnout included) and no rule says where an engine stands. Enumerated family (R0, R1, R2, R2b, P1, P3, P4, R3 and R3's readings): five distinct named sets (none; Altmoor and Skirwold; Tollesham, Wrenfold and Pelmarsh; those three with Altmoor and Skirwold; Wrenfold and Pelmarsh). Standing family (the readings that place free engines where the log has them: last report, nearest report, interpolated, five-minute sampling): one set, spread 0.022 points, the same whole per cent in every district. Loudness of the ruling-out: no clause rules the station replays out; only the data does, 13.9 per cent of the wholetime engines' free minutes out of base.
- Analyst test: a strong analyst who reads the cover standard, the status log and the road network correctly replays 2026 minute by minute, credits every free engine with its own station's ten-minute reach and names Altmoor and Skirwold, when free wholetime engines spent weekday mornings at the Brandeth training centre and afternoons on home fire safety visits and Calderstow engines stood by at rural on-call stations, so read from where the engines stood Wrenfold and Pelmarsh fall below the line and the rural two clear it.
- Estimand: each district's share of its homes (residential addresses) that a fire engine free in that minute could reach within ten minutes, its crew's turnout included, over the road network, averaged over every minute of 2026, against 75 per cent; filed in the service's cover standard, section 2.
- No decoy: nothing is planted: the training mornings, prevention visits and standby moves are the service's ordinary 2026 operations, recorded as they happened, and no document states a cover figure, ranks the districts or describes how cover was modelled before.
- No certified replay: no published or certified cover figure ships, so nothing recovers the method by reproducing a figure; the 2023-2027 plan's cover table is not in the pack.
- Lesson: a mobile server's cover is where it stands, not where it is based: a status record marks availability, and available servers spend part of the year elsewhere on other work or standing in at other bases; task143 teaches joint availability at fixed bases and task273 reach through operable valves in a fixed network, and no build on file reads reach from where servers stood.
