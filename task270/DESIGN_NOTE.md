# task270 design note

Architecture 2, re-rooted 2026-10-19 (wave 4, Descriptive lane, sathay3012) after architecture 1's two stage-4 readers both landed the call. A member-owned motor insurer in England and Wales makes protection of the no-claims steps standard on every private car policy from the 1 January 2027 renewals, so an at-fault claim will no longer move a policy down its steps. Its pricing committee places three of the eight rating regions in the top frequency band of the 2027 rating basis, by the claim frequency each region's 2025 and 2026 policies show on the 2027 wording.

## Three rules

- `Task type:` claims-frequency-severity (Actuarial Science & Insurance), which serves Descriptive & Distribution Analysis in the taxonomy.
- `Domain test:` the quantity that decides the call is each region's claim frequency on a wording where an at-fault claim costs no no-claims steps, the actuary's frequency of accidents above the excess recovered through the truncation a step scale imposes on reported claims: the claims every policyholder makes, scaled by the ground-up size distribution of those for whom a claim already costs nothing.
- `Swap:` "which three regions' patients will report the most symptoms once a report no longer raises their premium": there is no scale whose cost of a report depends on the reporter's own position, no excess and no size through which absorbed events could be recovered, so the step-by-step truncation and its recovery stop making sense.
- `Layer 1:` the 2025 move from the legacy policy system to the new platform (policy numbers re-issued, joined through a crosswalk); mid-term adjustments that move a vehicle to another rating region (exposure split by days); the no-claims scale renumbered at the 2024 revision (a conversion table); fault settled, split or still pending, not-at-fault claims recovered from the other insurer, windscreen claims outside the scale; amounts in pence in the legacy claims system and pounds in the new one; two postcode districts remapped between regions from April 2026; incident dates in local time against system timestamps in UTC. Each is produced by the mutual's own systems, and nothing is placed to be caught.
- `Layer 2:` estimation_fit: a frequency under a changed reporting incentive, recovered through the cut in claim size that the old incentive imposed step by step.
- `Decisive step:` recover each region's absorbed at-fault claims step by step, counting the at-fault claims above the size no policyholder absorbs and scaling them by the share of claims that large among the policyholders a claim costs nothing (step 0 and Step Protection), with the not-at-fault claims everyone already makes added as they stand.
- `Not semantics:` every field read correctly (the step at inception, fault, the excess, the add-on, windscreen, the rating basis note's wording) still leaves the reported frequency as the natural estimate of a region's frequency; no file says that a claim was absorbed, by whom or how many.
- `Not rule application:` the rating basis note fixes what counts (the claims made on the 2027 wording) and no filed rule or procedure estimates the claims the old wording kept out of the record; executing every stated rule files the reported frequency.
- `Analyst test:` a strong analyst who builds each region's 2025 and 2026 vehicle-years and claims correctly ranks the regions by their reported claim frequency, or lifts steps 6 to 9 by the Step Protection holders' frequency against other policyholders on those steps, and places the wrong third region in the band; only the size-based recovery on every insured step places the region whose policyholders stand mostly on the middle steps.
- `Estimand:` the 2027 rating basis note (a filed PDF) fixes the population (each region's policies over the 2025 and 2026 policy years, as they were), the outcome (claims on the 2027 wording, at-fault and not-at-fault, above the excess, windscreen excluded), the window, the unit (claims per 100 vehicle-years) and the comparison (the three highest regions go in band C); the exact sentence is written at stage 2.
- `No decoy:` no memo, draft, briefing or stakeholder figure argues for a band; Step Protection is an add-on the mutual sells, its holders self-selected as any add-on's buyers are, and nothing in the pack ranks the regions on the 2027 basis.
- `No certified replay:` no published or certified frequency is shipped to be reproduced, and the method is not recovered from controls.
- `Lesson:` under a no-claims scale the claims record is cut below a cost that each policyholder's step fixes, deepest on the middle steps; a frequency for a wording that removes the cost is recovered from the claims every policyholder makes and the size distribution of those for whom a claim costs nothing, and a group that bought out the cost gives the size of claims, not their rate. task255 teaches a severity fit through deductibles written on every claim; no build on file teaches the incentive cut of a frequency.
