## 1. Final Recommendation

**The 2027 entry examination should keep the written test and the oral board.**
Across the 3,668 candidates who sat all five components, with the appointees' correlations corrected for the lists' selection on the written test and oral board composite, the written test leads and the oral board stands at least 0.14 above the work style inventory, the first component below. Not the work style inventory, which leads among the 787 appointees only because the lists never selected on it. Not the situational judgement test or the physical ability test, fourth and fifth.

## 2. Critical Components

1. **3,668** candidates sat all five components in the four 2024 and 2025 examinations; **787** of them were appointed and carry an academy criterion.
2. Among the appointees the work style inventory (**0.25**) and the written test (**0.24**) correlate most with the criterion, the oral board **0.14**.
3. Corrected for the selection, the order is the written test, the oral board, the work style inventory, the situational judgement test, the physical ability test, the oral board at least **0.14** above the inventory under every model of the selection.

## 3. Step-by-Step Solution

1. Put the five score files on the Commission's candidate numbers (the crosswalk for Pellwyn's and the inventory's identifiers) with the trial plan's scores: 3,668 candidates sat all five.
2. Took the appointments from the eligibility lists and each appointee's criterion from the gradebook (the final average, or the average at separation, which the new layout from class 25-3 keeps in its own column): 787 of the pool were appointed.
3. Correlated each score with the criterion among the appointees: the work style inventory 0.25, the written test 0.24, the oral board 0.14.
4. Modelled the appointments as the rules made them, in rank order of the final score (the written test and the oral board with the veterans' credit) among the candidates who passed the course, and corrected with the pool's covariances of those four selection variables and the appointees' covariances of every score and the criterion (the multivariate correction for selection on several variables): the written test and the oral board are kept, the oral board at least 0.14 above the inventory under every model of that selection.
5. Costed each component from the ledger over the four examinations: its cost center's lines (payroll placed by its pay period on the calendar's examination days, the April 2025 retroactive pay, credits), without the 30 June 2024 accruals, whose invoices posted in full and whose balance was reversed in one line on the administration cost center, and with the 30 June 2025 accruals, whose invoices posted only their difference; plus the administration cost center's lines for the four examinations shared by minutes; divided by the candidates who sat it.
6. Counted the people who applied: entry applications submitted by the closing time in both exports (the new system's times read in Eastern time, the autumn 2025 lateral applicants left out by the officer question), forms joined into one person when they share a name and date of birth, an email, or a name and phone number, since the legacy mobile app and the 2025 batch upload read a day-first date of birth month first.
7. Divided the candidates who sat each component by the 8,769 people who applied in the four examinations for the completion rates.
