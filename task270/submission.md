## 1. Final Recommendation

**The Research Committee should name Quarndale and Thurlwick as the recruiting sites for ARV-1102.**

Once the one-time gain a patient's second completed ACE-III carries from having sat the test before (2.5 points) is taken out of each patient's own decline, Quarndale's patients decline 5.0 ACE-III points a year and Thurlwick's 4.5, ahead of Mosswick on the unrounded figures. Not Penwardine, fourth once the gain is out, which a slope through every assessment ranks second because nearly half of its patients sat their first ACE-III before 2022 and carry the gain through the whole period, where most of Mosswick's are new to the test. Not Quarndale's last place on that slope, which is the gain flattening the slopes drawn through a patient's first two or three assessments at the clinic whose patients are nearly all new to the test.

## 2. Critical Components

1. The second completed ACE-III carries a one-time gain of **2.5** points over the patient's own trend, estimated within patients.
2. Quarndale's patients decline **5.0** ACE-III points a year net of the gain, the fastest of the eight.
3. Thurlwick's patients decline **4.5** points a year; Mosswick, the first clinic below, rounds to **4.5** but trails it unrounded.

## 3. Step-by-Step Solution

1. Joined the CIS forms to the EPR sets through the master patient index (merged hospital numbers included, sets entered in error left out) and numbered each patient's completed assessments over their whole record.
2. Kept the patients whose latest diagnosis on the record on 30 September 2026 is Alzheimer's disease or mild cognitive impairment, with two or more completed assessments from 1 January 2022 to 30 September 2026, at the clinic of the last (paper RC/26/11, section 3): 5,074 patients.
3. Estimated the gain at the second completed assessment within patients, each patient with three or more assessments given their own intercept and slope and one shared step from their second completed assessment on: 2.5 points.
4. Took each patient's decline as the least-squares slope of their totals net of the gain and averaged each clinic's patients, ranked unrounded: Quarndale 5.0 and Thurlwick 4.5 points a year lead, Mosswick first below.
5. Questionnaire B1 and B2: episodes open on 30 September 2026 in the EPR episodes extract, less the patients the appointments show discharged at a review, or handed to the care home liaison team, between the episode's start and that day, less deaths on the Spine, by the latest recorded diagnosis and by age on 1 January 2027.
6. Questionnaire C1 and C2: for diagnoses given from 1 October 2025 to 30 September 2026, the tracker's referral date to the appointment whose outcome records the diagnosis given, not the tracker's diagnosis date, which is the day it reached the problem list.
7. Named Quarndale and Thurlwick as the recruiting sites.
