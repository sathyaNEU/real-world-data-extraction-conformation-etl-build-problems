## Three rules

- Task type: Survey Research & Official Statistics, census-register-administrative-data-quality (a timely self-report validated against an administrative register on the years both cover: the record-check design with its reference periods aligned, its reporting error split into recipiency and amount, each reporter's error rate estimated with the credibility a short record allows), which serves Data Extraction & Conformation.
- Domain test: the quantity that decides the call is each faculty member's 2026 remuneration from a single manufacturer, estimated as a record-check study estimates a reported amount: each report compared with the register over the window the report could cover (the payments made by the day it was submitted), the reporter's amount-reporting ratio on those matched reports credibility-weighted toward the population's, and the year's report grossed up for the part of the year it could not see; reading reports as covering the whole year, face value, the population ratio, the overall share disclosed or the unweighted personal ratio each change the docket.
- Swap: with another field's nouns but a different structure (hospitals' self-reported annual discharges ranked against a state register, every report filed after the year closes) the reference windows already match and the task collapses to a ratio; the task needs a self-report whose reference period ends when it is submitted, checked against a register that dates every event in the full period, which is the census-register task type's own reference-period problem, not a conflict-of-interest backdrop.
- Layer 1: the register's real layout and its January refresh (program years 2021 to 2025, records keyed by Record_ID and the covered-recipient profile, NPIs blank on some records, the office's name matcher attaching some records of other physicians with the same names, payments made to the university at a faculty member's request flagged in their own column, the five remuneration natures among the others); the disclosure system's export (submission versions, amendments, a clock in UTC, lines picked from the office's entity list or typed, relationship types, a 2024 form change); the roster's effective-dated appointments, joint appointments, the July 2025 department reorganisation and employee numbers reissued in 2026; manufacturers renamed with their Open Payments IDs kept, and two listed companies that began reporting for 2025; each produced by the system named and none placed to be caught.
- Layer 2: field_method, the record-check correction: the report's reference window aligned with the register's, the amount-reporting error measured on matched reports and separated from recipiency error, each reporter's rate credibility-weighted by the number of matched reports, the weighting chosen out of sample on the last register year.
- Decisive step: comparing each disclosure with the payments the register shows made by the day it was submitted, not the register's whole year, and grossing up a disclosure submitted before the year closed by the share of the year the same manufacturer had paid by that date the year before.
- Not semantics: every field, date and code read correctly (the payment dates, the submission times in UTC, the version in force, the five remuneration natures) still leaves the analyst choosing what a disclosure is compared with, and the natural comparison, the register's program year against the disclosure's year, is the one the form's own wording ("the remuneration you received from it in the year") suggests; no column marks a disclosure as partial or a payment as missed.
- Not rule application: the policy files the docket's quantity, the fifteen places and the criterion that selects the estimate (closest on the latest published year, in total absolute difference); it names no correction, grain, window or weighting, and executing every filed rule over the corrections a strong analyst builds selects the whole-year matched-line ratio weighted by dollars.
- Analyst test: a strong analyst who conforms the register and the disclosures, reads every field correctly, builds the matched-line ratio weighted toward the faculty's and applies the filed back-test (the leak reader's route) files the whole-year docket, the five December 2026 filers left off for five faculty with censored earlier records.
- Estimand: the conflict-of-interest policy's docket article (`coi_policy_2024.pdf`): the fifteen faculty members with the largest remuneration from a single manufacturer in the calendar year just ended, remuneration being payments for services made to the faculty member personally that the manufacturer reports to Open Payments, estimated until Open Payments publishes the year from the amount each faculty member disclosed for the manufacturer, faculty members who have filed no disclosure for the year being referred to their chair and not estimated.
- No decoy: nothing is planted: no memo, prior docket, briefing or stakeholder figure ranks the faculty or argues a correction; the register and the disclosures are each what their systems produce, the December filings are a third of the faculty's habit and every faculty member's own timing, and the distractors (the research payments extract, the NIH award list and the June 2026 publication) carry no 2026 remuneration.
- No certified replay: the selection criterion is filed in general terms and the 2025 register is the register's own publication, not a certified figure built to bless a method; no estimator reproduces 2025 exactly, and the whole-year read is not blessed by it (it comes 1.32 times further from 2025 than the aligned golden).
- Lesson: a model learns that a self-report validated against a register must be compared over the window the report could cover, so a report filed before its period closed is partial and a reporter's apparent error mixes habit with timing; no build on file aligns a self-report's reference window with a register's, and the whole-year ratio is the generalist's comparison.
