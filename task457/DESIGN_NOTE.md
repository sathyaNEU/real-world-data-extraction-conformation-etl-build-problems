# task457 design note

Architecture 1, drawn 2026-10-14 from seed ET14 (an academic medical center's conflict-of-interest office choosing the physicians to review from Open Payments). The seed's world, decision and quantity are kept (the committee's fifteen cases a cycle, highest single-manufacturer remuneration first); its decoy-era traps (payments made to the university read as personal, a total counted twice across publications) are not the stump, and the pitfall is found from the data.

## Draw

- **Card:** `.claude/skills/fingerprint/cards/task457.json`, architecture v1 (guard WARN only: overuse.objective, repeat.gate_g, overuse.gate_g, answered below; task136, task185 and task80 differentiated on the card for the older same-driver signature).
- **Pairing:** Survey Research & Official Statistics / census-register-administrative-data-quality / Data Extraction & Conformation (ETL). Marrowfield University Health, an academic medical center in Ohio, takes fifteen cases to its Conflict of Interest Committee each February: the faculty members with the largest remuneration from a single manufacturer in the calendar year just ended. Two sources hold that remuneration. The faculty's annual disclosures are timely and self-reported, and they leave some of it out. The federal Open Payments register carries what the manufacturers report, but it publishes a program year only at the end of the following June. In January 2027 the register holds 2021 to 2025 and not 2026. The Office of Research Integrity and Conflicts of Interest conforms the register, the disclosure system and the human resources roster into one faculty-by-year dataset and estimates each faculty member's 2026 single-manufacturer remuneration from the 2026 disclosures. In Survey Research & Official Statistics this is a record-check problem: a timely self-report validated against an administrative register on the years both cover, each reporter's error measured there and carried to the year only the self-report covers.
- **Why not Healthcare Delivery's payment-claims task type:** Open Payments is a transparency register of manufacturers' payments, not claims; the claims pitfalls (adjudication lag, completion factors on paid claims) are not this world's, and with claims nouns the deciding quantity would be a completion factor, not a reporter's error against a register. General Statistics data integration and Physical & Environmental emissions inventory are banned against the last three builds.
- **Shape:** 01 (a ranked list under a cap: the committee's fifteen cases, highest single-manufacturer remuneration first). Provisional arithmetic: the fifteen docket places (15), the first faculty member left off and the gap to the fifteenth (2), each docket member's estimated 2026 remuneration from the manufacturer (15), the docket's chart (4) and the two files (2): about 38 criteria before the asks; stage 2 fixes which estimates are graded and at what rounding against the estimator variants they must survive.
- **Strategy:** forecast_method_backtest (F6, G16). The register measures each faculty member's disclosure completeness on 2021 to 2025, and completeness differs widely between faculty (some disclose nearly every payment, some well under half). A faculty member's own measured completeness is precise on a record of many payments and noise on a record of few, whatever the record's length in years: three small honoraria in five years, one of them forgotten, read as a third. Disclosures at face value, or all divided by the faculty-wide completeness (which changes no rank), leave the long-record under-disclosers off the docket; each disclosure divided by its author's own completeness sends faculty with thin records to the docket on a forgotten payment or two. Each faculty member's completeness is weighted between their own record and the faculty-wide figure by the volume of their record (its payments or dollars, not its years), the weighting chosen as the one whose 2025 estimates, made from 2025's disclosures and the years before, come closest to what the register published for 2025: the office's procedure files that selection criterion and no method.
- **World:** Ohio. Marrowfield University Health (fictional), its Office of Research Integrity and Conflicts of Interest, about 1,900 faculty physicians of whom about 430 receive remuneration in the register's years. The pack carries the office's register extract (Open Payments general payments for 2021 to 2025 matched to the faculty, in the register's real layout, about 100,000 records, most of them food and beverage), the disclosure system's export (annual disclosures for 2021 to 2026), the human resources roster, the register's covered-recipient profiles, the manufacturer list and the policy. The extract follows the register's January 2027 refresh; the 2026 disclosures were due on 15 January 2027 and the committee meets in February 2027. Planned: about five long-record under-disclosers join the docket under the golden and are off it at face value; about five faculty with thin records join it under their own completeness and are off the golden's.

Organisation hospital_or_health_provider, role compliance_or_audit. Personas from `guard.py names --geo "United States" --seed 457`: Karen Shea, Diana Hickman, Herbert Dean.

## Tried and rejected

- Per-payment omission with relationships that persist year to year (scratch simulation at the draw): the additive correction (the disclosure plus the record's average undisclosed dollars) won the back-test, because the record's level predicted the year better than the disclosure did; relationships that start and end within a few years make the year's disclosure the timely signal, and the credibility-weighted correction then wins.
