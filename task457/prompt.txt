Who goes on the Conflict of Interest Committee's February 2027 docket under our policy, judged on 2026 remuneration from a single manufacturer before Open Payments publishes 2026? The docket, coi_committee_docket_2027-02.docx, needs each member's manufacturer, a chart of their estimates, how many faculty owed a 2026 disclosure and how many of them, if any, had not submitted it by 11:59 p.m. Eastern on 15 January 2027, and two tables: by nature of payment, the 2025 remuneration Open Payments last published to faculty with appointments in 2025; by relationship type, the 2026 remuneration, if any, faculty disclosed from companies that do not report to Open Payments. Each faculty member's manufacturer remuneration for each year 2021 to 2026 they owed a disclosure, disclosed and as Open Payments last published it, belongs in faculty_remuneration_2026_conformed.csv. The tables' dollars are rounded to the nearest thousand.
