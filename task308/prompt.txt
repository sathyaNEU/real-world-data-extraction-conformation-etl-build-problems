Which of the four 2027 capacity offers should the committee award, and what is the award worth? Value each offer under RA-3 as the expected unserved energy it removes from the 2027 fleet at the standard's value of lost load, less its 2027 capacity payment. Put the evaluation in capacity_award_2027.xlsx with the 2027 fleet's expected unserved energy and, for every offer, the energy it removes, its payment and its net value, and write the committee paper as capacity_award_2027.docx with a chart of the four net values. The workbook also carries the 2025 figures we allocate the contract's cost on: each member's net energy delivered in 2025 and each member's 2025 12-CP average. Fleet and offer energy to the nearest 100 MWh, member energy in whole MWh, payments to USD 0.1 million, net values to the nearest USD million, 12-CP averages in MW to one decimal.
