Where among Ternmouth's container terminals should the Port Authority's six 2027 replacement ship-to-shore cranes go under the Crane Replacement Program rules, and by what margin in berth hours does that terminal lead the runner-up? The board paper is ternmouth_2027_crane_placement.xlsx: the recommendation, each terminal's berth hours the new cranes would have saved on its fiscal year 2026 large-vessel calls with a bar chart of them, and, terminal by terminal, the hours ship-to-shore cranes stood out of service for breakdowns at the terminal over the fiscal year and the maintenance labor hours spent on the cranes there, the Authority's own staff and contractors alike. With it goes ternmouth_fy2026_large_vessel_calls.csv, one row per call with its terminal, ship, IMO number, first and last container move and berth hours saved. Round each call's berth hours saved to two decimals and every other figure to whole hours.
