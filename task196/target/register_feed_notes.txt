Kelverstone Mutual: scheme register feed
Operations note, reissued 02 July 2027 by Lydia Page, underwriting and pricing

The scheme sends the mutual the whole beneficiary account register each week, not only the
accounts our members paid. The register is industry wide: an account appears on it because
the firm that holds it reported it, whichever firm's customer was scammed.

Account references are not written the same way on both sides. The register holds the sort
code without separators and the account number zero padded to eight digits. Our own
matching run writes the sort code hyphenated and drops leading zeros from the account
number. Pad the account number back to eight digits before matching anything against the
register, or the match loses every account whose number starts with a zero.

The weekly run writes one row per member payment that matched a registered account, with
the register's date for that account.
