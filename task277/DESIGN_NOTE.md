Architecture 1 in Forecasting & Predictive Modeling, drawn 2026-11-02 after the author re-tasked the number from Descriptive & Distribution Analysis on 2026-11-01 (that objective's ground exhausted, retirement not taken); no architecture was ever drawn under the old objective, the seed DA27 world is not used, and START_HERE.md keeps the range while the card, `pipeline.json` and the ledger carry the real objective. The client domain is Human Factors & Ergonomics, task type human-reliability-assessment, under the house domain Business & Operations Analytics. Kroonvaart Fulfilment runs a contract-logistics site at Venlo for one brand owner, Siersma Care, picking the drugstore and supermarket chains' store orders and the brand's webshop orders on one floor; the contract measures pick accuracy as the share of order lines that pass pack-out verification each calendar month, against 99.80 per cent. The site's operations planning manager signs the February and March 2027 accuracy forecast the client's quarterly review receives: how many lines will fail verification each month, and whether the KPI holds.

## Three rules

Task type: human-reliability-assessment (Human Factors & Ergonomics), which serves Forecasting: the forecast is a human error probability per order line, estimated from the site's own verification record and carried to the opportunities the February and March plan creates.
Domain test: the deciding quantity is the expected count of order lines failing pack-out verification each month, the site's human error probability per line times the plan's lines, built the human-reliability way from the record of the task as performed and the condition that shapes each pick (whole inners counted as packs, loose units counted out, or packs and loose units together).
Swap: "will the site hold 99.80 per cent of hospital visits in February when the megawatt-hours arrive in fours instead of sixes" has no inner pack, no loose count and no picker, so there is no pick a line can call for and nothing for the forecast to be built from; the task stops reading.
Layer 1: two WMS export layouts across the June 2026 cut-over (Dutch headers, local time and old location codes before; English, UTC and new codes after); split lines confirmed in two goes; lines confirmed at zero after a stock-out; the pack-out stations' log in UTC keyed by a prefixed order reference, with damage and labelling findings beside the KPI ones; an effective-dated item master; channel codes renamed at the cut-over; old WMS user codes against labour badges; a claims register with EDI and portal routes, EAN and article keys, delivery notes and store POs, Dutch labels; the dispatch module's cage contents ending at the cut-over. Each is what the system writes, none placed to be caught, and the call holds under every mishandling (`checks.py` SF1 to SF4, SF13, SF14, R2).
Layer 2: estimation_fit: the error probability belongs to the pick a line calls for, a relation between its quantity and the inner its article carries on the pick date; category rates fit 2026 because the categories held the same mix of picks all year, and the forward change moves the Daily store lines into the pick that fails ten times as often.
Decisive step: recognising that a line's chance of failing verification is set by what its quantity asks of the picker against the inner in force (whole inners, loose units below one inner, or whole inners with loose units counted on top), estimating each from the 2026 record, and carrying every planned line at the rate of the pick it will call for once the Daily range is held in fours.
Not semantics: every field reads plainly (a quantity, an inner, a date, a finding code, a plan line), and an analyst who reads every one correctly, follows Schedule 3 and the plan's basis line still builds category rates that validate on the second half of 2026 and file 99.83 to 99.88 per cent; no file, code or column names the pick a line calls for, so no reading flips the call.
Not rule application: no filed rule computes the forecast (Schedule 3 defines the KPI, the plan files the forward lines and mix, the PCN files the configuration); the enumerated family a competent analyst reaches for (site rate, channel and range rates, same-month rates, the inner-size cross-section, channel and quantity rates, the opened-inner condition, a per-loose-unit regression, range and quantity cells, rates by the pick each line calls for) spans 99.68 to 99.89 per cent in February, 0.21 points across the 99.80 line; the standing family the data and rules leave defensible is the same 0.21 points, because every category model fits 2026 in aggregate and passes its own half-year back-test; and the ruling-out is quiet: only a comparison of mixed picks with the other picks inside a category refutes the category models, and nothing in the pack asks for it.
Analyst test: a strong analyst who conforms both layouts and clocks, counts lines and failures exactly as Schedule 3 states, validates a channel and range model on the second half of 2026 and treats the Daily change by the record's own evidence (the inner-four range's low rate, or the opened-inner rate) files about 0.12 to 0.17 per cent of lines failing, 35 to 53 lines in February, 99.83 to 99.89 per cent and a kept target; the golden is 100 and 110 lines, 99.7 per cent in both months, the target missed.
Estimand: Schedule 3.2 fixes the outcome (order lines picked in a calendar month that pass pack-out verification; a failure is short, over or a wrong article), 3.6 the record (the site's records kept under the Schedule, all of 2026), the plan's header line the forward population (order lines per range and channel, article mix and order sizes in each month as the same month of 2026), PCN-2026-11 and the item master the forward configuration (the Daily range in inners of four from 1 February 2027); the forecast is each month's expected failing lines over the plan's lines against 99.80 per cent.
No decoy: nothing is planted: no memo, draft, briefing or stakeholder figure argues a forecast, the close-out reports only 2026 as Schedule 3 counts it, the PCN carries the client's own logistics content, and no document ranks or forecasts anything.
No certified replay: the golden reproduces no certified figure to recover a method; the close-out's monthly accuracy agrees with every route alike, so it cannot select one, and the pick-kind rates are estimated, never matched to a published value.
Lesson: a forecast built from category error rates is only as good as the stability of the task inside each category; when a forward change alters what a line asks of the picker, the rate has to be rebuilt per line from the task it will be, read from a relation between the line's quantity and a dated pack configuration that no column holds, and no build on file teaches a per-line task structure set by quantity against pack.

## Survivor anatomy

Actor: the order line; the property that decides (whole inners, loose units, or inners with loose units on top) is the relation between the line's quantity in the WMS exports and the inner its article carries on the pick date in the item master, and no file has a column for it.
Link: the comparison of each line's quantity with its article's inner in force on the pick date, through the effective-dated item master, which the field's category method never asks for and no clause invites; a key joins the files, nothing names the comparison.
Reflex: the field's own method, a human error probability per task category adjusted by shaping factors (picker tenure, channel) and carried to the plan's opportunities, sits at rung 1 and files 99.87 to 99.88 per cent, a kept target.
Back-test: the channel and range model fitted on January to June 2026 predicts each month of July to December within 2 to 12 failing lines on about 45, inside each month's chance variation, and the inner-four range shows the lowest retail rate on the site, so the record confirms the easy route.
Second use: every file the decisive step reads is in the pack for the call's ordinary work (the WMS exports and the finding log for any rate, the item master for article and range, the plan for the forward lines, the PCN for the forward configuration), and no file is there for the step alone.
Shock: the movement the step recovers is the forward change on the Daily store lines, which the record never shows; in 2026 the mixed picks sit inside the Men and Sun ranges' ordinary rates and every month runs 99.84 to 99.90 per cent, so nothing in the history stands out.
Pins: the WHAT is filed in the organisation's own genres (Schedule 3's KPI and review clauses, the plan's basis line, the PCN's configuration and sell-through dates), and no filed sentence names a pick kind, loose units, a store order unit or the comparison of quantity with inner.
