## Default path

1. Stack the two WMS pick-confirmation exports (the old layout to the June 2026 upgrade in local time, the new layout after it in UTC), one row per order line, and put every line on its Europe/Amsterdam pick date.
2. Join the pack-out exception log (UTC, keyed by order number and line number) to the lines and count each month's failing lines: 2025 and 2026 run at about 99.86 per cent, every month above 99.80.
3. Estimate the line failure probability the human-reliability way, a rate per task category (channel, range, order-quantity band) with picker tenure and zone as shaping factors, fitted on 2025 and checked on 2026, where it tracks every month within a few lines.
4. Map the client's February and March plan through the article cross-reference and carry each category's rate onto its lines; for the Daily range's new inners, take the Pro range's 2025 pack change as the analog and scale the Daily lines by the Pro range's measured rise.
Ends on: about 0.14 per cent of lines failing in each month (0.17 with the Pro range's rise carried to the Daily lines), about 100 to 120 lines a month, the 99.80 per cent KPI kept in February and March.

Golden: about 0.25 per cent of lines failing, about 170 lines a month, 99.75 per cent, the KPI missed in both months.
