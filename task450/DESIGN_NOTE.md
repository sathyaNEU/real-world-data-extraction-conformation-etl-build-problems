## Three rules

Task type: Education & Learning Sciences / item-response-theory-test-equating, serving the ETL objective (the assessment platform's item-response exports for two years in two layouts, the item bank and the vendor's key notice, the 2025 conversion, the student information system's enrollment extract and id merges, the registration crosswalk and the campus directory conformed into each campus's 2026 results on the reporting scale).
Domain test: each campus's share of tested full-academic-year students meeting the standard on the reporting scale is the quantity, and psychometrics gets it by equating the new form to the old one through the items both forms share under a non-equivalent groups design, after screening those items for a change in difficulty between the cohorts, so that the link separates the form's difficulty from the cohort's ability and no item the cohorts no longer meet alike carries it; read through last year's conversion the call designates 32 campuses, with equating that assumes equivalent cohorts 23, and linked through every shared item 13 or 6, against the golden's 15.
Swap: put a hospital in ("which wards get infection-control nurses, by the share of patients meeting a standard on a new audit form") and there are no test forms, no items shared across forms, no reporting scale and no cohorts answering both, so the call no longer reads.
Layer 1: two years of item-response exports in different layouts (2025 wide by position under the old platform, 2026 long by item id); the vendor's key correction for one 2026 item, issued after testing; omitted, not-reached and multiple-marked answers; the vendor's test-taker ids against the district's student ids through a registration crosswalk; a student information system migrated in January 2026 that renumbered campuses and gave some winter-break movers a second id, merged in August; a campus closed in June 2026 and a new campus with a single grade-5 class; the policy's full-academic-year and n-size rules. Each is produced by the district's or the vendor's systems; none is placed to be caught, and none carries the stump.
Layer 2: estimand_identification, then estimation_fit. The 2026 raw scores move with two things at once, a new form and a new cohort; only the items both forms share, answered by both cohorts, separate them. And the shared items do not all hold: the three written to TEKS 5.4F rose about 28 points while the rest rose 1.8, because the new curriculum reaches that standard a year earlier, so the link runs through the 22 whose difficulty held before any student is placed against the 300 line.
Decisive step: screening the 25 shared items for a change in difficulty between the cohorts (the change in Rasch difficulty, Mantel-Haenszel, the delta plot or the change in share correct all set aside the same three) and linking the 2026 form to the 2025 reporting scale through the 22 that held under non-equivalent cohorts (any common-item equating), which puts the passing raw score on the new form at 25; nothing filed names the shared items' role, a screen, the three items, a linking method, a cohort difference or a passing raw score for 2026.
Not semantics: every field read right (each response scored with the corrected key, every test placed with its student and campus, the 2025 conversion read as the 2025 form's, the policy's rules executed) still leaves the 2026 form's relation to the scale unknown; the item bank says which items both forms carry, never that three of them stopped meaning the same thing, and the common-item link through all of them, the textbook route, designates 13 or 6 campuses.
Not rule application: the policy files the WHAT (the reporting scale set on the 2025 form, the standard at 300, full-academic-year students, campuses as organised for 2026-27, the 40 per cent line) and the technical report the 2025 form's conversion; no filed rule or procedure links a new form to the scale or screens the items that would link it, so executing every stated rule still leaves both steps open, and the default path executes every one of them.
Analyst test: a strong analyst who scores every response with the corrected key, places every test through the crosswalk and the enrollment extract, applies every policy rule and, seeing that the conversion belongs to the 2025 form and that the cohorts differ on the shared items, links the 2026 form through all 25 shared items designates 13 campuses (passing raw score 24, Tucker, Braun-Holland, chained linear) or 6 (23, Levine, frequency estimation, chained equipercentile, Rasch); one who matches the raw-score distributions designates 23, and one who applies the 2025 conversion as published designates 32.
Estimand: the district's designation policy: a student meets the standard when the student's scale score on the reporting scale set on the 2025 grade-5 form is 300 or above (later forms report on the same scale); a campus's share is its tested full-academic-year students meeting the standard over its tested full-academic-year students, campuses rated as organised for 2026-27 and with ten or more such students; a rated elementary campus under 40 per cent is designated.
No decoy: no document, memo, figure or stakeholder carries a 2026 conversion, a passing raw score, a designated set, a list of items or a screen; the 2025 conversion is the 2025 form's own, published in 2025 for that form; the three items' change is the 2026 students' own answers under a curriculum that teaches their standard earlier, not a defect in any record, and every other shared item moved with the cohort.
No certified replay: nothing certified or published for 2026 is reproduced; the 2025 conversion answers 2025 and ties under every handling of 2026.
Lesson: a score on a new form means nothing on the old scale until the form is linked through what both cohorts answered, and an item both cohorts answered links them only if it means the same thing to both; last year's conversion credits the cohort's gain to nobody, matching distributions credits it to the form, and a link through every shared item credits one standard's curriculum gain to the whole cohort; no build on file teaches the screen.
