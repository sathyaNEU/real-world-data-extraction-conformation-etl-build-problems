## Default path

1. Score each 2026 response with the 2026 key (the item bank's key, with its correction), sum each student's raw score on the 2026 form, and keep the tested students of each elementary school in the district's roster.
2. Convert each raw score to the reporting scale with the 2025 raw-to-scale table, the district's only published conversion, and count a student proficient at 300 or above.
3. Rate each school as its proficient students over its tested students, and designate the schools under 35 per cent.

Ends on: the wrong set of schools, more than the golden's (fixed at design).
Golden: the schools under 35 per cent once the 2026 form is linked to the 2025 scale through the fifteen shared items (fixed at design).

## Stump sentence

The default path designates too many schools because step 2 reads the 2026 raw scores through the 2025 table as if the two forms were the same, so the harder 2026 form costs every student about two raw points and the passing raw score stays at the 2025 form's; equating that matches the 2026 score distribution to 2025's does no better, because it books the stronger cohort's real gain as an easier form; linked through the items both forms share, the passing raw score on the 2026 form is lower and fewer schools fall under the line (schools and figures fixed at design).

## Three rules

Task type: Education & Learning Sciences / item-response-theory-test-equating, serving the ETL objective (the assessment platform's item-response exports for two years in two layouts, the item bank, the 2025 conversion table, the district roster and school directory conformed into each school's 2026 results on the reporting scale).
Domain test: each school's proficiency rate on the reporting scale is the quantity, and psychometrics gets it by equating the new form to the old one through the anchor items under a non-equivalent groups design, separating the form's difficulty from the cohort's ability; read through last year's table the call designates too many schools, and with equating that assumes equivalent cohorts it still does (fixed at design).
Swap: put a hospital in ("which wards get infection-control nurses, by the share of patients meeting a standard on a new audit form") and there are no test forms, no items shared across forms, no reporting scale and no cohorts answering both, so the call no longer reads.
Layer 1: two years of item-response exports in different layouts (2025 wide by item position, 2026 long by item id); a scoring-key correction issued after testing for one item; omitted and not-reached responses; the vendor's student ids against the district's ids through a crosswalk; schools merged and renumbered between years; make-up administrations on a parallel form and invalidated sessions; the district's accountability policy with its n-size and full-academic-year rules (inventory at stage 3).
Layer 2: estimand_identification. The 2026 raw scores move with two things at once, a new form and a new cohort; only the fifteen items both forms share, answered by both cohorts, separate them, so the new form is linked to the 2025 scale through those items before any student is placed against the 300 line.
Decisive step: linking the 2026 form to the 2025 reporting scale through the shared items (any common-item equating), which puts the passing raw score on the new form below the 2025 form's; nothing filed names the shared items' role, a linking method or a passing raw score for 2026.
Not semantics: every field read right (each response scored with the corrected key, every student matched to a school, the 2025 table read correctly) still leaves the 2026 form's relation to the scale unknown, and reading it through the 2025 table or matching the two years' score distributions designates the wrong schools.
Not rule application: the accountability policy files the WHAT (the reporting scale set in 2025, proficient at 300, a school under 35 per cent of its tested students designated) and the technical report files the forms' composition; no filed rule or procedure links a new form to the scale, so executing every stated rule still leaves the step open, and the default path executes every one of them.
Analyst test: a strong analyst who scores every response with the corrected key, matches every student through the crosswalk, applies every filed rule and converts the 2026 raw scores with the 2025 table, or equates by matching the 2026 distribution to 2025's, designates the wrong set of schools, more than the golden (fixed at design).
Estimand: the district's accountability policy: a student is proficient when the student's 2026 score on the reporting scale the district set in 2025 is 300 or above; a school's rate is its proficient full-academic-year students over its tested full-academic-year students; a school is designated when its rate is under 35 per cent, schools with fewer than ten tested students not rated.
No decoy: no document, memo, figure or stakeholder carries a 2026 conversion, a passing raw score or the wrong set; the 2025 table is the 2025 form's own, published in 2025 for that form.
No certified replay: nothing certified or published for 2026 is reproduced; the 2025 published school results answer 2025 and tie under every handling of 2026.
Lesson: a score on a new form means nothing on the old scale until the form is linked through what both cohorts answered; last year's table credits the cohort's gain to nobody and matching distributions credits it to the form; no build on file teaches it.
