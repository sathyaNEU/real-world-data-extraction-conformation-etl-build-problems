## Three rules

Task type: Education & Learning Sciences, `multilevel-classroom-school-research`, whose objective code in
the taxonomy is Root-Cause Analysis and nothing else; the call is the cause of a move in a progress measure
with the rivals ruled out on evidence.
Domain test: the deciding quantity is the contribution the move from mixed-attainment to setted teaching
groups makes to a fall in a progress measure defined against each pupil's own prior attainment, which the
field estimates by centring prior attainment within the teaching group so the within-pupil gradient and the
group-composition gradient are separated.
Swap: **this line fails, and it is the half that decides.** Put a hospital's wards and case severity in
("which change carried the fall in the trust's risk-adjusted outcome: the new care pathway rolled out to
some wards, or the move from mixed-acuity to acuity-segregated wards") and the sentence reads identically,
because a group-mean contextual effect is general machinery that any nested record satisfies. The only half
that holds is the measure's definition, a progress score against each pupil's own earlier assessment, which
is education's and not a hospital's; the deciding mechanism is not.
Layer 1: a school-year calendar against the trust's financial year, mid-year joiners and leavers, a changed
assessment scale across the two years, teaching-group reorganisation at the end of the autumn term, an
imperfect pupil key across the management information system and the assessment platform. Each is a named
system's own artefact and none carries the stump.
Layer 2: `estimand_identification`, library pattern
`education-learning-sciences/multilevel-classroom-school-research/2`.
Decisive step: centre prior attainment within the teaching group, carry the group's mean prior attainment as
its own term, and read the trust's fall against the change in the distribution of those group means.
Not semantics: reading every field, code, unit and definition correctly (the progress measure's definition,
the prior attainment scale, the teaching-group identifier, the two years' sittings) still leaves the pooled
coefficient conflating the two gradients, and no column holds a teaching group's mean prior attainment or
flags the setting policy, so the easy route files the mastery scheme at 0.0402 to 0.0992 points with every
field read right.
Not rule application: **this line fails.** No filed rule names the method, which is the half the line is
usually answered against, but the question is what is left to decide once every filed rule is read, and
what is left is the SHAPE of the peer term, which is a free method choice. Measured on the clean truth over
30 worlds: the five licensed forms a competent analyst would use spread the graded figure 179.89 bins at
the design's own setting and 85.72 to 229.68 bins across the dial, against a separation of 22.75 bins, and
they do not agree on the call either, the linear form (the library pattern exactly as the card states it)
naming the re-sorting in 5 or 6 of 30 worlds against the quadratic form's 30 of 30. Narrowed to the two
forms the pattern itself names, the spread is 22.72 bins against a separation of 22.75, a ratio of 0.999.
Analyst test: a strong analyst who cleans every record and reads every definition correctly runs step 2 and
step 3 of the default path and names the mastery scheme of work, the `Ends on:` figure, at 0.0402 to 0.0992
progress points.
Estimand: the contribution of each named change to the fall in the trust's Year 11 cohorts' mean progress
score between the two years, in progress points per pupil, over the pupils on roll at the January census in
each year.
No decoy: nothing is planted to be caught; no memo argues a cause, no shipped artifact ranks the changes.
No certified replay: no certified or published figure carries a cause or a group-level composition figure.
Lesson: refused. The lesson this draw would teach, that a group-relative outcome measure can fall because
members were re-sorted across groups rather than because anything was done to them, cannot be taught here,
because the step that recovers it is a specification extension and its own licensed family disagrees with
it by as much as it disagrees with the easy route.
