## 1. Final Recommendation

**Surcharge CR113, CR118, CR131, CR149, CR167 and CR176 from 1 January 2027.**
Their expected 2027 retained claims exceed the claims components they elected; no other employer's do. Most member employers are small, and a small employer's three years carry the luck of a few members' claims, so each employer's trended experience is weighed against the trust's by its credibility. Read at face value, the experience would surcharge CR104, CR109 and CR136, whose experience was raised by a few large claims, and spare CR113, CR118, CR131 and CR149, whose experience was low by luck and whose claims components sit well under the trust's level.

## 3. Step-by-Step Solution

1. Joined the two claims lag reports by employer, month of service, paid month and category, the May 2026 cells both platforms sent counted once, cents in dollars.
2. For each employer and plan year 2023 to 2025 took claims incurred in the year and paid through March of the next, less Westerby's reimbursements (the employer through the policy schedule), over the year's member months, trended at 7 per cent a year to 2027.
3. Estimated the within-employer and between-employer variance of those trended PMPMs (Buhlmann-Straub, member-month weights) and gave each employer a credibility of its member months over its member months plus their ratio.
4. Took each employer's expected 2027 PMPM as its credibility times its own three-year trended PMPM plus the rest times the trust's member-month weighted level; limited-fluctuation credibility at 1,000 to 3,000 lives gives the same employers.
5. Compared with the claims component of the elected 2027 contribution: CR113, CR118, CR131, CR149, CR167, CR176 over.
6. Ask A: July to September 2026 eligibility counted once per person a month (members reissued new ids at the summer 2026 move matched on employer, birth date, sex and relationship).
7. Ask B: each member's latest row on the 2026 high-cost claimant report, a member's rows under the old and the new id joined as one member.
8. Recommended surcharges for CR113, CR118, CR131, CR149, CR167, CR176.
