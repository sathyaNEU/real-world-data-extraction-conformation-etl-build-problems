## Three rules

Domain test: the call turns on experienced trip-time reliability, the transport network analysis that composes a vehicle's journey from segment travel times read at the moment the vehicle reaches each segment as the morning queue builds, and sets a timetable departure from the share of days it arrives on time; with another field's nouns there is no probe segment, no period a vehicle reaches, no queue spilling back through the segments ahead of it, and the task stops reading the same.
Analyst test: an analyst who reads the probe export, the TMC table, the route file and the standard correctly (the passenger class, the sixteen segments in order, the fixed legs, the working weekdays) and computes each day's trip as the sum of the segments' travel times for the departure's five-minute period files 7:15 a.m.; the reflexes on top of it (dropping incident days as non-recurrent: 7:15; reading the whole route at the trip's midpoint: 7:10; the 90th-percentile time-of-day profile read at departure: 7:10, or walked: 7:00) never file the 7:05 that only taking each segment at the period the shuttle reaches it, day by day, gives. `stump_class: analytical`.
Layer 1: twelve monthly probe exports with three vehicle classes in one file, a density flag, speed and reference-speed columns beside travel time, all days of the week and every hour, epochs in local time across both daylight-saving changes; a TMC identification table carrying the 2025 and 2026 network releases; route files whose freeway legs join and leave part-way along a segment, so each carries fixed access legs; the HR holiday calendars for the evidence year and for 2027; a parking reassignment workbook built from two sources under two identifier schemes; an HR extract holding scheduled start as an integer HHMM; the operator's 2027 schedule of charges; distractors (a state traffic-count export, the transit authority's express-route timetable, the 2026 staff commute survey). None of it sits on the main call's rows in a way that can move the call (`## Closure table`, axes 15, 16 and 19); the reassignment workbook, the HR extract, the operator's charges and the deadhead route sit on the ask paths only.
Layer 2: the estimand is the share of days a departure's trip arrives in time, and the easy constructions build a trip no vehicle made (every segment at the departure period, the whole route at the trip's midpoint, a profile of per-period percentiles read at departure or walked), where the trip has to be composed day by day by taking each segment at the period the shuttle reaches it. A transport analyst recognises the departure-period sum as the instantaneous travel time, which the reliability literature distinguishes from the experienced travel time precisely because conditions change while the trip is under way.

## Strongest-solver test

The most careful path: the solver parses the twelve exports, keeps the passenger class on the sixteen segments, checks the working weekdays, notices that the corridor slows sharply between 7:00 and 7:45 and that a trip leaving at 7:15 spends half an hour on the road, and either reads the route at the departure period (7:15), or shifts the reading to the trip's middle (7:10), or uses a 90th-percentile profile, read at departure (7:10) or walked (7:00); each is a corridor index or a trip assembled from different days. It still files the wrong time because only composing each day's own trip, segment after segment at the period the shuttle reaches it, measures what a departure would have met that day. What the build cannot close: a solver who simulates each day's trip by entering each segment at the period it reaches it lands the call; the portal measures how often. ## Determinism

The construction: a shuttle leaving the boarding bay at time t reaches the first segment at t plus the fixed lot leg; for each segment in the route file's order it takes the passenger travel time recorded for that segment in the five-minute period containing the moment it enters, and enters the next segment at the moment it leaves this one; after the sixteenth it adds the fixed stop leg. The day is on time when the arrival is at or before 8:00:00. The departure is the latest on the grid whose on-time days are at least 227 of 252. The estimator is unique once the trip is defined; the variants a literal reader could choose where the vendor dictionary is silent (the period at a segment's midpoint, interpolation between periods) are asserted to give the same call and the same counts at 7:05 and 7:10.
