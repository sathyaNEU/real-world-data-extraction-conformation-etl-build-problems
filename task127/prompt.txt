How much credit write-off can the business support unit keep off Ruahine Bank's books through the watch-list business borrowers it takes on within its 2027 envelope? That choice and its total should head a workbook with a line for every watch-list borrower giving the 2027 write-off its package would keep off, the value at 31 December 2026 of the property we hold as security for its lending and its total commitment to us at that date, and a second sheet giving each sector's share of business borrowers performing at the end of 2024 that defaulted in 2025, saved as bsu_intake_2027.xlsx. A one-page note for the credit committee, bsu_intake_2027.docx, carries the reasoning. Write-offs are in New Zealand dollars to the nearest 50,000, other amounts to the nearest thousand, and shares in percent to one decimal.
