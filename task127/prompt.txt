Which two of our watch-list business borrowers should the business support unit take on in 2027 to keep the most credit write-off off Fernmark Bank's books? Every watch-list borrower needs a line with the 2027 write-off its package would prevent, under the two names and the total they keep off the books, in a workbook called bsu_intake_2027.xlsx, and a second sheet there should give, for each of those borrowers and each month of 2026, the money that reached its accounts from anyone other than itself. Alongside it, bsu_intake_2027.md is a one-page note for the credit committee with the recommendation and its reasoning in plain words. Dollar amounts are in New Zealand dollars rounded to the nearest thousand.
