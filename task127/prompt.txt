Where should the winter discharge fund's 1,200 discharge-to-assess packages go among Marchshire's acute hospitals for December 2026 to February 2027, judged on the occupied bed-days each hospital's share would free? The board will check the split in d2a_package_split_winter_2026-27.xlsx, so it should show every hospital's packages and bed-days, the total bed-days, and any hospital left with none, together with how quickly the brokerage placed home care last winter: for each of the council's districts and each month from December 2025 to February 2026, the referrals it took whose care started no more than seven days after referral. The delays behind it belong in a chart, delayed_bed_days_winter_2025-26.svg, giving for each hospital and each of those months the occupied bed-days lost to delayed discharge of patients aged 65 and over on any pathway and how many patients were delayed, with every bar showing its value. Packages, referrals, patients and bed-days are whole numbers.
