How many of the fund's 1,800 heat-pump rebate slots can we place in 2027, and with which of our six co-ops? The trustees settle that on 17 December, two weeks before programme year 2 opens. Sara Duncan, who leads our outreach, is certain Uplands has more qualifying homes than any other co-op. It is my programme, so the call is mine to make. Give me the slots each co-op receives in 2027, in tens, and how many of the 1,800 we leave unallocated, as one split I can defend line by line.

Three files. The trustees read `slot_split_board_note_2027.docx`, which opens on that split and then names the co-op taking the most slots and how many slots it is ahead of the next.

Finance loads the same split from `coop_slot_split_2027.csv`, one row per co-op plus a total row that matches the note. Beside each co-op's 2027 slots, give me what the 2026 pilot cost us there: the average installed cost per rebated install, the rebate as a share of that cost to one decimal place, and the rebate dollars paid through the end of November with the number of pilot installs they cover. Dollars are whole dollars.

That split goes on the screen as `coop_slots_2027.svg`, a pair of bars per co-op, 2027 slots beside 2026 pilot installs, largest allocation first, every bar labelled with its value, the unallocated slots as a bar of their own, and the total we place in 2027 in the title.
