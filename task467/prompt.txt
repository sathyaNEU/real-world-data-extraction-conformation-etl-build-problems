How much must Wrenfield & Holt pay into its customer goodwill fund under the written assurance it gave Kesterfield Trading Standards, and in which months from January to June 2026 did it break the commitment on refund times? Kesterfield wants the return by 30 November in goodwill_fund_return_h1_2026.csv, one row per month with the refunds made, how many were made more than 14 days after the returned goods were delivered to the Cotterby returns centre, that number as a share of the month's refunds and whether the month met the commitment; also send refund_timeliness_note.pdf stating the payment and the breach months, with a chart of each month's share against the commitment line. Refunds are whole refunds, shares are per cents to one decimal and the payment is to the nearest 10 pounds.
