# task266 design note

Architecture 1, drawn 2026-10-17 (wave 3, Descriptive lane, sathay3012). Seed DA16's forced-sale world was worked at the draw and set aside (`## Tried and rejected`); the slot draws a new world in the same objective. Wexmoor County Community College, a public community college in Pennsylvania with three campuses (Wexmoor, Fenstow and Hollenby), reviews its associate-degree programs each June. Its Program Review Policy sends a program into review (an improvement plan, and a sunset vote two years later if it does not recover) when fewer than 30 per cent of its entering students earn the program's award within three years of entering. The share is reported for the students who entered in fall 2023, fall 2024 and fall 2025, including those still enrolled, from the student record as it stands after the spring 2027 term. The board of trustees votes the review list at its June 2027 meeting. The call is forward facing: most of the entrants it covers are still within their three years, and the review it starts runs from 2027-28.

## Default path

1. Read the policy: a program enters review when fewer than 30 per cent of its entering students earn the program's award within three years of entering, reported for the fall 2023, 2024 and 2025 entering students, those still enrolled included.
2. Stack the two student-system exports (the legacy system to summer 2024, the new one from fall 2024), keep one record per entry and place each student in the program entered.
3. For each entering student, the number of terms to the award, or censored at the term of a transfer out, a withdrawal, or the spring 2027 census.
4. Fit Kaplan-Meier for each program and read the share with the award at six terms (three years).
5. Programs below 30 per cent enter review.

Ends on: one program enters review (Medical Assisting at about 27 per cent); the four transfer-oriented programs stand at about 34 to 39 per cent (design targets, replaced by the built figures at stage 3).
Golden: six programs enter review, the four transfer-oriented programs at about 18 to 24 per cent with Medical Assisting and Early Childhood Education (design targets).

## Stump sentence

The default path sends one program into review, keeping the four transfer-oriented programs at about 34 to 39 per cent, because it treats a student who transferred out or withdrew as censored, as if the college had lost sight of a student who might still earn its award, so each of those students keeps a share of the award probability; counting transfer and withdrawal as endings that compete with the award, with only the students still enrolled censored, puts the four transfer-oriented programs at about 18 to 24 per cent and sends six programs into review.

## Three rules

Task type: event-history-life-course-analysis (Sociology & Demography), which serves Descriptive & Distribution Analysis in the taxonomy; the setting is a community college's program review.
Domain test: the deciding quantity is each program's three-year completion share, the cumulative incidence of the award among its entering students with transfer out and withdrawal as competing exits and students still enrolled censored, the event-history estimator for one transition among several from a life-course state; replacing it with a survival curve of the award that censors the other exits, or with the share of entrants already awarded, changes which programs enter review.
Swap: "how many of a warehouse's pallet lanes fall below 30 per cent of pallets shipped within three days" keeps a share and a floor, but loses the student's three recorded ways out of college, the transfer that ends the college's chance of conferring its award, and the entering classes still enrolled at the census, so the competing exits the call turns on have nothing to attach to and the task no longer reads the same.
Layer 1: the student record in two exports from two student information systems (the 2024 migration), with different term codes; students who changed program after entry, kept in the program entered as the policy reports; transfers out found by the state transfer exchange's quarterly match, with the term only; withdrawals coded by the registrar after two consecutive terms with no enrolment; awards conferred at term end in a separate graduation file; returning students re-admitted under a new student number. Each is how a college's systems hold their records; nothing is placed to be caught.
Layer 2: estimand_identification (competing risks): the share the policy asks for is the probability of the award among several exits, and the familiar survival curve of the award returns a different quantity (the award's probability in a world without transfer or withdrawal); the choice moves every program's figure and flips the transfer-oriented programs.
Decisive step: the cumulative incidence (Aalen-Johansen) of the award with transfer and withdrawal as competing events and only open enrolments censored, in place of one minus a Kaplan-Meier curve that censors the competing exits.
Not semantics: an analyst who reads every status code correctly, including the registrar's note that a withdrawal is recorded after two consecutive terms without enrolment and the exchange's note that a transfer is an enrolment at another institution, still has a correct date and status for every student and still runs the standard survival curve of the award, which censoring the other exits makes easy; nothing in a field is misread on that route, the error is in what the curve estimates.
Not rule application: the policy files the estimand (the share of entering students who earn the award within three years, the three entering classes, students still enrolled included, the 30 per cent floor); no filed rule says how to estimate the share for students whose three years are not over, and executing every filed rule on the default path files the one-program list.
Analyst test: a strong analyst who reconciles the two exports, places each student in the program entered, reads every code and censors students still enrolled at the census runs Kaplan-Meier on time to the award with transfers and withdrawals censored too and ends on the default path's one program, Medical Assisting at about 27 per cent, with the transfer-oriented programs above the floor, because a censored transfer keeps a share of an award the student can no longer earn.
Estimand: the Program Review Policy (section 3, shipped as the college's policy document) files the population (every student who entered the program in fall 2023, 2024 or 2025, those still enrolled included), the outcome (the program's award), the window (three years, six fall and spring terms, from entry), the unit (the entering student) and the comparison (30 per cent).
No decoy: no memo, prior review, dashboard or stakeholder figure gives a program's completion share; the program register lists programs, credentials and campuses only; no file argues for or against a program.
No certified replay: the method is not hidden behind published figures; no shipped file states a completion share for the review's entering classes, and the policy files the quantity, leaving the estimator to the analysis.
Lesson: when a state can be left in several ways and some spells are still open, the share leaving one particular way is not one minus a survival curve that treats the other ways out as lost sight of; no build on file teaches competing exits (task265 teaches late entry into the risk set, task157 the development of an open window).
