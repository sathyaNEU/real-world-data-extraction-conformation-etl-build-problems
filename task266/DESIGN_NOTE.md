# task266 design note

Architecture 1, drawn 2026-10-17 (wave 3, Descriptive lane, sathay3012). Seed DA16's forced-sale world was worked at the draw and set aside (`## Tried and rejected`); the slot draws a new world in the same objective. Quenlow County Community College, a public community college in Pennsylvania with three campuses (Quenlow, Tarvelly and Hollenby), reviews its associate-degree programs each June. Its Program Review Policy sends a program into review (an improvement plan, and a sunset vote two years later if it does not recover) when fewer than 30 per cent of its entering students earn an associate degree within three years of entering. The share is reported for the students who entered in fall 2023, fall 2024 and fall 2025, including those still enrolled, from the student record as it stands after the spring 2027 term. The board of trustees votes the review list at its June 2027 meeting. The call is forward facing: two of the three entering classes are still inside their three years at the extract, and the review it starts runs from 2027-28.

## Default path

1. Read the policy: a program enters review when fewer than 30 per cent of its entering students (the Office of Institutional Research's fall 2023, 2024 and 2025 entering files, those whose ninth term is still to come counted in full, their part of the share an estimate) earn an associate degree from the college within nine terms of entering, summer terms included.
2. Stack IR's three entering files, carry the fall 2023 students' legacy numbers to the new student system through the conversion map, and decode both term-code schemes with the term calendar.
3. Join each entering student's admission to the admissions extract (exit term and exit reason) and to the conferral file: the term of the associate degree, or censored at the term of a transfer out, a withdrawal or the end of spring 2027.
4. Fit Kaplan-Meier on terms to the award for each program and read one minus the curve at nine terms.
5. Programs below 30 per cent enter review.

Ends on: one program enters review, Early Childhood Education at 22.2 per cent; the four transfer-oriented programs stand at 34.8 to 41.4 per cent and Medical Assisting at 38.1 (built).
Golden: six programs enter review, the four transfer-oriented programs at 20.9 to 25.9 per cent with Medical Assisting (26.2) and Early Childhood Education (19.5) (built).

## Stump sentence

The default path sends one program into review, Early Childhood Education at 22.2 per cent, and keeps the four transfer-oriented programs (34.8 to 41.4 per cent) and Medical Assisting (38.1) above the floor, because it treats an admission that ended in a transfer out or a withdrawal as censored, as if the college had lost sight of a student who might still earn its award, so each of those students keeps a share of the award probability; counting transfer and withdrawal as endings that compete with the award, with only the admissions still open at the extract censored, puts the transfer-oriented programs at 20.9 to 25.9 per cent and Medical Assisting at 26.2, and sends six programs into review.

## Three rules

Task type: event-history-life-course-analysis (Sociology & Demography), which serves Descriptive & Distribution Analysis in the taxonomy; the setting is a community college's program review.
Domain test: the deciding quantity is each program's three-year completion share, the cumulative incidence of the award among its entering students with transfer out and withdrawal as competing exits and admissions still open censored, the event-history estimator for one transition among several from a life-course state; replacing it with a survival curve of the award that censors the other exits, or with the share of entrants already awarded, changes which programs enter review.
Swap: "how many of a warehouse's pallet lanes fall below 30 per cent of pallets shipped within three days" keeps a share and a floor, but loses the student's three recorded ways out of an admission, the transfer that ends the college's chance of conferring its award, and the entering classes still inside their window at the extract, so the competing exits the call turns on have nothing to attach to and the task no longer reads the same.
Layer 1: the student record held in two student systems (the 2024 conversion): IR froze the fall 2023 entering file in the legacy system under its student numbers and term codes, and the later files and every extract use the new system's, joined through the conversion map; program changes after entry (the policy reports the program entered); transfers out found by the state transfer exchange's quarterly match, an admission first closed as a withdrawal and recoded when the match arrives; a withdrawal recorded at the census of the first fall or spring term without enrolment, a returning student readmitted under a new admission; associate degrees conferred at term ends in a separate conferral file; deaths removed from the class. Each is how a college's systems hold their records; nothing is placed to be caught.
Layer 2: estimand_identification (competing risks): the share the policy asks for is the probability of the award among several exits, and the familiar survival curve of the award returns a different quantity (the award's probability in a world without transfer or withdrawal); the choice moves every program's figure and flips five programs.
Decisive step: the cumulative incidence (Aalen-Johansen) of the award with transfer and withdrawal as competing events and only open admissions censored, in place of one minus a Kaplan-Meier curve that censors the competing exits.
Not semantics: an analyst who reads every exit reason correctly, including the student system's own labels beside each code (Graduated, Transferred Out, Withdrawn, Deceased) and the exit term each admission closed in, still has a correct term and reason for every admission and still runs the standard survival curve of the award, which censoring the other exits makes easy; nothing in a field is misread on that route, the error is in what the curve estimates.
Not rule application: the policy files the estimand (the students in IR's three fall entering files, the share for the students whose ninth term is still to come an estimate of how many will have earned the degree by then, an associate degree under the admission at entry within nine terms, the 30 per cent floor); no filed rule says how to make that estimate, and executing every filed rule on the default path files the one-program list.
Analyst test: a strong analyst who joins IR's three entering files to the new system through the conversion map, places each student in the program entered, reads every exit reason correctly and censors the admissions still open at the extract runs Kaplan-Meier on terms to the award with transfers and withdrawals censored too, and ends on the default path's one program, Early Childhood Education at 22.2 per cent, with the transfer-oriented programs and Medical Assisting above the floor, because a censored transfer keeps a share of an award the student can no longer earn under that admission.
Estimand: the Program Review Policy (section 3) files the population (every student in the Office of Institutional Research's entering files for the second, third and fourth falls before the review, fall 2023, 2024 and 2025 for June 2027, students who die removed), the outcome (an associate degree from the college, under the admission recorded at entry), the window (nine terms counting the entering fall, summer terms included), the treatment of the classes still inside it (counted in full, their part of the share an estimate, made from the record at the end of the spring before the review, of how many will have earned the degree by the end of their ninth term), the unit (the entering student) and the comparison (fewer than 30 per cent).
No decoy: no memo, prior review, dashboard or stakeholder figure gives a program's completion share; the program register lists programs, credentials, campuses and the subjects each program owns; no file argues for or against a program.
No certified replay: the method is not hidden behind published figures; no shipped file states a completion share for the review's entering classes, and the policy files the quantity, leaving the estimator to the analysis.
Lesson: when a state can be left in several ways and some spells are still open, the share leaving one particular way is not one minus a survival curve that treats the other ways out as lost sight of; no build on file teaches competing exits (task265 teaches late entry into the risk set, task157 the development of an open window).

## Literal reader

Executed word for word, the policy sends a program into review when fewer than 30 per cent of the students in IR's entering files for the second, third and fourth falls before the June 2027 review (fall 2023, 2024 and 2025) who entered it earn an associate degree from the college within nine terms of entering, followed under the admission recorded at entry; the classes whose ninth term is still to come are counted in full, their part of the share an estimate of how many will have earned the degree by the end of their ninth term, made from the record at the end of spring 2027 with that term's degrees, for the program's three classes taken together, each student counting in full for every term the record follows. The admissions extract closes each admission in an exit term with Graduated, Transferred Out or Withdrawn (or Deceased), or leaves it open at the extract. The one estimate of how many will have earned the degree that the record supports counts, term by term, the degree's share of the endings among the admissions still open, with open admissions the only censored cases, which gives the golden. Degrees to date over entrants (R0) is not an estimate of the share by the ninth term, it counts the open classes as non-completers against 3.3; dropping the open admissions (R0') leaves out students 3.3 counts in full; the fall 2023 class alone (R2) is not the three classes taken together; SF4, SF6, SF7, SF8, SF9, SF13, SF14 and SF16 converge; and R1 is an estimate of another quantity (the degree's share in a world without transfers or withdrawals), not another reading of the estimand. Round 1 of the leak reader read the first form of 3.3 ("those still enrolled included, from the student record as it stands") as degrees to date over entrants; the filed estimate closes it.
