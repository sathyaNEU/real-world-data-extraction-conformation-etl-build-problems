What horizon should the Former Asbestos Workers Review Program run to for the term from 1 July 2027? Our board sets it under the new program terms, and the recommendation goes in review_horizon_board_paper_2027.docx with a chart of the share of our workforce's mesotheliomas diagnosed by each number of years after first exposure, every setting from 40 to 70 years marked.
