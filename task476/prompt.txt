What stands should Cinder Fork Timber Company mark for its 2027 salvage program under its salvage policy? Reconcile the 2021 and 2026 permanent plot tallies, the plot register and the stand table into tree_fates_2021_2026.csv, one row for every tree tallied live in 2021 with its plot, stand and 2026 fate, and write salvage_marking_2027.pdf for the board naming the stands to mark, with each stand's 2021 to 2026 mortality as a share of its 2021 live basal area, its 2026 live basal area per acre and its 2026 Scribner board-foot volume per acre. Basal areas go to one decimal of a square foot per acre, shares to one decimal of a per cent and volumes to the nearest 10 board feet.
