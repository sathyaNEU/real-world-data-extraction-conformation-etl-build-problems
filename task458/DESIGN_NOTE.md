# task458 design note

Architecture 1, drawn 2026-10-15 from seed ET15 (behavioral-health workforce designation from NPPES monthly snapshots and weekly deltas). The seed's world, decision and quantity are kept: a Medicaid program designates counties for a behavioral-health prescriber pilot on workforce ratios, with the provider master rebuilt from the NPPES monthly file and its weekly incrementals. Its decoy-era traps (retired clinicians and mail-only addresses in the count) are Layer 1 here, and the pitfall is found in how the ratios' need populations are counted.

## Default path

1. Build the prescriber master: the October 2026 NPPES file updated record by record by the three weekly incremental files, deactivated NPIs dropped (reactivations kept), behavioral-health prescriber taxonomies, practice location county on 1 November 2026, and at least one service in the contract year in the claims.
2. Build the adult members: eligibility spells in the contract year joined into persons by person number, county of residence and eligibility group on the last enrolled day.
3. Count the adult members with a serious mental illness and with an opioid use disorder as those whose final claim lines in the contract year carry a qualifying diagnosis code.
4. Compute each county's three standards (prescribers per 1,000 members with each condition, the share of members with a serious mental illness seen by a behavioral-health prescriber), compare with NP-31's thresholds and designate the counties failing two or more.

Ends on: the two counties short of prescribers outright (Douglas, Okanogan), the farm counties clearing every standard.
Golden: Douglas and Okanogan with the farm counties whose recorded need falls short of their members' (Adams, Franklin, Grant, and the further farm counties the build fixes), every farm county failing the population standards once need is measured on members enrolled all year and carried to every member by eligibility group.

## Stump sentence

The default path files only Douglas and Okanogan because step 3 counts the members whose claims record a qualifying diagnosis, and a member's serious mental illness or opioid use disorder reaches the claims only through a service billed while they are enrolled: the farm counties' expansion adults churn through spells of one to four months, so many who have a condition were never billed for it during their spell, the farm counties' need reads low and their prescribers per member in need and their share seen read high; measured on the members enrolled all year, by eligibility group, and carried to every member, the farm counties fail the population standards and qualify.

## Three rules

- Task type: Healthcare Delivery & Health Economics, payment-claims-health-plan-analytics (claims-based case identification against enrollment: a condition's prevalence among enrollees measured where the claims had time to record it, ratios over a contract year's members), which serves Data Extraction & Conformation.
- Domain test: the quantity that decides is each county's number of adult members with a serious mental illness and with an opioid use disorder, estimated the way health-plan analysts estimate a chronic condition's prevalence from claims: on members enrolled long enough for the condition to appear in billed services, carried to every member by eligibility group; the recorded count, the continuously enrolled count and the pooled recorded rate each change the designation.
- Swap: with retail nouns (shoppers with a standing preference recorded only when they buy during a loyalty spell) the trait has no clinical meaning, no eligibility spell starts and stops with the seasons' income, and no standard counts people who have the trait; the task needs a lasting condition recorded only through a billed service during an enrollment spell, which is the claims task type's own case-identification problem, not a health-plan backdrop.
- Layer 1: the NPPES monthly file superseded record by record by weekly incrementals, deactivations and a reactivation, practice and mailing addresses, multiple taxonomies with a primary switch, ZIP codes spanning two counties; the claims and encounter extract's frequency codes (original, replacement, void), rendering NPIs missing on some encounters, diagnosis codes in four positions, an encounter and a fee-for-service claim for one visit; the eligibility spells' retroactive enrollment, overlapping spells, member numbers reissued at re-enrollment and tied by a person number, county moves; the state fiscal year against the calendar; each produced by the system named and none placed to be caught.
- Layer 2: estimation_fit with the field's case-identification method: a member's condition is observed only through the claims of their enrolled months, so prevalence is read where observation was complete and carried to every member by eligibility group, not counted from the records of members observed briefly.
- Decisive step: counting the members with each condition as the prevalence the members enrolled all year show, by eligibility group, carried to every member, not as the members whose claims recorded the condition.
- Not semantics: every diagnosis code, frequency code, spell and eligibility group read correctly still leaves the analyst choosing how to count members whose condition no claim has recorded, and the natural count, members with a qualifying claim, is what the claims hold; no column marks a member as having an unrecorded condition.
- Not rule application: NP-31 files the conditions, the population, the window, the ratios and the two-of-three test; it says the plan estimates the members with each condition and says nothing about how, so executing every filed rule over the recorded diagnoses designates only the two counties short of prescribers.
- Analyst test: a strong analyst who conforms NPPES, the claims and the spells and reads every field and NP-31 correctly counts members with a condition as those with a qualifying 2026 claim (or, by the continuous-enrollment reflex, only those enrolled all year) and designates only Douglas and Okanogan (the default path's `Ends on:`), leaving out the farm counties.
- Estimand: Network Policy NP-31 (`np31_prescriber_service_area_designation.pdf`): for each county, the practising behavioral-health prescribers (as the policy defines them) per 1,000 adults enrolled at any time in the contract year July 2025 to June 2026 who have a serious mental illness, the same per 1,000 who have an opioid use disorder, and the share of adults with a serious mental illness who had a visit with a behavioral-health prescriber in the contract year, the plan estimating the number of members with each condition; a county qualifies when it fails at least two of the three thresholds.
- No decoy: nothing is planted: no memo, prior designation, briefing or stakeholder figure ranks the counties or argues a count; the short spells are the farm counties' seasonal enrollment, every member's diagnoses are the claims their services produced, and the distractors carry no 2026 condition counts.
- No certified replay: no certified or published figure is reproduced to recover a method; the policy states what to measure and leaves how to count unrecorded conditions to the analyst.
- Lesson: a model learns that a chronic condition counted from claims is under-counted wherever members are observed briefly, so prevalence must be measured where the window was complete and carried to every member; no build on file counts a population whose trait a record shows only through events during each unit's observation.

## Tried and rejected

- Over-coverage of the provider master (NPPES records of prescribers who stopped practising) as the decisive step, estimated from a revalidation wave or a recredentialing cohort (design, on paper): the verified half generalises to the unverified with one ratio a strong analyst writes by reflex, and the scheduled version reduces to "status as of the last check", the lesson task457 already teaches; kept as Layer 1 through the policy's own definition of a practising prescriber.
- A two-step floating catchment (prescriber capacity shared among every county within reach) as the decisive step (design, on paper): the estimand either says the capacity is shared, which files the method, or does not, and then the one-step count is a literal reading; a fork either way.
- Prescriber retention by Kaplan-Meier as a third standard (design, on paper): the textbook survival estimate is the reflex (task134 loop 0 died on it), so it carries no stump.
- First scratch simulation (qualifying claims about one every two months, a third of expansion adults in short spells): the recorded count fell only 3 to 6 per cent short in the farm counties, too thin for the thresholds to sit between the readings; rarer qualifying claims and the farm counties' seasonal spells put the shortfall at 13 to 19 per cent, and all three standards now rest on need.
