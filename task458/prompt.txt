What hourly rate should each Elsworth meter zone charge from January 2027 under the city's performance pricing rule? The council memo, elsworth_2027_meter_rates.docx, needs every zone's fiscal 2026 weekday occupancy from noon to 2 p.m. against the 60 and 85 per cent lines in a chart, its 2026 and 2027 rates, and for each residential permit area beside a district the permits in force on the first of each month of 2026. Each zone's figures also go in zone_midday_occupancy_fy2026.csv, with the spaces each city garage had free at weekday noon in each month of fiscal 2026. Occupancy is in whole per cents, permits and spaces whole numbers and rates to the cent.
