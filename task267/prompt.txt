In referral_rule_review_2027.xlsx give each rule's shares of people with undiagnosed diabetes and of all adults screened it would have referred in the 2026 season, backed by the season's tests: how many HbA1c test cartridges each of our twelve analyzers used and, for each month, what the laboratory billed us, net of its credits, for the glucose tolerance tests done that month and how much of that was for tests of adults we screened.
