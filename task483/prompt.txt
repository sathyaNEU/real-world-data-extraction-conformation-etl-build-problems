Where should our two UTI pathway pharmacists work from April 2027? The pathway specification puts one in each of the networks where moving 2026 first courses on other antibacterials to nitrofurantoin would have stopped the most first courses meeting a resistant organism. In uti_pathway_placement_2027-28.xlsx give each network's 2026 first courses on other antibacterials and the mismatched courses moving them would stop, name the networks the pharmacists go to and the runner-up, and chart the courses stopped by network; put the placement in uti_pathway_placement_note_2027-28.docx. The pharmacists will also advise on women aged 65 and over and men aged 16 and over, so for each network give how many of those patients had a 2026 urine culture from a practice or the hub that grew an enterococcus, and how many had one that grew Enterobacter cloacae. Courses and patients are whole numbers, with courses stopped to the nearest 10.
