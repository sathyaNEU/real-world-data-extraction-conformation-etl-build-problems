Should we self-report any of our Marsh and Wolds pumping station storm overflows to the Environment Agency, with the 2026 event duration monitoring return, for discharging in 2026 while its station was passing forward less than the flow its permit sets? The self-report, overflow_self_report_2026.docx, names the overflow and its permit or says none breached, with the flow the permit sets and the flow the station was passing forward while it spilled. The return is overflow_return_lines_2026.csv, one line per storm overflow with its permit, the flow its station passes forward beside the permit's and whether it met it, its 2026 spills counted as our reporting procedure counts them, its hours of discharge and the percentage of the year its discharges were monitored; spill_hours_by_month_2026.svg charts every overflow's hours of discharge in each month. Flows go in whole litres per second, hours and percentages to one decimal place.
