Where should our biorepository send its blood-handling audit before collections restart in March 2027, judged on what each assessment center's own handling has done to the DNA we genotyped in Release 1? Brief the decision in handling_audit_briefing.pptx, a short deck with one chart comparing the centers, and set the supporting counts in release_accounting_2026.xlsx: for each center, the participants still available for the 2027 release with a usable buffy coat and consent in force, and the share of its 2016 to 2020 blood shipments that reached us more than 24 hours after the earliest draw they carried, each with its total across the centers. Participant counts are whole participants and shipment shares are to the nearest whole percent.
