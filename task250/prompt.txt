Should the tracking data standard's scan alert fire at any of our sort hubs for October, and if so at what hubs? The figure that decides it is each hub's missed-scan rate over the parcels it handled, given with a 90 per cent interval that takes each of those parcels as an independent chance of a missed sort scan and read as lying wholly above the standard's tolerance, across it or wholly below it. Each hub's parcels handled in October, missed sort scans, rate, interval and alert belong in tracking_scorecard_2026-10.xlsx, beside, for each depot, how many of the extract's courier events, if any, its handhelds sent to the platform twice and how many parcels, if any, shippers pre-advised for October collection there that it never picked up. The slide scan_alerts_2026-10.pptx charts each hub's rate with its interval against the tolerance, with the hubs whose alert fires marked. Rates and interval bounds are per cents to two decimals and every count a whole number.
