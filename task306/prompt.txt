How much serious harm would the federation's pharmacist team avoid in 2027 with each of its candidate deprescribing reviews, if the review ran across every member practice? A short paper for the medicines optimisation committee, medicines_safety_review_2027.pdf, should recommend the review to run, name the runner-up and the gap, chart for each review the patients it would take off their medicine beside the harms that avoids, and set out each practice's specialist letters per 1,000 registered patients in each half of 2026, and deprescribing_reviews_2026.csv should give, for each locality and quarter of 2026, the reviews the team completed and the share of reviewed patients still off their medicine six months on. Patients and harms are whole numbers, and shares and rates are to one decimal.
