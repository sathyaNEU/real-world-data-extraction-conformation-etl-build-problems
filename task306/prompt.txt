How much serious harm a year would each of the federation's candidate deprescribing reviews avoid if its pharmacist team ran that review from April 2027? A short paper for the federation's board, medicines_safety_review_2027.pdf, should recommend the review to run, name the runner-up and the gap, chart for each review the patients it would take off their medicine beside the harms that avoids, and give the hospital medicine letters each primary care network's practices received in each half of 2026; pharmacist_consultations_2026.csv should give each network's patient consultations with the team in each quarter of 2026. Patients are to the nearest 10 and harms are whole numbers, and letters and consultations are per 1,000 patients registered at the start of the period, to one decimal.
