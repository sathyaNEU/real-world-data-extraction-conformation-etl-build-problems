How much water a day can we apply to take from Borehole 2 when our licence is renewed, under the derogation test in the pre-application letter, and does the 1,800 cubic metres a day the new line needs pass it? Our application goes in by 18 December, so set out in bh2_licence_variation_note.pdf the largest daily quantity that passes, the protected borehole that limits it, each protected borehole's predicted drawdown at 31 March 2039 at 1,800, a chart of the limiting borehole's drawdown over the term at both quantities against its available drawdown, and the litres of water line 3 took per litre packed in each month of 2025-26, cleans included, as line 5 copies it. In bh2_returns_check.csv give, for each month from April to September 2026, the volume we took and the number of days we took more than 1,200 cubic metres. Quantities are to the nearest 100 cubic metres a day, drawdowns to the nearest metre, litres per litre to two decimals and monthly volumes to the nearest 100 cubic metres.
