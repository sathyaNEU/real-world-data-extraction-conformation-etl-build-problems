How much water can we apply to take from Borehole 2 each day when our abstraction licence is renewed, under the derogation test in the licensing team's pre-application letter, and does the 1,800 cubic metres a day that the new bottling line needs pass it? Our application goes in by 18 December, so set out in bh2_licence_variation_note.pdf the largest daily quantity that passes, which protected borehole limits it, and each protected borehole's predicted drawdown at 31 March 2039 at that quantity and at 1,800, with a chart of the limiting borehole's drawdown over the term. Put in bh2_returns_check.csv, for each licence year from 2015-16 to 2025-26, the volume we abstracted and the number of days we took more than the licensed daily quantity. Quantities are to the nearest 100 cubic metres a day, drawdowns to two decimals of a metre and volumes to the nearest 1,000 cubic metres.
