# task282 design note

## Draw

Not drawn. Seed DA32, kept as world, decision and quantity: a county highway authority ranks its minor-road resurfacing programme on annual vehicle-km, holding one 12-hour manual count on each of 30 links taken on single days through the year, and must expand every short count with factors from its automatic counters under one factor-group structure. The seed's mechanism rows were set aside under the 10/10 rules.

## Tried and rejected

- Factor groups assigned by each count's own hourly profile (the seed's axis, prototyped with four rhythm patterns over 46 counters and 30 links): the decisive move, a period total following the calendar of the reference group whose rhythm the unit's own short record matches rather than the group its label puts it in, is task474's driver in other words (a total at most sites follows the reference sites' calendar, but at the few sites serving one facility it follows that facility's own activity), so it fails the abstraction test whatever the nouns.
- Factor groups by road class (the guidance's default): on a mixed network the class-based and the profile-based factors put the same top five links on annual vehicle-km, so the call moves only on a network of minor roads alone, where the class axis disappears and the step reduces to matching one profile.
- Counts taken on days chosen for the counter's convenience (length-biased or point-in-time sampling of the year): spent across the corpus (task130, task135, task182, task268 and task269 notes) and, where the dates are recorded, a reweighting a strong reader applies by reflex.
- Seasonal factors from a counter year disturbed by a closure or diversion: the closure is a dated record that announces itself, so the repair is Layer 1 and cannot carry the call.
- Twelve-hour to 24-hour conversion by group: a filed national table executes it (rule application), and the night share moves annual vehicle-km by a few per cent, under any ranking margin a credible network gives.

## Retired

Retired 2026-10-25 at the author's choice, never drawn. The seed's natural decisive step repeats task474's driver at the abstraction test, and every other step screened in the traffic-count world is spent in the corpus, a filed table, a dated record that announces itself, or too small to reorder the links. No card was registered. The number is not reused.
