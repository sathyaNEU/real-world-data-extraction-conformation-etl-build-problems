# task232 design note (architecture 2)

Tobar Social Research's Household Life Panel (HLP), a quarterly face-to-face household panel in eight Connacht
and Munster counties. The Field Quality Committee refers interviewers to the first-quarter 2027 field quality
review under FQ-3 (Field quality standard, version 3.2) on each interviewer's roster effect, the persons per 100
households by which their rosters over-count usual residents because they took them. Architecture 1 (the public
health unit's per-feed SLA alerts) is spent; its record sits at the end of this note and its reports are kept as
`determinism_check_report_arch1.md` and `leak_check_report_arch1.md`.

## Three rules

Task type: nonresponse-calibration-measurement-error-adjustment (Survey Research & Official Statistics), serving
data-quality-monitoring; interviewer measurement error is the task type's measurement-error half.

Domain test: the deciding quantity is each interviewer's systematic roster error, interviewer measurement error in
survey methodology, whose standard estimator is a multilevel interviewer-effect model on the sample's records with
respondent and area covariates.

Swap: "which meter readers over-read, judged on each reader's effect on the readings" keeps the sentence and loses
the substance: meters do not rotate through a five-wave panel of street segments, are not reissued to a senior
reader when nobody answers, and a reading has no residence rule that can count a visitor.

Layer 1: the reissue export keyed by Eircode and address while the case file keys dwellings (the senior team's
system); the roster file's residence codes (usual resident, away more than six months, visitor) from the CAPI
roster grid; the contact log stamped in UTC by the CAPI sync against Irish local time across the March and
October clock changes; interview dates in two formats across the April 2025 CAPI release; a dwelling whose
household moved out and a new household moved in, numbered within the dwelling.

Layer 2: estimand identification (the right counterfactual under non-random assignment): the interviewer effect
has to be read where the household is held fixed, which the panel offers only through its repeat visits under a
changed interviewer.

Decisive step: holding the household fixed, so each interviewer's effect is read from the households the panel
visited again after their interviewer changed (cover, hand-over or reissue), against the households one
interviewer kept, rather than from interviewers' different households adjusted for what the frame and the area
record.

Not semantics: reading every code right (usual residents only, the reissues linked, the dates and clocks
converted) still leaves the standard model comparing different households, and it refers 2433, 2440 and 2447;
nothing in a field's meaning separates a household's size from its interviewer's counting.

Not rule application: FQ-3 files what the effect is (the over-count because this interviewer took the roster),
the body, the window, the line and the pool, and no sentence names a model, a comparison group or the panel's
repeat visits; executing every filed rule still needs an estimator, and the enumerated family (interviewer
means, frame-adjusted fixed or random interviewer effects, area-held cross-classified effects with and without
the reissues, household, dwelling and segment fixed effects) spreads from 3 referrals to 12, while the standing
family (the household held: household, dwelling or rotation-segment fixed effects) refers the same six with the
lowest at 18.1 and the highest unreferred at 8.6 under any member; the data rule the others out loudly, since
the same households counted by two interviewers differ only by the six's over-count.

Analyst test: a strong analyst who conforms the panel and reads FQ-3 right fits the standard interviewer-effect
model with the frame's covariates and the small area and refers interviewers 2118, 2174, 2181, 2216, 2405, 2433,
2440, 2447 and 2454, three of them for their households' size.

Estimand: FQ-3 section 3 (`hlp_fq3_field_quality_standard_v3.2.pdf`): the persons per 100 households by which an
interviewer's rosters over-count usual residents because that interviewer took them, against the panel's
interviewers as a body (zero is the average over every household interview completed in the window), over the
household interviews completed from 1 January 2025 to 31 December 2026, referring at 12 or more among the
interviewers on the field staff on 1 January 2027 with 30 or more household interviews completed in 2026.

No decoy: nothing is planted: no copied block, memo, draft, briefing or stakeholder figure carries a wrong answer;
the reissues, the patches and the streets are how a face-to-face panel is fielded, and the field quality
committee's papers state no effect for anyone.

No certified replay: no published or certified figure is reproduced to recover a method; the panel's quality
report gives response rates only, and the call is estimated, not matched.

Lesson: an interviewer effect read across interviewers is a household difference unless the household is held,
and a panel holds it only where its repeat visits changed hands; no build on file identifies a recorder's own
effect from the same units seen by two recorders (nearest drivers 0.12 and under).

## Survivor anatomy

Actor: the deciding property is each interviewer's over-count on a household, which no column holds: the
interview file records the residents counted, the roster file each person's code as the interviewer entered it,
and the over-count exists only as the difference between one household's rosters under two interviewers.

Link: the signal is read through the same household across waves under different interviewers, a cross-unit
comparison the field's interviewer-effect model never asks for (it compares interviewers on one sample's
records); the household's waves share a dwelling and household number, but no key or sentence invites comparing a
household with itself under another interviewer, and the reissued interviews sit in the senior team's export keyed
by Eircode.

Reflex: the field's method (a multilevel interviewer-effect model with the frame's dwelling covariates, then the
small area held) sits at rungs 0 and 1 and files `Ends on:` (nine referrals, three for their households' size).

Back-test: the textbook route validates: the frame-adjusted model's interviewer intraclass correlation is about
0.02, inside the range the literature reports for household rosters, its dwelling covariates explain most of the
household-size variance, and its residuals show nothing odd.

Second use: every file the decisive step reads has its own reason in the pack: the interview file is the panel's
spine and feeds the response-rate ask; the dwelling frame is the sample's register; the case file carries the
issues the response rates count; the senior team's reissue export carries the reissued outcomes the response rates
need.

Shock: the movement the step recovers reads as ordinary: the six referred interviewers are not the six largest on
any shipped statistic (mean household size, frame-adjusted effect, area-held effect, interview length, response
rate), and their patches span town, rural, mixed, family and retired areas.

Pins: FQ-3 files the WHAT in the standard's own genre (the effect's meaning, the body, the window, the line, the
pool), and no filed sentence names a model, a comparison, the panel's repeat visits or the reissue practice;
deleting any one sentence either leaves the call forced or unfixes a WHAT (the window, the line or the pool),
never the step.

## Literal reader

Executed word for word, FQ-3 section 3 asks for each interviewer's over-count because they took the roster, against
the body over the window; the literal reader must estimate what the body would have counted for the interviewer's
households, and the panel supplies it only where those households were counted by another interviewer, which
returns the golden six; the frame-adjusted and area-held models estimate it under assumptions the panel's repeat
visits contradict.
