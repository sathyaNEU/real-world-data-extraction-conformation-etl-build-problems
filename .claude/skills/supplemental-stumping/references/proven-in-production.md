## Part 1. The arithmetic, measured

### A1. Reachability, not count, decides the bar  [M]

A response that lands the main call banks about `0.45 + 0.55 c` where `c` is the share of ask weight
reachable from the landed call. task58 v15's first draft had 13 of 24 asks reachable and scored 62.5 per
cent; task58 v1 put about 75 per cent of ask weight downstream of the main discovery, so a landed call
scored 75 to 95. Count `c` on paper before the pack is cut; with every ask decoupled it is near zero.

### A2. The denominator is a lever the ladder is not  [M]

task60 v7, against a generated rubric at 87 weight points with two responses at 65 and 32: adding `N`
asks both fail gives `97 / (2 (87 + N))`, under 50 per cent from `N = 11` and under 40 from `N = 35`.
That is the move when the ladder is measured out, and then it is the only move that works.

### A3. The second slot is the response that missed the call  [M]

task181: both in-house rounds missed the call and still kept 65.2 and 59.0 of the proxy, mostly ask
weight; the proxy was recorded and never gated, the build shipped, and the portal returned 52 with the
call held. Seven more portal results in the window (102, 103, 105, 106, 107, 133, 181) scored 50 to 100
with the main call stumped or deterministic. The ask gate in `grade.py` (exit 3, ASKS LEAK, at 35 per
cent of ask weight kept or any item cracked by a response that missed the call) exists because of these.

## Part 3. What held, measured

- **A stored label taken before a silent switch** (98 v4, portal): the one thing that caught a top
  response. Every row looked valid and the wrong vintage was complete.
- **An event's quantity repeated on every covered line, in an extract whose grain is not stated** (20
  DEC-6, portal): the responses summed the repeats and filed 92.0 per cent yield against 97.48. The tell
  (a line larger than the receipt it covers) sat in another file.
- **A clock against a filed cutoff** (134 rating ask, in-house): rows changed period with no error and the
  rating counts held; the chart built on them did not (Part 2), which is why a visual's order and title
  are checked on the cracker sheet too.
- **An inconsistency visible only at a cross-file cut nothing invites** (133 loop 1 6b, in-house): the
  two asks that had been cracked held once each device's adjudication was moved off every filed sentence
  and the receipt ledger was made to tie under both handlings.

## Part 4. Density inside three files

The deliverable ceiling is three files, so ask density comes from criteria per file, never from a fourth
file. The shipped vehicles: a code deliverable that prints a numbered list of figures (task64's script
prints 36 separately graded figures); a workbook with several sheets, each on its own population, one of
which deliberately does not reconcile to the others and says why (task81); a grid whose every cell moves
on one analytical choice (task74's 16-cell grid). These are shipped shapes, not measured holds; what they
buy is the criteria count, and whether the cells hold is decided by the devices under them.

## Part 5. Checks the record says to run

1. **Write the cracker sheet first** (A3, Part 2): the response that landed the call, ran the battery and
   executed every filed rule, scored on every ask. Every row misses or the ask is rebuilt.
2. **Count reachability** (A1): ask weight reachable from the main call above about a third puts the bar
   out of reach whatever the devices do.
3. **Score the ceiling against the pack as cut**, then grep the whole bundle for every device's own
   vocabulary (76 v1, 65 v1).
4. **Run the hygiene battery on every wrong path** and assert it comes back clean (73 v2).
5. **No filed rule adjudicates a device on the ask's own path** (105 v4, 118, 122, 123, 228, 229).
6. **No referee ties only under the golden handling**; the one referee arbitrates a designed
   disagreement and leaks no level (122, 123).
7. **No ask is keyed to the call or to the construction** (133 v1, 118, 119, 134).
8. **A chart's order, its labels and its title are rows on the cracker sheet** (134).
9. **Give every device a stated wrong value**, outside the bin, so the miss is a figure and not a hope.
10. **Gate the round on the asks** (`grade.py` exit 3) as well as on the call (181).
