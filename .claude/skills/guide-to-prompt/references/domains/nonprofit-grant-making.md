# Nonprofit & Grant-making

**Axis 0 domain.** Canonical label, do not rename it. Every task sits in exactly one domain, and the one to pick is the one whose decision-maker would actually own the call.

- **Typical decisions:** Grant portfolios, grantee health, funding gaps, giving strategy
- **Typical data:** Award data, 990 filings, program budgets

## Enumerated subdomains

Pick one and write it into `DATASET_NOTES.md` with the domain. These lists are a browsing aid rather than a closed set, but a subdomain you cannot map to one of these is a signal to change the trap, not to stretch the scope.

- **Grant & award portfolios:** who is funded, how much, by program, agency, or geography
- **Grantee financial health:** 990/990-PF/990-EZ filings, revenue, assets, program vs expense linkage
- **Funding gaps & landscape:** where funding is thin across a sector or region
- **Giving strategy & program funding:** foundation and agency giving strategy, alignment of awards to stated priorities
- **Other:** other questions about the funding of programs or research using grants, awards, or funding portfolios
