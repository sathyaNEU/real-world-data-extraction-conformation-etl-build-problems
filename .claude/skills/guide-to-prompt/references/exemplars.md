# Client exemplars: 49 accepted tasks with the measured model mean

The client's hardest accepted tasks (batch 14), each with the current model's mean score over four runs (two for
Freight Rail Siting). Read the two or three nearest the draw for the **depth** of analysis the client pays for, then
learn the stump, never the structure. Shape is the nearest of the eighteen (`fig` is one committed figure with no
repeated unit). The stump column says what the models actually did wrong.

**Their architecture is capped now.** Every task under 0.25 shares one design: a published control set made by a
method no document spells out, a clause that makes reproducing it the condition of use, a unit or population the
governing text defines but no file stores, an obvious construction that reproduces most but not all of the
controls, and exactly one that reproduces all of them and changes the call. A hidden method recovered from
certified figures is capped at review, so take from these the unit, population, window and construction errors a
strong model makes, and pin the answer by a stated estimand instead (`stumping`). The measured counts behind the
strategies are in `../../stumping/references/strategies.md`.

## What the measured record says

- **Under 0.25** the miss is the unit or population (grain, applicant unit, stall, seat-month) or a construction that
  reproduces most controls; strong models report the mismatch and adopt the wrong basis anyway.
- **0.25 to 0.45**: right decision, wrong construction (window, lag, circularity, near-miss cohort); the figures fail
  even when the call holds.
- **Over 0.45**: the call lands and points go on scope (a segment untreated, a slate searched too narrowly, a
  binding constraint treated as a caveat). These build width, not a stump.
- Decoys are defused almost every time; what costs the models is a default construction nothing in the pack flags.
