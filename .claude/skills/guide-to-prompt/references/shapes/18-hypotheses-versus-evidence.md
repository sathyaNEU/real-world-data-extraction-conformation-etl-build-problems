# Shape 18 · Hypotheses versus evidence

> **Where the criteria come from:** each cause-against-evidence cell.

You lay out candidate causes against lines of evidence in a grid and mark each cell as consistent
or not with the figure behind it. The answer is the one cause you act on and the movement it
explains. The many criteria come from each cause-against-evidence cell.

**Canonical Axis 1 objective under our roster:** Root-Cause Analysis.

## Sizing it to 25

Four candidate causes against four lines of evidence is sixteen cell verdicts. Add the surviving
cause, the share of the movement it explains, the decisive affected-versus-unaffected gap, the
corrective action, the total movement, the grid completeness check, and the two deliverables.

**What makes it hard rather than long.** Every cell verdict has to rest on **a figure the solver
computes**, not on a plausibility judgement, or the grid is an essay in a table and the rubric
cannot grade it. Design the elimination so that exactly one cause survives all four lines, and so
that each of the other three is killed by a *different* line, which forces all sixteen cells to be
worked rather than three. The affected-versus-unaffected split is the decisive line, and the size
of the movement the surviving cause explains has to fall short of the total, with the remainder
attributable and stated.

**Gate G caution.** This shape sits closest to the banned surface-read rejection, because
"eliminate the wrong explanation" can slide into "catch that the reported number is misleading".
Keep every figure in the pack correct and every stakeholder claim about their own numbers true.
The candidate causes are real changes that really happened in the window; what the evidence
settles is which one carries the movement, never whether a reported figure is a lie. Read
`../../../stumping/SKILL.md` Part 1 before drawing this shape.

### Supply Chain & Logistics · 2 files · ~26 criteria
*Columbus DC on-time delivery corrective action*

I run the regional DC network for a grocery 3PL. Four things changed in the window and I owe one
cause acted on plus its corrective action, judged against the evidence the account will accept.
Write the corrective-action note the account signs off, columbus_otd_rca.docx, the cause we act
on, the corrective action, and the size of the on-time drop it explains in points, then each
candidate cause against each evidence line marked consistent or not with the figure behind it. And
the review slide, a PNG, the candidate-by-evidence grid with each cell marked consistent or
inconsistent against its figure, the surviving cause highlighted, and the weekly on-time rate for
affected and unaffected lanes beneath.

*Criteria:* 4 candidate causes x 4 evidence lines = 16 cell verdicts + the surviving cause + the
drop it explains + the decisive affected-vs-unaffected gap + the corrective action + the total
