# Stumping

> A stump is the analytical step a competent analyst skips because the default path looks finished
> without it: every figure is correct, every real rule filed, and the natural pipeline reconciles and
> commits to a specific wrong answer.

**The client's 10/10 rules bind every draw, rework and repair** (`CLAUDE.md`, first section): 1.1 answers
them in the design note before the default path is written. Work Part 6 first (the draw), then Parts 1 to 5.
The record behind each rule is `references/evidence.md` (cited as `evidence.md` with its section) and the
families with every death are `references/strategies.md`; the client's rigor cards sit in
`references/client/` (learn one, write your own). `../guide-to-prompt/references/exemplars.md` shows the
depth, never a withheld method, and `analytical_tasks/` seeds give a world, never a mechanism. **The portal
is the only stump oracle.** The leak reader and the judge's Gate F check the paper; no solver, scored reader
or probe runs on any build (`CLAUDE.md`, the STOP block), and an in-house miss was never evidence of a stump
(273 v2 and 255 loop 1 were in-house misses the portal landed).

## Part 0. The goal and the opponent

The top two average under 70 per cent with at least one of them genuinely stumped on the call; build to
60. A response on the call banks about 45 and one off it about 12: the ladder keeps at least one of the top
two off the call, and the asks hold each of them under two fifths of their weight (`supplemental-stumping`
Part 0). Builds fail when the call lands (Gate F), rigorous solvers disagree (A to E), or the difficulty is
a wrong number, a plant, a reading or a rule (G, H).

**The opponent, as the portal measured it** (37 approvals and about 60 returns read in full, `evidence.md`
Part 0 for the earlier count): it executes the field's named method for the deciding quantity by reflex,
quoting the textbook (analyse as randomised; condition on survival to date; the cover-conditioned extension;
the stratified induced-exposure construction; Rogan-Gladen with recall), and **in none of the eighteen
recorded portal landings did it take the design's default path**: the default path was the designer's model
of an analyst who stops one rung short, and the portal's models took that rung on sight. It reads every
helper document and every clause, follows a filed sentence to the quantity it names, back-tests and
reconciles everything, and runs the hygiene battery. What it does not do is read a relation between rows in
a file the question gives no reason to open, compare across files or sibling units where the field's method
never asks for a comparison, or doubt a validation record that says the textbook route is sound. Every
approved call lives there (1.4).

**Gate F is judged on the count of cheap routes that reach the call, measured on the clean truth.** One
cheap route landing it is the intended outcome and two is the ladder failure. `checks.py` counts them on the
generator's clean truth (`dataset-generation` §7.1); an audit of thirteen builds found multiple cheap routes
landing each call, which is what puts both top responses on it.

## Part 1. Find the pitfall

### 1.1 The three rules, first and in writing

The client reviews every task against three rules and sends a break back even when the task is
deterministic and correct (`references/client/two-layer-task-design.md`): the task really uses its domain;
it has two layers, realistic mess and the analysis that decides; a plant, data semantics or rule application
may appear but never decides. They are answered before the default path is written, in the design note's
`## Three rules`, one labelled line per point of the client's own check. `precut.py` refuses a missing or
one-word line.

| Label | The line answers |
|---|---|
| `Task type:` | the domain's task type the build sits closest to, one that serves the objective in the taxonomy (the guard and `precut.py` check it) |
| `Domain test:` | the quantity that decides the call and the field's own estimator, model or test for it |
| `Swap:` | the call sentence rewritten with another field's nouns, and what stops making sense |
| `Layer 1:` | each mess item and the system that produces it; nothing placed to be caught |
| `Layer 2:` | the family and the methodological choice that flips the call |
| `Decisive step:` | the analytical choice that flips the call, in the field's own words |
| `Not semantics:` | why reading every field, code, unit and definition correctly still leaves the easy route on the wrong answer |
| `Not rule application:` | which step no filed rule or procedure performs, so executing every stated rule still files the wrong answer, **and the measured spread of the estimator family the rule leaves open** (a line answering only the first half is worth nothing) |
| `Analyst test:` | the easy route a strong analyst takes with every field read and every rule applied, and the wrong answer it files (the default path's `Ends on:`, named) |
| `Estimand:` | the filed sentence fixing population, outcome, window, unit and comparison, and the file it sits in |
| `No decoy:` | that nothing is planted to be caught: no copied block, hidden rule, memo, draft, briefing or stakeholder figure written to carry the wrong answer |
| `No certified replay:` | that the method is not hidden and recovered by reproducing certified or published figures |
| `Lesson:` | what a model learns by getting it right, and why no build on file already teaches it |

**Rule 1, the domain.** The quantity that decides must be one the field estimates, models or tests, worked
the way its task type works it. Run the swap on the sentence, never in the head: if the call still reads with
a hospital for a school, the domain is a backdrop and the draw is redone. A sort, filter, join, threshold or
stated score deciding the call is a backdrop at any depth. In General Statistics & Data Science the swap is
of the method: the deciding quantity is the task type's own statistical object, and replacing it with a
generic summary must change the call.

**The `Not rule application:` line carries three numbers.** "Does a filed rule name the method?" is not the
test; **"is anything left to decide once every filed rule and the data are read?"** is. The line gives the
spread of the **enumerated** family (every construction a competent analyst would reach for), the spread of
the **standing** family (the subset the data and the filed rules leave defensible) and the loudness of
whatever does the ruling-out. A standing spread of zero on the graded figure means the step is a reflex,
whatever the documents say, and the build is drawn elsewhere; eleven builds answered the first question
truthfully, measured a standing spread of zero and were landed (`evidence.md` 1.1).

**Convergence and reach are one dial wherever the easy route is itself a member of the licensed family.**
Where the decisive step is a choice among estimators, making the family agree (Gate C) makes the easy route
agree too (Gate F lost); where the easy route sits outside the family because the data refute it, a joint
cell can exist. So ask first whether the easy route is in the family; if it is, a decisive step that is an
estimator choice has no setting where the choice both matters and is determinate (measured on 20 redraws, 0
settings; six stage-2 repairs on six grounds refused on it). What carries a stump is a step the data make
**necessary and unique**: the population the decision funds, the structure a pooled quantity hides, the
quantity no file holds, where the family that skips it lands somewhere else and the family that takes it has
nothing left to choose. Price the cheapest licensed member, never the one the design intends, and print its
hit rate beside the family's unanimity (`evidence.md` 1.1, the dial paragraphs).

### 1.1b The field's method sits at the bottom of the ladder, never at the top

Rule 1 requires the deciding quantity to be worked the way the field works it; Gate F requires the decisive
step to be one a competent analyst skips. A strong analyst applies the field's own method, so **a design
whose golden is the field's method has written its answer as the opponent's first move**: the portal
landed it every time (139 "analyse as randomised", 166 v1 and v2, 156, 137, 133, 255, 273 v2 and v4, 464 v2;
45 and more in-house architectures on the same shape, `evidence.md` 1.1b and the F2 to F4 records). The
resolution is a place on the ladder: the field's method is rung 0 or rung 1, written into `## Default path`,
and the decisive step is the recognition that **this dataset violates that method's precondition**. The
reader then applies the method correctly, as rule 1 requires it to be present, and lands wrong, as Gate F
requires. `precut.py` refuses a note whose `Domain test:` and `Decisive step:` lines name the same method
family. **The library test:** if the stump sentence reads "use X on Y" with X a named correction, the step is
a reflex.

### 1.1c The grouping dichotomy: an estimator's grouping cannot carry a stump

A decisive step that is a choice of estimation grouping (pooled against stratified, one clock against one
per class, a shared parameter against a per-unit one) has two outcomes and both fail a gate: the data
separate the groupings, so the fit shows it and the analyst takes the better-supported one (a reflex, Gate
F); or they do not, so two defensible estimators give two committed answers (Gate C, `underspecified_
objective`), and filing the choice is rule application. Measured on one build (per-class against pooled at a
likelihood ratio of 511.1 on 668 degrees of freedom, 4,866 against 5,705; the blind reader fitted per class
unasked). A grouping sits at rung 0 or 1, and the asks may turn on one; a draw whose decisive step reduces to
"at what level is this estimated" re-roots before data is cut.

### 1.2 The stump lives in HOW, never in WHAT

The estimand (population, outcome, window, unit, comparison) is filed so its literal execution is the
golden's quantity (`determinism-check` §1). The method stays open only when the design and the data's
structure (preferred), a standard's general method or a filed selection criterion force it. If the literal
estimand makes the call easy, it was a fork.

**The WHAT screen, run at the draw.** Name the decisive step and ask which part of the estimand it settles.
If it settles the population, the outcome, the window, the unit or the comparison, the draw is dead: file
that part and the computation follows from the clause (rule application); leave it unfiled and the call is
not determinate. There is no third position, and no pin repairs it: 190 (nowcast or count), 192 (the unit),
193 (the comparison population), 269 (which departures end a wait), 306 (the 2027 population), 305 v1 (the
origins population), 483 (the day-180 dating), 464 v3 and v9 (the estimator is the step) all died on it,
most of them after a determinism repair had filed the step and the next round landed the call. **The fork-
is-the-stump test, before any repair:** if the sentence that would close a judge's fork names the thing the
decisive step recognises, the fork is the stump and the build re-roots; a repair there spends the stump and
buys a landed call within a day (118 v2, 141 SF33, 179 v3 section 6, 181 v2, 190 v6).

**A definition that implies the step is not a filing of it.** A filed outcome in plain words that the
golden's step follows from does not rule out the field's standard computation of that outcome: the judge
executes the standard's formula on the data the clause names, and when that is the easy route the call is a
fork. Every such reading gets a fork-table row; a row that cannot be closed is a dead draw. A ratio of
totals against a mean of ratios survives only where the two estimate one quantity under a design the pack
does not fix, and a realistic pack records the design (`evidence.md` 1.2).

### 1.3 Couple the pitfall to the domain

The strong solver's route is the field's own method applied as the textbook states it (`evidence.md` 1.3:
of nine builds read blind, the four whose golden was a card's method applied as the card states it were
landed, the five whose golden turned on a judgment the method takes for granted were missed). So the client's
rigor cards (`references/client/layer2-rigor-patterns.md`) supply the Layer 2 family and the default path's
shortcut; **the stump is the judgment between knowing the method and getting the figure** (which units are
the comparison, which exits compete, which exposure is at risk, where the curve bends, what a cap
truncates), priced like any other candidate (Part 3) before a card is drafted. The card's pin is a WHAT,
never a filed HOW: what the card pins by naming a method is filed as the estimand and the design, with the
data's structure ruling the shortcut out. One card, one build, the card's wording never; the card's key goes
on the fingerprint card as `layer2_pattern`.

### 1.4 What held on the portal: the survivor anatomy

Every main call the portal approved under the current rules shares one anatomy, and no returned call has all
of it (`evidence.md` 1.6a for the earlier statement; the 10-30 record: 110 to 126, 128 to 196, 236 to 309).
It is written into the design note's `## Survivor anatomy`, one labelled line each, before the default path;
`precut.py` refuses a build in design that lacks a line and warns on a shipped one.

| Label | The property, and the pre-cut test |
|---|---|
| `Actor:` | **The deciding property has no column in any file.** The actor has rows (a stay, a permit, a bore, a hitter, a feeder) and the property that decides is a relation between rows, or across files, that nothing stores: consecutive charges on different permits at one unit (117), a stay against a third patient's wait (119), invitees against two badge systems (114), the tap cards of the other lines on the same main (124), position relative to the day's last dial (128). Test: list every column of every file; if the property is one of them, or one join away from one (117 round 1's onboard_charger_kw, 119 rounds 1 to 3's admission_type, 120 v1's schedule, 124 loop 0's coverage), the step is readable and the build moves it off the column. |
| `Link:` | **The signal is read through a link no key offers and no sentence invites**: identity resolved across different entities (surname, first given name and birth date across two agencies, 179; a hundred-block from an address string, 175; a payment reference across three files, 196), or a cross-file or cross-unit comparison the field's method never asks for (sentinel positivity against notifications, 236; observed against unobserved trips on the same ground, 275; the term in force against the class maximum, 141; breaker tickets every sibling feeder opened in the same minute, 303). Test: name the join or comparison and the key it would need; a shipped key, a shared code or a clause that names both sides makes it one line of code. |

| `Second use:` | **Every file the decisive step reads sits in the pack for a reason it independently has** (readability test 7). 182's climate series was read by the decisive rung alone and the build was filed as landed; 179 v5 gave the police file its own ask. Test: list every sentence filed for the call; delete each and recompute; a sentence whose deletion unforces the call handed over the step (57 v21, 141 SF33). |

Two more properties the record shows and the labels do not carry on their own: the deciding quantity is
usually **one per case, forced by a second file at a finer grain than anything the main file holds, with no
free parameter** (`evidence.md` Part 3, step three), and **helper documents describe fields, never process**
(Part 7). A candidate failing any line has a named route to the golden before a world is drawn.

### 1.5 Write the default path first

Write the pipeline a competent analyst builds without thinking, from the prompt's words and the files'
grain: numbered steps naming files, joins, filters and construction, ending on a named figure, **with the
field's own method for the deciding quantity in it** (1.1b). If it ends on the golden there is no stump
(134 loop 0, 130 v3, 100 v1). A rung that bites only a solver who adds a correction is not a rung. A default
path made of generic moves the opponent does not make is the ladder upside down.

### 1.6 The stump sentence

One sentence: the wrong answer the default path files and the step that lands it there, naming the `Ends
on:` figure. `precut.py` refuses a default path ending on the golden or a sentence not naming it.

### 1.7 The data-native sentence

One line: what in this dataset (its generating process, grain, calendar, censoring, selection, identity or
capacity) makes this the stump an analyst trips on here. A move that reads the same anywhere is not built.

## Part 2. Make it survive the opponent

### 2.1 The readability screen

Each rung runs seven tests on paper; any that fires makes it readable, and a readable decisive rung is dead.
**Enumerate the reflex row from the field's vocabulary for the deciding quantity, never from the rungs
beneath it** (a row listing alternatives to rung 1 declares the decisive rung silent and passes the gate
wrongly).

**1 Filed sentence:** a clause, definition or formula names the decisive quantity, or only makes the solver
ask the question: a clause it cannot avoid reading sends it looking, and a join forced by a key it already
holds is one line of code. A filed confidence level, significance level or test threshold is a filed method
when, once filed, only a variance remains; the variance need not be a textbook one, the schema pointing at
which variance it is suffices (190 v6; 6 of 12 textbook routes landed one build whose clause named the level
and the schema said whose interval, `evidence.md` 2.1). **2 Schema:** a column, key or marker makes it a row
predicate or a header-found join. **3 Reflex:** an opponent default, the field's method included (1.1b).
**4 Control bench:** a control or corpus scores the rules or refutes the naive read. **5 Hygiene:** the
battery finds it. **6 Linear:** one subtraction, ratio or half of a reported figure. **7 Dedicated organ:**
delete the decisive step from the design and ask whether the pack would still carry the file, column,
instrument or corpus that makes the golden computable; if not, its presence announces the step. Anything
shipped to make the answer computable is shipped to the solver too (a sensitive-limit laboratory beside a
legacy one; a climate series read by one rung), so the escape is a **multi-purpose** organ: a property of
records the organisation keeps for a reason it independently has. The note gives a row per rung, verdict
last, the decisive row `silent`.

**A readable decisive rung is not re-hidden; it is moved.** Every approved stump that was once a shipped
column moved off the column (117, 119, 120, 124); every return that re-labelled, renamed or buried the same
column was landed again.

### 2.2 No semantic fork on the main recommendation

A term read two ways is a fork and data semantics at once. The `## Semantic fork table`
(`determinism-check` §2) gives each main-ask term and computation convention a row, every alternative ruled
out by a quoted sentence or `same call`, asserted in `checks.py` as a cross product where two rows are both
live readings of the deciding quantity (one build's pair and pooling rows were each green and forked 32 cells
to 3 together). The stump is never a reading.

### 2.3 The deletion tests

Each kills a candidate decisive step. **1** Delete every wrong number and misleading claim: still hard, or a
surface-read rejection. **2** Clean data: the easy route on the clean truth still files the wrong answer,
executed in `checks.py` (`dataset-generation` §7.1). **3** Lens swap (`determinism-check` §4). **4**
Identity: if the governing arithmetic closes over shipped quantities, change the graded quantity. **5**
Control direction: each control is absent or ties under every basis for a reason true now at its grain; one
refuting the naive read is a search bench, and one blind only until the forward window is the validated
decoy the guard blocks. **6** First moves: the one-period group-by, the pilot decomposition, each clause's
natural reading, each corpus back-test, the hygiene battery; any landing kills. **7** Every filed sentence
for the call, one at a time (1.4, `Pins:`).

### 2.4 Oracles

List every control, published figure, reconciliation column and corpus with what it confirms on the wrong
path. One tying only under the golden is an oracle: cut it or make it tie on every basis. A second source
that agrees on the wrong reading too is the shape that held (126's Table P2, 275's terminal check, 196's
advices). The ask layer's referee never arbitrates the call.

## Part 3. The ladder

Three to five rungs, four by default, each a defensible analysis climbed by refusing it: rung 0 the default
path (the field's method, 1.1b), 1 the obvious correction, 2 the sophisticated one, 3 the silent decisive
move. **Rung 0 is never shipped:** no artifact ranks the candidates wrongly, at any depth, except a declared
wrong-basis distractor (`dataset-generation` §8.3). **Every rung** reproduces from the raw files, names its
own candidate, dies to exactly one shipped fact or construction, and is satisfying to stop at; a
loop-fixable slip or one group-by is no rung. Read rung 0 off what the shipped files name, free text
included. Measure the decisive rung on the graded name and the graded figure separately: a rung that only
changes who is blamed, while the figure is the same whoever is blamed, is worth nothing on the figure.

**The pricing sequence, run in this order before a word of design, each one computation on the clean
truth** (the numbers behind each in `evidence.md` Part 3):

1. **Name the residue the filed WHAT leaves.** A knob fails at once; only a method survives (1.2).
2. **Price the move against the ceiling.** The points the call must move for the candidate to decide
   anything, against the largest move the affected population could produce if every record flipped
   (task198's cutover family needed 8.79 and could produce 2.644).
3. **Write the one-group-by route to the call in one line and do the algebra on it.** If the construction
   is affine in a shipped column, that column plus one regression is the decisive step, and no Layer 1
   between the files changes it (a blend-unmixing step at corr +0.9582, one regression beating the designed
   estimator). Enumerate each reflex in its rate form and its count form; the count form is the cheaper and
   the one a solver reaches (losses per traversal landed a call priced only per hour).
4. **Price the standard error of whatever the step must estimate**, in the graded figure's units. A step
   recovering a per-unit differential from that unit's own thin slice injects more noise than the bias it
   removes (6.96 points of error against a 3.0 rise); prefer a quantity the whole population estimates. A
   marginal standard error overstates an ordering's uncertainty, so count redraws (20 of 20 is the standard).
5. **Check whether the lever and the ranked quantity's noise are the same variance.** A step that moves a
   variance cannot carry a ranking of N (the variance that reorders the candidates randomises the quantity
   they are ranked on); it stays sound where the answer is a verdict on one named candidate far from its
   boundary, measured as distance to the boundary in units of the lever's own noise (under one standard
   error is seed-hostage whatever the shape).
6. **Where the step corrects a variance on a call about a change, check the unit's own history.** If its
   period-to-period standard deviation is within about a factor of two of the correct standard error, the
   decisive rung is readable off the series.
7. **Where the quantity is recovered exactly from a subset of rows, check what that subset hands over.**
   Censoring, capping, flooring and suppression split a file into bent rows and rows where the mechanic
   bound exactly, and on the second set the hidden quantity is printed (a lesser-of rule published the rate
   to the cent on censored lines; the share of bound rows was itself the ranking signal). "Estimate the
   nuisance where the signal vanishes" fails the same way, because the null region publishes the nuisance.
8. **Run the reflex sweep as code before any separation is measured.** Enumerate by asking what the
   decisive quantity substitutes for, then list every other way the pack measures the same thing; residualise
   the signal's driver against every visible attribute at draw time and assert it (a driver drawn
   independently still correlates up to about 0.75 by chance); a slate of k needs k real effects.

**Screens that each closed a whole family in one computation** (each with its measurement in `evidence.md`
Part 3):

- **A causal design may not grade a total over the population and window:** the crude before-and-after
  excess equals the causal excess (0.954, 0.953, 1.001 of truth) unless a second mover exists, and splitting
  one total between two movers is attribution, not a causal design.
- **A withheld confounder has a shipped proxy:** adjusting for every recorded attribute lands within 4 per
  cent (a job's duration proxies the difficulty withheld; case-mix standardisation absorbs patient
  movement), and the instrument that escapes it has a standard error above its own estimate.
- **A rate's denominator** is exact arithmetic on a shipped aggregate (rule application) or estimated per
  unit from a panel (the weighting family reopens, four readings spread 0.51 against a printed 0.01).
- **False-discovery quantities** reach a decision three ways and all are closed: a count crossing a bar is a
  random walk's crossing; a pick at a fixed budget is monotone in per-test power; a pick at a fixed trigger
  is collinear with breadth (+0.9986), and breadth is one group-by.
- **A level at a selected unit** is a shrinkage target, and shrinkage is both the opponent's reflex and the
  better estimator (James-Stein 40 of 40); a rank verified and an extremum against a null extremum are both
  spent, so on selected-unit ground draw elsewhere.
- **A documented transformation of recorded values** (a per-window maximum, a banded publication) cannot
  carry a silent rung: documented it is test 1, undocumented it is unidentifiable. The forcing file pins a
  structure, an order, an opportunity count or a condition, never a transformation of the values.
- **The mandated indicator:** where the deciding organisation must publish a quality indicator for the
  process the quantity is forced from, the correction is published beside it (corr +1.0000); on regulated
  ground force the quantity from a file that is not the organisation's own process log. Operations ground
  is adverse for the mirror reason: the metric dictionary realism requires names the operational quantity.
- **A physical intensity entering through a response function** leaves the exponent a free method choice
  (2.55 distinct designated units of 5 across five exponents); the second file's quantity enters as a
  count, a duration or an amount, and a fit is licensed only where the design fixes the functional form.
- **A construction over a selection event** is the same single dial: curvature falls as the selection grows
  extreme while the bias removed grows (spread 13.2 against a bias of 10.5, worse than doing nothing).

**The arithmetic, on paper before any row.** Position, margin, separation and dominance as
`dataset-generation` §6 asserts them (4th or 5th on rung 0, no margin under 1.15x, every rung's highest
figure clearing the decision boundary by the same floor the golden clears it by, asserted in `checks.py`
beside the set; one build shipped three reflex rungs inside 1.106x with every assertion green). One live
degree of freedom in the graded quantity holds no ladder; the decisive move is never the smallest; every
correction walks one way and only the decisive move reverses; a quantile absorbs a selection rung and a
share cancels one moving both terms, while totals, counts and levels carry rungs; every grid cell is a named
wrong answer or outside the floor; the answer is extreme or bracketed. **Count the dial jointly, never as two
marginal patterns:** list the requirements (reach, unanimity, the rung floor, a mid-bin call) and count the
cells that pass all of them at once (245 of 300 marginal passes were 0 of 22 jointly). Two separations
financed by one quantity are priced together, in the graded figure's units, before the design is accepted.

Stack two to four mechanisms from different gaps, the decisive gap not rung 1's; same-pattern stacking is
one mechanism twice, and **a rung stacked above a solved step never held** (124 loop 1, 258 loop 1, 305
v2, 445 loop 2). **A hold** is determinate when a computed blocking quantity reproduces, every candidate
fails for a named reason, the prompt licenses a non-pick on equal footing without advertising it, the
default path still names a candidate, and the note says what would have made a pick.

## Part 4. Force one answer

`determinism-check` owns it. HOW is forced, and a filed method is rule application (§1). The
literal-reader test (§2) runs on the ladder before data is cut. No filed rule, basis or formula reads to
another answer (an official decoy).

**A pin fixes the WHAT, never the HOW, and never the clock the stump rides on.** The portal's record is
exact on this. Pins that fixed population, window, unit or schedule and named no method turned returns
into approvals (169's methodology 4.1 on custody days; 135's policy 4.3 filing daily returns; 151's
register header filing the payment schedule; 162's working agreement counting a report once). Pins that
named the clock, window, grouping, test or comparison the stump rides on landed the call on the next round
(141's filed 90-day review sentence, both responses to the golden; 179 v3's cutover section 6; 181 v2's
count basis; 118 v2's sign-off naming what a working must reproduce; 124 loop 0's verification account),
and each judge's own suggested pin was that kind (169 four times, 162, 309, 141). So: name the observable,
never a file, field, join or move; sit once at authority, never in the prompt; delete the sentence and
recompute, and if the answer stays forced the sentence was never needed; if the answer stops being forced

found a new defensible model). **Grade the decision unit or the precision the data carry, never a filed
grouping**: the pods alone and the projection a band (141), whole millions rather than a filed grouping of

first (C1): rebuild the world so every sound route taking the step prints the golden (169 round 1, 124 round
3, 196), and never re-centre the data to close a fork. Figures sit mid-bin on non-round values with a stated
flip condition, no money on a half-cent. The call is forward facing by default; a retrospective one carries
its reason.

## Part 6. The draw: a unique stump

**The card:** `guard.py recent`, `coverage` and `mechanisms --objective <o>`; draw every card axis

answered; `register` before the ladder. **Hand the draw the commands, never a copied ban table:** cards
register while a pass runs, and a written table is stale within the hour in both directions.

**Where a fresh stump comes from.**
- **The file that does the forcing, before the statistic.** For a quantity estimated per unit from the
  unit's own records a construction is forced in three ways and each is dead: exact arithmetic makes it
  observed (rule application); a non-parametric construction is a stratification (the opponent's first
  group-by); a parametric one reopens the family. Every held build's forcing input comes from a different
  file at a different grain whose variety no stratification of the decision file reproduces (an archive
  holding a trajectory as endpoints, a second capture channel produced by the unit's own follow-up, the
  observation effort behind the counts). Enumerate the files the organisation keeps, ask of each what it
  pins that no other file pins, then screen every construction it forces and expect the obvious one to be
  proxied by a column the file already prints (5 for 5 on one ground, +0.9960).
- **The field's estimator meets the data's process.** List the field's methods for the deciding quantity;
  write how each file's rows come to exist (who records them, when, by which route, what they cannot see).
  Pitfalls live where recording differs from the event: delay by route, reporters not yet filed, checks that
  see a unit just before resetting it, replacements restarting a wear curve, detection falling as targets
  grow rare, sub-units sharing a budget, a lagged response to a shared driver, competing exits left in a

- **Every reflex lands wrong, and the sweep runs as code before the ladder's numbers.** A reflex reaching

- **Wrong now:** the default construction is wrong on the data in hand; a record blessing the naive method

- **Under-used ground, read for why it is unspent.** Count the strategies never drawn on the objective, strike
  the four the guard flags for semantics risk, and take what survives; `binding_constraint` sat unspent on

**Test it against the cards and the record.**
- **The mechanisms on file first:** `guard.py mechanisms` prints every driver and earlier architecture on
  that ground; write one line on the insight each spends. The driver score misses a mechanism told in other
  nouns (two re-roots passed at 0.05 and 0.08 and repeated spent drivers).
- **The abstraction sentence, written into `## Draw`:** the one sentence a reviewer holding this build and
  its nearest approved build would say is the same, written without domain nouns. If it can be written, the

  differentiation line. Spent families the sentence has named: a local count floor; a class re-derived from

## Part 8. Diagnosis and repair

**A diagnosis comes from the portal or the judge, never a solve in the main thread, and a portal result is
the only return.** A build left off a results list stays SHIPPED and pending until an explicit result names
it: task182 was filed FAILED_TO_STUMP from absence on a list, re-rooted eight times without shipping, and
approved as returned. Returned tasks take `/fix-determinism`, `/fix-rubric`, or stage 2 when the call landed
(`build-pipeline`, Returned tasks). Diff each response against the golden cell by cell: the step it never
took is the finding, and an assumption every response holds unchecked is where to change the world.

**What turned a return into an approval** (thirteen builds, `evidence.md` has the earlier record):
1. a WHAT pin in a governing document fixing population, window, unit or schedule, naming no method;
2. the world rebuilt so every sound route taking the step converges, nothing filed;
3. graded precision set to what the honest family shares (the decision unit, a band, a coarser total);
4. ask devices re-sited off filed rules onto the sentence's silence (`supplemental-stumping`);
5. the decisive step moved, in the same world (124 round 2) or a new one (258 loop 2, 141 architecture 4,
   162 architecture 2), after a landed call: **no approved build was repaired by hardening the asks alone
   after a landed call**;
6. a pointing sentence or file deleted (196's feed note, 141's review sentence, 258's field notes);
7. the write-up reordered so the construction leads and the rejected lens follows (126, 135).

**What was tried and never worked:** stacking a rung above the solved step; a pin naming the clock or window
the stump rides on; a filed forecast test on a record lacking the deciding configuration; re-siting asks
inside files the frozen call pins; renaming a device's file without removing its control; hiding an
estimation sample with decoys; honest persistence that reopens the estimator family; re-siting the same
identity device three times; a device the judge reads as a soft fork.

- Landed on the default construction: make it wrong, a rung below rung 0 from another gap.
- Executed the decisive step by reflex or as a work order: move it off every sentence and column into a
  relation no key offers (1.4), or re-root if only a plant stays silent.
- Reached every insight and defended another answer from a file, or read a term two ways: a fork; add the
  `SF` row and converge (`determinism-check` §2). If the closing sentence would name the step, re-root (1.2).
- The last rung never moves the ranking: widen the edge or shrink the carried advantage. A control quoted
  back: tie it under every basis, or cut it. Missed the call yet scored high: rebuild the asks. "The figure
  looks wrong": Gate G; rebuild, never harden.

**Harden, in order:** a rung from another gap below the decisive one; the discriminator behind a join; the
pin's evidence split across two documents; the winner's default-path rank worse; a conditioned yield; the
corpus tightened. **Never** noise, removed evidence, an invented threshold, harder parsing, a wrong-answer
artifact, or a deleted signpost that carried a parameter.

**Re-root rules.** One architecture per world: after a world's decisive step is spent, the next draw moves
to another world, another dataset and another shell (251, 249 and 269 each spent two architectures in one
world where one would have). No same-day serial re-roots without new evidence (118 ran three architectures
on one day and the two with a result failed within a day; 166 v2 the same); the re-root waits for the
portal's word on the version it replaces unless the judge or the leak reader has already killed it. A
reader and judge split on Gate H is resolved before the ship, never filed as an open concern (447, 306). The
stop rules are written before the first portal test: two solves by different routes re-root the ask; a
determinism repair that would make its rung a reading re-roots the difficulty; repeated solves change the
decision, not the mechanism. One mechanism per repair, the dead end written when it dies.
