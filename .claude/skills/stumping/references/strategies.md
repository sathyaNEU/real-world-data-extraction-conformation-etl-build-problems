# Stump strategies: the record, the laws and the dead

What has carried a main-call stump and what killed one: a record, never a menu (in the first corpus every
strategy listed by name was copied onto a dataset that did not produce it). A family is drawn because the
dataset produces it (`../SKILL.md` Parts 1 and 6). Build numbers are the evidence (`shipped-ledger.md`,
cards).

**Outcome words:** *portal held* (the portal missed the call), *held* (a measured response committed to a
wrong answer), *held after repair* (the repair after a solve is the transferable part), *died* (solved, or
failed a gate).

**The guard now blocks** architectures the older record used: the withheld rule with an official decoy;
any filed rule, basis or metric planted to give the wrong answer; a decisive rule left unstated beside a
conflicting stated one; and the **validated decoy**, a closed record on which the wrong method reproduces
every figure until a forward condition breaks it (27 of 30 builds from 99 to 130, and every portal hold
from 102 to 107). Each family keeps its insight; its current form says what is legal now.

## Part 1. The families, by measured record

Each family is a card `stump_strategy`, ordered by portal holds, then other holds; the guard blocks one
used in the last three builds. The one portal hold under the current standard is F4's 181.

### F1. Time: the window the decision bets on
`time_semantics` · gap 1 · pattern A · G13, G4, G7
- **Insight.** The evidence is a statement about a closed window and the decision is a bet on an open one; a
  clock the closed window never exercised (a period end on a weekend, a service anniversary, a lapsing term,
  the year an adjustment attaches to) moves the quantity.
- **Default path.** Carry the closed window forward (trailing rate, scheduled date, today's rate).
- **Record.** Portal held: 102 (payments due on a Saturday year end are released the next Monday, into the
  next fiscal year; pair about 50) and 107 v4 (an intake reaches the experienced rate at its twelve-month
  anniversary just before the window; about 50). Held: 35 (an adjustment attaches to the year a work order
  is endorsed), 55 (one vat anchors a production cycle's expiry dates, so the print offset is a mixture no
  per-line constant reproduces), 60 v4 (a date read with `.year` when the period opens 1 August, the corpus
  reconciling under both readings). Held after repair: 62, 70, 80 (a term dates from its criterion event
  while operation lagged). Died: 50 (a twelve-model rollout), 92 v1 to v3 (both top responses exact), 117
  and 123 (the date was in a filed sentence), 36 (a dated cutover, found by event study).
- **Why it held.** Every closed period reproduces the carry-forward, and the clock lives in a history
  (payments, hires, lots) no column names.
- **Current form.** Those holds are validated decoys. Legal: a clock process already in the data, estimated
  by the field's method from every unit that went through it (a hazard by time since entry, 134 v2), no
  closed figure blessing the wrong method.
- **Vary.** The clock (calendar, service, age, exposure, development), what steps, the decision type. Never
  a dated cause whose effect is a step in an outcome series.

### F2. Grain: what one unit of the decided thing is
`grain_or_unit` · gap 2 · pattern D · G2, G10
- **Insight.** The file's row is not the decision's unit; the unit is built by grouping, splitting or
  linking rows, and building it moves the answer.
- **Default path.** Count or sum rows (the client's habit 2, decided 11 of 64 tasks).
- **Record.** Portal held: 103 v3 (one hamper per household a week; a partner's collection for a named
  household is one of its two hampers; the call held and the asks gave way). Held: 60 (a person filed by two
  districts, both correct, no column separating them; held only by the rubric denominator), 65 (a source
  constituted from activities on contiguous ground, each located by its own parcel), 48 v2 (a correct
  monitor blind at its configured grain cannot refer a population split across its cells). Pattern D was
  approved on 34 (household weights against person rows). Died: 44 (an order id bridged the grains in one
  group-by), 48 (two columns named the move), 67 (the filed construction executed), 103 v1 and v2 (the
  fan-out read off a handbook; any count in the middle band landed), 49 (one live degree of freedom).
- **Why it held.** The grains differ in shape, not level, what the decision funds pins the grain, and no key
  bridges them.
- **Vary.** What constitutes the unit (a span of time, a place, a household, a continuing hire, a production
  cycle) and the bridge (a history, a geometry, a one-allowance rule); never a key column.

### F3. Binding constraint: the limit the metric never checked
`binding_constraint` · gap 3 · pattern C · G8, G11, G7
- **Insight.** The metric is correct and is not what the decision is scored on: a joint limit (a shared
  pool, reachability, an operating window) removes the natural pick, as a removal that re-places nobody.
- **Default path.** Rank by the reported metric; size each candidate against the whole pool and add.
- **Record.** Portal held: 106 run 3 (suppliers on one corridor share the same spare return legs, so sizing
  each against every leg double-books; both top responses missed, at 41 and 70). Pattern C approved on 25
  and 28 (filed under F7). Died: 87 v4 and v8 (an argmax over a filed capacity vector, 100 per cent), 92 v3,
  58 v3 and v4 (a constraint that re-placed the pick), 49 (members peaking on one settlement calendar give
  1.15x), 32.
- **Why it held.** The limit is shared across candidates, never a per-candidate cap.
- **Vary.** What is shared (legs, slots, staff hours, a reviewer pool, a budget line), how candidates
  compete for it (order, corridor, window); reachability binary, never graded.

### F4. Maturity and censoring: the count is not finished
`maturity_censoring` · G12, G13
- **Insight.** Open periods are not fully developed and arrival speed differs by unit, so the arrived count
  understates exactly the units that report slowly.
- **Default path.** Compare what has arrived; the reflex (one pooled or aggregate completion factor) lands
  in the same place.
- **Record.** Portal held under the current standard: 181 (specimens notified against collected: the
  notified count and the pooled-delay nowcast both pick the wrong area; only each route's own delay shows
  the courier-routed area several times above what arrived; 52 without the call). Held: 73 world seven (the
  maturity attack, `../SKILL.md` 2.1). Shipped: 445 (13F holdings completed holder by holder). Died: 134
  loop 0 (Kaplan-Meier censored at the cut was the golden), 31.
- **Why it held.** No file is wrong; the decision needs an estimate of what will arrive, and the field's
  method (a nowcast by route, completion by reporter) is not the generic correction.
- **Vary.** What is censored (specimens, claims, filings, outcomes), whose speed differs (route, reporter,
  cohort, channel), the competing exits. Keep the pooled reflex on a named wrong answer.

### F5. Composition: the pooled rate does not fit this pool
`composition_mix` · pattern E · G6, G14
- **Insight.** A correct pooled or stratum rate does not apply, because the pool's mix on a property no
  column names differs from the measurement corpus.
- **Default path.** Apply the pooled or stratum rate to the target pool.
- **Record.** Held: 68 (entrant inflow and premium repricing hold every aggregate flat while the matched
  incumbent base collapses). Held after repair: 83 (a save offer moves a reader once, so half the June
  middle band has been through the programme). Pattern E approved on 30 (yield conditioned on substitution).
  Died: 44, 43 (a moderator manufactured by an uneven assignment rate), 134 loop 0. Shipped: 134 v2 (a
  conversion hazard by time since entry after a surge filled the pool).
- **Why it held.** The split is absolute, confounders are controlled by construction, and the property is
  rebuilt from a history (a treatment log, a matched panel), never read off a column.
- **Vary.** The conditioning property (prior exposure, tenure, time since entry, continuity) and what drives
  the mix shift. One band must move against the others or standardisation never binds.

### F6. Method selection: the fit is wrong out of sample
`forecast_method_backtest` · the legal gap 4 · G16
- **Insight.** The data supports one method, forced by the design or a filed criterion; the natural fit
  fails forward because the rate is stationary only in a constructed exposure, or a yield belongs to the
  year it was measured in.
- **Default path.** A trailing or register-age route; a pooled yield carried onto a moved base rate.
- **Record.** Held: 46 (a rate law stationary in cumulative service exposure rebuilt from a movement log; a
  cohort crosses the step inside the months the archive does not cover). Approved: 33 (the forward yield of
  an ongoing level shift against a resolved one). Shipped: 446 v2 (error rates carry across years while the
  yield moves with the base rate; the trend reflex files 147). Died: 38 (an enumerable model class behind a
  shipped test, eleven rounds), 89 v5 and v6, 130 v3 (the analog construction was the default), 125 (a
  control bench and one subtraction).
- **Why it held.** The invariant is constructed from a log, and no shipped test enumerates the winner.
- **Vary.** The invariant (exposure, error rates, hazard by age) and the forcing organ (the design, a
  standard's general method, a filed criterion never in consecutive builds).

### F7. Population: who is in the denominator
`population_selection` · G9, G5, G11
- **Insight.** The population the decision funds is not the file's complete one: selection on the outcome,
  checks that see a unit because it was wrong, survivors, replacements that restart a curve.
- **Default path.** The population a flag, filter or list suggests (habit 5).
- **Record.** Approved: 25 and 28 (the share one intervention can reach, behind a join). Died: 100 v1 (a
  clause and labelled columns handed over the forward roll), 57 (the measure tracked unit size, +0.98).
  Shipped: 229 (checks see a unit's state just before resetting it, so the checked rate measures reset
  states), 132 (a failed item restarts at the bottom of its wear curve).
- **Why it holds.** The population is built from histories, never a status word or clause.
- **Vary.** The selection mechanism (on outcome, schedule, symptom, survival, replacement).

### F8. Attribution: whose movement it is
`decomposition_attribution`
- **Insight.** A correct movement is booked to the right driver; overlap, a transfer or a shared external
  driver decides the split.
- **Default path.** Book to the visible owner, difference from a fixed baseline, textbook separation.
- **Record.** Held after repair: 72 (a transferred enrolment stays with the originating subaward; every
  booking partitions the same rows, so every total ties). Died: 36 (an argmax under a stated rule over six
  filed candidates, seven solves across four architectures), 119 and 122 (a filed sentence), 228 twice (a
  reflex). Shipped: 182 (each bore's own driver-only path, fitted before the effect began).
- **Vary.** The shared driver, its lag, the overlap structure; never a cause with a cutover date.

### F9. Causal design: the comparison the design implies
`causal_design`
- **Insight.** The naive difference is not the effect: interference between units sharing a budget or a
  pool, a contaminated comparison arm, regression to the mean, a rate that depends on the pool.
- **Record.** No measured hold. Died: 107 v2 (a filed effect formula conceded the construction), 118 and 124
  (readable), 230 twice (every stratification was a reflex). Shipped: 131 (a household's cards share its
  baskets, so a card's lift is partly moved spend; read on memberships whose cards drew one arm).
- **Vary.** The interference structure and the level the effect is read at.

### F10. Identity resolution
`identity_resolution` (semantics risk, the guard warns)
- One real entity across reused, merged or split identifiers. No hold. Died: 120 (a control bench), 121
  twice (the schema), 81 v3's first cut (Gate G), 90 (a copy of 86 v1, a Template clone of 72 v2). Use it as
  Layer 1 or a lower rung.

### F11. Stated rule, hard construction
`stated_rule_hard_construction` (semantics risk)
- The rule is filed in full; the difficulty is building its quantity. No hold. Died: 133 v1 (a reading of a
  term, taken by a portal model at 100), 107 v3 (conformance as the top rung), 67, 37 (a filed rule over an
  enumerated population). Rule application never decides; it is depth.

### F12. Uncertainty and hold
`uncertainty_hold`
- Signal against noise, multiple comparisons, power; holding can be the answer. Never drawn: the freshest
  ground. A hold is legal only as `../SKILL.md` Part 3 sets it out (and G17, Part 4).

### F13. The withheld rule (retired as the decisive step)
`withheld_rule_official_decoy`, `withheld_rule_corpus_fit`
- The decisive rule is in no shipped sentence and is recovered from settled cases. It carried the first
  corpus (27, 29, 30 and 34 approved; 38, 45 and 105 v4 died) and the client's top four habits (Part 5). The
  official-decoy form is blocked; corpus fit is a semantics risk. 105 v2 is its last hold (a sister outlet
  opening within reach wins only its side of the bisector; both top responses filed 250 against 150); v4
  died.

## Part 2. The laws

Each was paid for, and each is a test run against the build.

- **L1. A shipped control is blind to the decisive move for a computable reason.** Write *"in every control
  case X is zero, identical or absent, because <structural fact>"* (72: no site changed hands in the closed
  window, so its six pieces agree within 0.0004; 60: double counting scales every basis alike, so the corpus
  returns the same basis under either reading). Inverse: a corpus built to refute the naive read is quoted
  back as the evidence (92 v2, 89 v5), and a control that scores the rules nominates the survivor (120,
  125). Blindness comes from structure (L8), never from a record built to bless the wrong method until the
  forward window breaks it (70's form, now a validated decoy). Blindness by tuning, or by excluding a class,
  leaves a fingerprint (56 v5, 56 v6, 57 v23, 76 v2).
- **L2. Score a control row by row.** 70: moving only the closed-day despatches reproduced the total within
  0.12 per cent and none of the 29 rows. Publish the per-row hit count for the rule and every rival (40: 12
  of 12 against 3 of 12).
- **L3. Price the partial correction; it lands further away.** 67: applying the change only where the
  register makes it loud files 35 and 32 against 37 naive and 31 correct.
- **L4. Two errors of opposite sign are the enemy.** Compute the grid cell by cell and report the nearest
  cell and its cost (65: 1.4 per cent across 216 cells, two opposite errors; 62: 0.59 per cent). Safe when
  it takes two filed-rule violations; broken when one defensible reading reaches it.
- **L5. The answer is the extreme cell, or bracketed** (62: 14.17 per cent below at the natural stop, 3.76
  to 20.64 above at the early stops). Never mid-grid.
- **L6. Pin by identity or exhaustion, never by fit** (55: the unique integer under which all 4,462 lot
  codes equal their vat day, zero tolerance). A flat loss curve has not produced a number. Now a filed
  criterion the data confirms (C2), never a recovered rule.
- **L7. The default path passes every check the solver writes for itself.** Every climb-free death failed
  one on the wrong path; the holds (60, 65, 68, 70, 72) all reconciled. The consistency comes from the
  default construction, never from a record built to bless it.
- **L8. No reconciliation can see the decisive move.** Complete-partition invariance: every reading
  partitions the same rows (72: the movement is -0.96 under all four bookings; 67: shares sum either way).

## Part 3. The organs

- **O1. Twin pair.** Two records identical on every visible column, outcomes far apart, so no rule
  transferred by resemblance reproduces the file (36: 3.00x; 45: 1,477 against 727; 62: 96 seats against
  none; 181: equal notified and expected counts on opposite routes). 68: identical -9.8 per cent changes
  with opposite dispositions kill a whole functional family. An organ, never a stump (36, 44 and 67 died
  with working pairs).
- **O2. The absolute split with the hole.** The data draws the line and the gap is empty, so the answer
  survives any cut (48: ordinary pairs top out at 7 shared orders, the ring's 137 pairs run 10 to 14,
  identical membership at every threshold from 4 to 10; 80: a return serves the whole roster or at most
  0.58).
- **O3. The free training instance.** The pattern twice: harmless where visible, decisive where not (80).
- **O4. The rung list with named violations.** Each rung's figure, its distance from the answer and the one
  fact that kills it (65: six readings and a 216-cell grid).
- **O5. Convergence asserted both ways.** State what does not move the answer (46: thirteen conventions span
  9,882 to 10,215 and all file 10,000).
- **O6. The harmful pin.** A pin the solver reads, executes correctly and is harmed by (56 v6: a
  billing-grain join makes a population vanish).
- **O7. Indeterminate rivals.** Make the rivals flat across a threshold band rather than distant, and the
  answer a set-valued count decorrelated from the obvious count (52).

## Part 5. The client's measured habits

Four runs of the current model on each of 64 accepted client tasks where it averaged under 0.70; a task
counts where the failure cost the call or its key figures, so one task can feed two. (N, M) is tasks
decided and of those under 0.50. Evidence of habits, never architectures: the top four decided 37 of 64
and share the withheld method behind a published control set the client rejects. 17 to 26 are emerging.

1. **Ships a method its own back-test failed** (11, 9): now only a filed criterion the data confirms; two
constructions fitting every control pin nothing, and the right one matches every control exactly.
2. **Counts file rows, not the real unit** (11, 7): F2; never a grouping on fuzzy text.
3. **Stops at a close, inexact match** (8, 5): ask for the runner-up and the gap so a near miss shows; a
dropped rule that moves nothing graded does not bite.
4. **Never tests its reading against the control** (7, 5): a closed case read as history; the
withheld-step form is banned.
5. **Takes the population a flag or filter suggests** (5, 3): the scope comes from a date or a join,
exceptions checkable record by record (F7).
6. **Treats a mixed segment one way** (5, 2): a clause splits it through a join, either blanket treatment
moving the headline; ask for the part counts.
7. **Uses the ready-made measure** (5, 2): a scorecard or raw rate over the defined measure (16.92 per
cent weighted against 9.90 raw), different in definition, never ranking the decision.
8. **Papers over a failed reproduction** (4, 4): pastes published values or takes the escape clause; ask
for a traceable series; withholding is wrong only when a reproducing method exists.
9. **Picks from the slate when none passes** (4, 3): a wider admissible set, the off-slate answer unique,
the veto and the status quo ruled out.
10. **Notes a binding limit as a risk** (4, 3): the policy says it binds, it derives exactly, and the ask
    is the committed figure (F3).
11. **Beats the headline trap, misses the quiet one** (4, 2): the quiet step has its own evidence and
    moves the call.
12. **Stops at the first control that passes** (3, 2): finer checks in the same record separate the
    constructions.
13. **Validates on one population, applies to another** (3, 2): the difference shows in published
    aggregates (446's form).
14. **Coarsens the segment asked about** (3, 1): the class list is spelled out at authority.
15. **Follows the requester's hunch** (3, 1): a hunch in the prompt is a roll-call risk.
16. **Lets small shortcuts flip a thin margin** (3, 1): a margin under any shortcut's shift is fragile.
17. **Guesses an attribution the data can settle** (2, 2): a latent marker, exact every time, confirmed on
    a settled case.
18. **Joins only on the visible key** (2, 1): a phone-credited order or a multi-hop roll-up, the link
    unambiguous.
19. **Breaks a big tie instead of questioning it** (2, 1): reconciling across files leaves one (a 111-way
    tie at 100 per cent).
20. **Leaves the deciding comparison unstated** (2, 1): ask for it by name.
21. **Adds exclusions nobody asked for** (2, 0): controls pin the screen set; ask for the carried counts.
22. **Solves a self-referencing rule in one pass** (1, 1): a fixed point with a ceiling that binds after
    it; ask who sits at it.
23. **Reads a closure notice as a market exit** (1, 1): the billing crosses the date onto a successor.
24. **Treats an unpublished figure as unknown** (1, 0): bound it by the total and its other component.
25. **Assumes an effect the log could measure** (1, 0): past instances are natural experiments; ask for a
    stated precision.
26. **Picks a window across a documented confounder** (1, 0): start after it ends; ask for an interval.

## Part 6. What is dead

Read before every draw: the shape, its evidence, its replacement.

- **The default construction is the golden.** 134 loop 0 (four minutes), 130 v3 (proxy 100), 62 s.29, 87
  v6b, 100 v1. Write the default path first. The textbook defaults run first: deduplication, merged
  intervals, max flow, pilot decomposition, Kaplan-Meier, explain-away and re-screen (44 v2, 58 v1, 58 v5,
  181 v1, 446 v1).
- **A reading of a term.** 133 v1 (a portal model at 100), 134's "booked again" against "return", 119, 127,
  128, 131. A fork and data semantics at once.
- **A quantity named by a filed sentence**, twelve landings (117, 119, 122, 123 x4, 124, 228, 229, 446); a
  filed rule plus a stated join route is a reading test (45), and so is a rule composed from two documents
  (76 v1); a dictionary explaining public layouts hands over the plumbing (57 v22).
- **A move visible in the schema**, six landings (117, 119, 121 x2, 124, 128; in 48 two columns named it).
- **A reflex correction**, ten landings (130 v3, 180 x4, 181, 228 x2, 230 x2).
- **A control that nominates or refutes** (120, 125; 92 v2 and 89 v5 on the portal), and **an oracle that
  ties only under the golden**, seven landings (117, 118, 120, 122 x2, 123 x2, 128), or a finer-grain

  another gap.

- **Simultaneity on a shared calendar** (49: six of eight peak in one month, 1.15x).

- **A hold reached by meta-inference** over flags each explained by a documented structure (48 v1): a hold

- **The blocked architectures** (header), and **the anti-patterns**: a shipped artifact that answers the
