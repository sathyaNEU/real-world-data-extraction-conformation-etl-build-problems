## Part 1. The laws

Every one of these was paid for. They are stated as tests you run against your own build.

### L1. A control certifies the shallow rungs and is blind to the decisive one, for a computable reason

A closed corpus, a control total or a published figure the solver can reconcile against confirms the
rungs below the answer and cannot see the last move, because of a stated structural fact about its
window. **Test.** Write the sentence *"in every control case X is zero / identical / absent, because
<structural fact>"*. If you cannot write it, the control either hands the answer over or refutes a rung
you needed it to certify.

- **70 [R]**: no hub trunk ran in the invoiced period, so the classless cut and the per-service cut
  select identical days for every invoiced consignment and the 29 of 29 validation holds for both
  models; they first part company five days after the statement's window closes.
- **60 [P]**: the criterion compares bases and double counting scales every basis alike, so the corpus
  returns the same basis under either reading.
- **72 [R]**: the closed review's window contains no site changing hands and no cross-grantee transfer,
  so its six pieces are identical to within 0.0004 of a point whether the decisive allocation is applied
  or not.

**The inverse failure, measured on two portal builds (92 v2, 89 v5):** a corpus built to *refute the
naive read* does not falsify a rival, it gets quoted back as the evidence for the rung it was meant to
hide. Build it blind, never argumentative. In the later record the same failure is the control bench
(task120, task125): a control that scores the candidate rules nominates the survivor.

### L2. Score a control row by row, never on the total

**70 [R]**: moving only the closed-day despatches forward reproduces the corpus total to within 0.12 per
cent while reproducing none of the 29 rows, GBP 12,904.77 against 16,078.88, *"exactly the sort of false
clean that gets a wrong figure signed off."* A total-level tie is not evidence. Publish the per-row hit
count for the correct rule and every rival (40 [R]: 12 of 12 against a best rival of 3 of 12).

### L3. Price the partial correction, and make sure it lands further away

A solver who half-sees the mechanism must not drift toward the answer. **67 [X]**: applying the plan
pricing only where the register makes the change loud files 35 and 32, against 37 naive and 31 correct,
so the partial application overshoots on both sides.

### L4. Two errors of opposite sign are the real enemy

**65 [P]**: across all 216 cells of the correction cross-product the nearest non-answer sits 1.4 per cent
away and reaching it takes two errors pointing opposite ways. **62 [R]**: the nearest wrong cell sits 0.59
per cent away and requires taking seats one clause excludes while removing seats another includes.
**Test.** Compute the full grid cell by cell and report the nearest cell and what it costs to reach it. A
near cell reachable only by two simultaneous filed-rule violations is safe; one reachable by one
defensible reading is a broken build.

### L5. Make the answer the extreme cell of the grid

Then no partial application reaches it by cancellation. **62 [R]** brackets instead: 14.17 per cent below
at the natural stop, 3.76 to 20.64 per cent above at the early stops, so the two failure directions sit
on opposite sides. Either shape works; a mid-grid answer does not.

### L6. Pin by identity or by exhaustion, never by fit

**55 [P]**: the vat lead is the unique integer under which all 4,462 shipped lot codes equal their lot's
vat day, zero tolerance, so no scored sweep reaches it. A fitted rule with a flat loss curve has not
produced a number, and saying so is how a rival is refused on evidence rather than preference. Under the
current standard the identity is a filed criterion the data confirms (`determinism-check` C2), never a
rule recovered from settled cases.

### L7. The naive basis is empirically excellent on the closed record

If the wrong answer is obviously careless, nothing is stumped. Every death in Part 4 where the solver
climbed free did so because a check it wrote for itself failed on the wrong path; the measured holds
(60, 65, 68, 70, 72) all left the naive basis reconciling to the closed record.

### L8. No reconciliation can see the decisive move

The strongest version is complete-partition invariance: every reading partitions the same rows, so every
total ties. **72 [R]**: *"the statewide movement is -0.96 under every one of the four bookings, the
participant-month total is identical under all four ... a pipeline that stops one step short passes every
check it would think to write for itself while naming the wrong grantee."* **67 [X]**: shares sum to every
total either way, so no reconciliation can see the structure.

## Part 2. The organs, as measured builds used them

### O1. The twin pair

Two records identical on every column a solver can see, with outcomes far apart, so no rule transferred
by resemblance reproduces the file. It forces the rule and kills every lookup at once.

| Build | The pair | Apart by |
|---|---|---|
| 36 [X] | two claims identical on every column the file shows | $18.75 against $56.25, 3.00x |
| 44 [X] | six pairs, same programme, module, case size, spring velocity and eaches per line | opposite sides of the boundary |
| 45 [R] | two campaigns, scope within 1 per cent, same carriers, same window length | 1,477 against 727 purchased |
| 46 [P] | two register-identical 2011 units surveyed the same day | 19 against 8 defect lines |
| 62 [R] | two accounts identical on every column the schedule shows | 96 seats stepped against nothing |
| 67 [X] | two accounts identical on segment, access class, basket size | mean shares far apart |
| 68 [P] | two assessments with identical -9.8 per cent value changes | opposite dispositions |

**68's refinement:** a pair with identical inputs and opposite outcomes kills an entire functional family
at once, *"no function of the headline value change can reproduce the record at all."* A twin pair in a
build that died (36, 44, 67) did its job and the death lay elsewhere, which is the point: it is one organ,
never a stump.

### O2. The absolute split with the hole in it

Never a threshold you chose: the data draws the line and the gap is empty, so the answer does not move
when the cut does. **48 [X]**: ordinary pairs top out at 7 shared orders, zero ordinary pairs at 8 or
more, the ring's 137 pairs run 10 to 14, and the identical membership returns at every threshold from 4
through 10. **80 [R]**: across all seventy settled site-years a return either serves the whole roster or
at most 0.58 of it, nothing in between.

### O3. The free training instance

**80 [R]**: the decisive pattern (a designation term dating from its criterion event while operation
lagged) appears twice. At one site it is discoverable and harmless, because the site stays open under
either dating; at another it is invisible and decisive. A solver who finds it at the harmless site has
been handed the pattern and still has to apply it where it costs something.

### O4. The rung ladder with named violations

Every measured hold ships an explicit list: each rung's figure, the per cent it sits from the answer, and
the one shipped fact or construction that kills it. **65 [P]**: six readings plus a 216-cell grid.

### O5. Convergence, asserted in both directions

State what does not move the answer, so no reviewer can find a fork left open. **46 [P]**: thirteen
conventions span 9,882 to 10,215 and all file 10,000.
