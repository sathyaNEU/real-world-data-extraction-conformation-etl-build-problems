## Three rules

Task type: Physical & Environmental Sciences, `emissions-inventory`: a measured inventory of carbon lost from drained organic soils, unit by unit, between a baseline and a repeat coring of the same monitoring points; it serves Descriptive & Distribution Analysis (each unit's loss and its place in the distribution the order is drawn on).
Domain test: the deciding quantity is each unit's carbon lost per hectare per year from the peat that formed the top metre of its 1997 cores; peat science measures it as a mass balance between repeat cores on the component oxidation cannot remove, the ash (the residue of loss on ignition), finding the 1997 material again in 2024 as the depth that holds the same cumulative ash per square metre, where the inventory's subsidence route (lowering times bulk density times carbon content) counts compression as oxidation.
