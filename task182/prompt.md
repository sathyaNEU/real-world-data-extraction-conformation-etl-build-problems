Which of our neighbours' bores has the pit drawn down past its trigger? Four landholders say theirs are sucking air. MG-02 4.1 is the test: a bore has impaired capacity when the decline the mine's take has caused, at its September 2026 level, exceeds the trigger for its aquifer. The executive team approves offers on Monday 26 October.

They will read the call off `bore_impact_assessment.xlsx`, one row for each of the 18 bores in the register: its September 2026 level in metres AHD to two decimals, its change since its baseline assessment to two decimals, the decline the mine has caused to one decimal, and impaired capacity yes or no.

With it goes a one-page note for Stephen Anderson, `make_good_recommendation.docx`: the bores and properties that get offers and how many offers that makes. For each complainant whose bore misses the test, say what lowered it in a sentence we can tell them.
