Which five dwellings should October's mitigation slot cover? It pays for acoustic treatment at five houses, picked on how much of the sound at each one is the effluent plant's. Put the answer in specific_sound_levels.csv, a row for each of the 14 monitoring positions: the plant's specific sound level in dB LAeq, the valid and the excluded assessment minutes, the one-third-octave band with the highest level over the assessment period, and whether a tone is prominent there. With it goes mitigation_shortlist.pdf, a note naming the five dwellings and the sixth with how far short it falls, a chart of all 14 levels, and for each of the six survey weeks the biogas burned in cubic metres, the blower air delivered in thousands of normal cubic metres and the centrifuge runs. The slot has to be ordered on Monday 26 October. Give levels and the margin to one decimal and everything else in whole units.
