Which five dwellings should October's mitigation slot cover? It pays for acoustic treatment at five houses, picked on how much of the sound at each one is the effluent plant's. Put the answer in specific_sound_levels.csv, a row for each of the 14 monitoring positions: the plant's specific sound level in dB LAeq, the valid and the excluded assessment minutes, the one-third-octave band with the highest level while the plant ran, and whether a tone is prominent there. With it goes mitigation_shortlist.pdf, a note naming the five dwellings and the sixth with how far short it falls, a chart of all 14 levels, and the hours the blower house ran, the biogas burned and the centrifuge runs in each of the six survey weeks. The slot has to be ordered on Monday 26 October. Levels and the margin to one decimal, hours and volumes to whole units.
