Which five dwellings should October's mitigation slot cover? It pays for acoustic treatment at five houses, picked on how much of the sound at each one is the effluent plant's. Put the answer in specific_sound_levels.csv, a row for each of the 14 monitoring positions: the plant's specific sound level in dB LAeq, the minutes of its assessment period a centrifuge was running and how many centrifuge runs reached into it. With it goes mitigation_shortlist.pdf, a note naming the five dwellings and the sixth with how far short it falls, a chart of all 14 levels, whether a tone is prominent at any position and if so where and in which band, and for each of the six survey weeks the blower air delivered in thousands of normal cubic metres, the centrifuge runs and the hours the gas engine ran. The slot has to be ordered on Monday 26 October. Give levels, the margin and engine hours to one decimal and everything else in whole units.
