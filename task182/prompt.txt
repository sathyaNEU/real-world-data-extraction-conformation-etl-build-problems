Which five dwellings should October's mitigation slot cover? It pays for acoustic treatment at five houses, picked on how much of the sound at each one is the effluent plant's. Put the answer in specific_sound_levels.csv, a row for each of the 14 monitoring positions: the plant's specific sound level in dB LAeq, the air the blowers delivered through each of its two deployments' assessment hours in thousands of normal cubic metres, and how many quarter hours of its assessment period the historian did not record the plant's state in. With it goes mitigation_shortlist.pdf, a note naming the five dwellings and the sixth with how far short it falls, a chart of all 14 levels, whether a tone is prominent at any position and if so where and in which band, and for each of the six survey weeks the blower air delivered and those unrecorded quarter hours. The slot has to be ordered on Monday 26 October. Give levels, the margin and air figures to one decimal and everything else in whole units.
