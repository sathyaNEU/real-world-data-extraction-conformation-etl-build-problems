Which five dwellings should October's mitigation slot cover? It pays for acoustic treatment at five houses, picked on how much of the sound at each one is the effluent plant's. Put the answer in specific_sound_levels.csv, a row for each of the 14 monitoring positions: the plant's specific sound level in dB LAeq, the air the blowers delivered through its assessment period in thousands of normal cubic metres, how many quarter hours of that period the plant's historian holds no reading of its own for and the longest unbroken run of them. With it goes mitigation_shortlist.pdf, naming the five dwellings and the sixth with how far short it falls, a chart of all 14 levels, whether a tone is prominent at any position and if so where and in which band, and the same three plant figures for each of the six survey weeks. The slot has to be ordered on Monday 26 October. Give levels, the margin and air to one decimal and everything else in whole units.
