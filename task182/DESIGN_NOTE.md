## ARCHITECTURE 4 RE-ROOT (stage 1, the draw)

**The diagnosis this slot's three deaths share, which is the thing worth writing down.** In every one of
them the decisive quantity was an unobserved component of an observed total: the plant's own share of the
sound at a dwelling. Architecture 1 reached it through each dwelling's lagged response to a shared driver
and shipped without stumping. Architecture 2 made the component observable in a window the pack has to
carry, and the window handed the route over, because a filed clause demanded a residual and the equipment
log labelled the only place to get one in its own words. Architecture 3 made it identifiable only through a
fit, and the fit left every graded figure open by nine to eighteen printed units while agreeing on the
names. **A latent component of a measured total is readable when a window supplies it and indeterminate
when a fit supplies it, and that world had no third source.** So architecture 4 is not another route to
that component. It has no latent component and no subtraction at all: every quantity it needs is recorded,
and what is wrong with the record is the probability with which an item enters it.

**And the world goes with it.** Physical and environmental sciences against this objective is the lane's
most crowded ground, task180, task189 and this slot all having sat on environmental monitoring exceedance,
so a fourth architecture there would be a monoculture finding waiting for the clone screen even if it drew
clean. The task type moves to general statistics and data science, exploratory data analysis and
distribution assessment, where the deciding quantity has to be the task type's own statistical object. It
is: a duration distribution, and the sampling mechanism the record of it was produced by.

**The decisive step.** A scholarly publisher ranks its twelve subject desks on the share of manuscripts
whose first decision takes longer than the house standard. The submissions platform's reporting view holds
only manuscripts that are still live, so the analytics desk's entire history of review times is a run of
Monday extracts of everything under review that day. A manuscript therefore enters that record once for
every week it was under review, which is to say with probability proportional to its own duration, and the
distribution the record carries is the duration-weighted one rather than the desks' own. The weighting
inflates each desk's long tail in proportion to its own spread of review times, so the desks whose review
times are widely spread are inflated most, and the ranking of tail shares moves. Deduplicating the rows to
distinct manuscripts, which is the hygiene step every careful solver takes, leaves the weighting entirely
untouched.

**Why it is analytical, and the three rules at the draw.** No shipped clause says how to estimate a desk's
distribution, says the snapshots are unrepresentative, or names a weight; the editorial standard files the
estimand, which is the share of a desk's manuscripts exceeding the standard, and nothing more. Nothing is
planted and no document argues a position. No field reading flips the answer: a solver who reads every
column correctly and dedupes perfectly still files the wrong slate, because the defect is in which items
the record contains rather than in what any field of it says. The domain really decides, because the
object is a distribution and its observation mechanism, which is this task type's own statistical object,
and the correction, un-weighting by each item's own duration, is the field's own treatment of
length-biased sampling.

**Why it answers architecture 3's own death.** The correction is exact arithmetic on fully observed
durations, a reciprocal-duration weight, with no fitted parameter and no choice of fit space, so the graded
figures have no live degree of freedom to begin with. That is the first thing stage 2 has to prove rather
than assume, under the rule this slot earned.

**The two rules this slot earned, carried into the draw.** A supplemental device may never touch the call's
own construction: architecture 3 failed that quietly, because its call would have read the plant's aeration
output, which is exactly where ask A2's primary device sat. And every graded figure's determinacy is
checked against the printed unit, across every route the data does not close, before any data is cut.

## ARCHITECTURE 4 ESTIMATOR SCREEN (run before a ladder number was written, and it fails)

The rule filed this hour is to enumerate every estimator the data does not close, compute the graded
summary under each, and compare the spread against the figure's own printed unit. Run on the design's own
numbers, twelve desks with medians of 4.8 to 9.0 weeks and log-spreads of 0.28 to 1.05, submissions over
104 weeks and snapshots on a fixed interval. Work in `scratchpad/r182v3/est.py` and `est2.py`. **The draw
does not survive it, and the screen also falsifies a clause of the registered card.**

**1. What the screen measures.** The graded summary is each desk's share of manuscripts whose first
decision passes the house standard of nine weeks. Routes: E0, the snapshot set summarised as it stands;
E1, reciprocal-duration weights; E2, reciprocal-appearance weights, which is the weight the snapshot grain
itself licenses; E3, reciprocal duration without the half-interval midpoint; E4, a weighted lognormal fit
with the corrected share read off it. Nothing in the pack would close any of E1 to E4.

**2. At the realistic interval the easy route is right, so there is no stump.** With weekly snapshots the
mean error of E0 against the truth is 2.9 percentage points and the slate it files is the same three desks
as the truth. The same holds at two and four weeks. The reason is structural: a manuscript is missed only
if it is decided inside one inter-snapshot gap, so with a one-week interval against five-to-nine-week
durations essentially every manuscript is caught, the set of distinct manuscripts in the snapshots is the
cohort, and there is no selection left to correct. What the snapshots do carry is one row per week per
manuscript, and that is row duplication, which deduplication removes completely. **So at a weekly interval
the mechanism is Layer 1 hygiene and not an analytical selection effect**, which is the opposite of what
the card's driver says.

**5. The route list was short by three, and completing it widens the spread.** The first pass screened E0
to E4. Length-bias correction also admits the renewal relation between the observed and the underlying
mean (E5, the moment identities for the un-weighted density read at a lognormal tail share), the
interval-censored quantile read (E6, interpolating the weighted distribution inside the snapshot bin
rather than stepping it, which is licensed because the record dates the completion only to its grain) and
a fit restricted to the longest observable items (E7). Nothing in a records pack closes any of them. The
seven-route spread per desk is 5.6 to 14.4 points at the weekly interval and 40.0 to 52.6 at eight weeks.
Dropping E7 as the least plausible still leaves 7.8 to 13.6 points wherever the correction is worth
taking. Architecture 3 was killed at nine to eighteen printed units; architecture 4 at its best available
point is 7.8 to 13.6 units against a whole point and 78 to 136 against a tenth, so it is in the same band
or worse.

**5a. The easy route is the best estimator at the realistic interval, so the ladder is inverted rather
than merely forked.** Mean absolute error against the clean truth over the twelve desks, percentage
points: at one week E0 is 2.9 and the corrected routes are 16.0 to 22.8; at two weeks E0 is 1.8 against
14.8 to 20.7. The reason is the one section 2 gives: with nothing missed there is no selection left to
correct, so a reciprocal-duration weight does not remove a bias, it introduces one, and it destroys the
tail share it is supposed to recover (D10's true 49.8 reads 16.1 to 30.6 across the seven routes). A
decisive step that makes every graded figure worse cannot be a rung at all, whatever the spread is.

**5b. Way out A, a graded quantity the correction is insensitive to, measured rather than argued.** The
first pass rejected it on a logical ground, that a quantity insensitive to the correction cannot be
decided by it. That conflates two insensitivities: to whether one corrects at all, which is fatal, and to
which corrected estimator one picks, which is exactly what is wanted. Twenty-two candidate quantities
(the top one to five as a set, the count over thresholds from 10 to 70 per cent, the full twelve-ranking)
were searched at seven intervals for the pair of conditions a way out needs, that the easy route differs
from the truth and all seven corrected routes equal it. Cells exist. None survives the world being drawn
again: over twenty seeds the best non-degenerate quantity is the top two as a set at 12 of 20, the top
three at 8 of 20, and the only quantity above 17 of 20 is the count of desks over 55 per cent, whose true
value is 0 in every seed. A count that is always zero supplies one criterion, not a rubric, and abandons
the ranked slate the draw and the card are built on. The sets that do pass pass by accident: at the weekly
interval the corrected routes reorder the desks (D03 falls from third to fifth under E1) while sitting 16
to 23 points below the truth, so a surviving top-two agreement is a rank pair preserved under a near
uniform distortion, not the correction working.

**5c. Way out B, the records closing the unstable estimators, priced and falsified by its own magnitude.**
The hypothesis is right in form: the snapshot interval does bound the shortest observable duration, so an
item seen at one snapshot has no measurable duration and the reciprocal weight needs a floor. The
magnitude kills it. The share of observed items appearing at exactly one snapshot is 7.4 per cent at the
weekly interval and 75.3 per cent at eight weeks, so at the interval the ladder needs, the floor
convention is applied to three quarters of the record and is the largest single HOW choice in the build.
Measured across four defensible floors (half an interval, one interval, one week, one day) it moves every
desk's share by up to 0.91 points at one week and 7.12 at eight, without moving the slate at either. That
is architecture 3's signature restated: the call robust, the figures forked. And the estimator the record
most naturally licenses is the wrong one, E2 at 10.9 points from the truth at eight weeks against E1's
2.3, so closing E2 with the records would be closing the better estimator out by a sentence, which files
the HOW.

**5d. The one dial.** Ladder strength (the easy route's error less the best corrected route's) and the
licensed spread are both monotone in the ratio of snapshot interval to median duration, and they rise
together: at a ratio of 0.16 the ladder is minus 13.1 points and the six-route spread 14.4; at 0.62 the
ladder is plus 2.3 and the spread 8.1; at 1.25 the ladder is plus 16.4 and the spread 11.5; at 1.56 plus
16.4 and 13.6. There is no cell where the ladder is positive and the spread is inside a printed point, let
alone the 0.4 of a unit a mid-bin figure needs. Re-tuning the interval moves along this curve; it does not
leave it.

**6. The shape this shares with architecture 3, which is the finding.** A corrected summary is an
estimator's output, and the property that makes the correction big enough to decide the call is the same
property that makes the licensed estimators disagree. Architecture 3 met this as curvature against
identifiability; architecture 4 meets it as interval against selection, and section 5d measures it as one
dial rather than two. **A decisive step that is a correction to a summary cannot carry a call whose graded
figures are that summary.** The way through, if
this slot goes a fifth time, is a call that turns on a correction while every printed figure is a quantity
the records fix exactly, so that no graded number is an estimator's output at all.

**1. The mechanism tested.** F5 composition, the one family on this ground with a measured hold (68, 83,
30, 134 v2) and the only one whose decisive step is a method for a filed quantity rather than one of the
estimand's five parts. Fourteen plants are ranked on recorded operator-error events per 100,000 rostered
duty-hours. The per-hour error rate depends on a state of the exposure that no column records and only
each operative's own roster history defines; the plants' rates within each state are alike; so the crude
rate ranks the plants by how much of their exposure sits in the high-rate states, and the plants whose
counts genuinely exceed what their own state mix implies sit mid-table. Every printed figure is an exact
integer or an exact ratio of two integers, and the correction is never printed.

**3. Model A, the state as position in a run of consecutive duties (fatigue). A ladder, at a rate curve the
field will not credit.** Eleven of thirty-six cells passed every condition, including the reflex sweep at
20/20. The best is fourteen plants, a positional error-rate spread of 12, a true plant effect of 1.60 and
240 events per 100,000 hours (about 540 events per plant per year): the crude route misses the golden slate

**The kill is the rate curve.** The passing band needs a spread of 9 to 16 between the first and the sixth
consecutive duty; the shift-work literature gives 2 to 3. At a spread of 3 the plants' mix-weighted
multipliers range only 1.40 to 2.32, a ratio of 1.66 against a plant effect of 1.60, so there is no room
for the effect to hide and no margin left over. At a spread of 6 the crude route lands the slate in 15 of
20 seeds.

**4. Model B, the state as experience on the line worked, driven by redeployment. The curve is credible and
the ladder is gone.** The power law of practice carries an order of magnitude honestly, so the state
becomes the operative's count of prior duties on the line they worked that duty, in six bands, with
relative rates 11.0, 6.2, 3.4, 2.0, 1.35 and 1.00. The driver has to be invisible, so it is redeployment
rather than turnover: a redeployed operative looks, on plant service, grade and permanent-against-agency,
exactly like an experienced one. Redeployment at 0.02 to 0.30 a year, 678 events per plant per year:
**the crude route reaches the golden slate 20 of 20 and twenty reflexes reach it,** because at a realistic
redeployment rate the share of hours at low line experience is a few per cent and nearly the same at every
plant, so the mix-weighted multiplier barely moves and the crude rate is the plant effect.

**5. Model C, the same state driven by rotation breadth. The ladder is still gone, and the driver is one
group-by away.** The only plant-level driver of the low-experience share that is not a plant attribute is
how many distinct lines each operative is rostered across, one to six. Drawn orthogonal to the visible
attributes (max abs corr 0.000) it produces a 5.27x contrast in the low-experience hour share across
plants, and still **the crude route reaches the golden slate 20 of 20.** Over three years an operative
accumulates a hundred or more duties per line, so the top band holds the overwhelming majority of hours
whatever the breadth, and a 5x contrast in a small share is a small distortion. Worse, standardising on
hours by lines-per-operative, one group-by on the roster, reaches the golden slate in 14 of 20 seeds, so
even where the ladder existed the decisive rung would be readable on the schema, and one group-by is no
rung.

**6. The one dial, and it is magnitude against credibility and invisibility together.** The mix distortion
has to exceed the true plant effect by a comfortable factor to carry a ladder. The only recorded property
of a duty whose per-hour error rate spreads far enough to do that is the operative's experience on the
task. But the share of exposure sitting at low experience is small in any steady operation, so the
distortion is small exactly where the rate curve is credible; and the two ways to make the share large are
an incredible rate curve (model A) or a staffing fact the roster shows on its face (models B and C), where
the service-length standardisation reflex reaches the golden slate 20/20 and the breadth group-by reaches
it 14/20. There is no cell where the distortion is large, the rate curve is credible and the driver is
invisible. Re-tuning moves along this curve.

- **Domain test:** what decides the slate is the determination of a specific sound level, the separation of one
  source's contribution from the residual sound by energetic subtraction, which is the acoustics
  practitioner's own method and which requires the residual to be measured with the source shut down.
- **Swap:** "which five dwellings does the plant contribute most sound to" rewritten with a hospital's nouns
  ("which five wards does the ventilation plant contribute most infections to") stops making sense, because
  infections do not add in energy, a ward's background infection rate is observable while the plant runs, and
  there is no quantity that the presence of the source makes unmeasurable.
- **Layer 1:** UTC meter exports against field sheets in Irish Standard Time; five meters rotated over
  fourteen positions on a Monday round, so no record is a round week and each carries the technician's own
  handling and field-check minutes; a field check either side of all 28 deployments, two meters reading
  outside the plan's window all survey and in opposite directions; the met station logging ten-minute means
  against the meters' minutes, its rain gauge counting tips since midnight, and wind in metres a second
  before a sensor swap and kilometres an hour after it; minutes the meter wrote its no-data value for;
  positions known by a dwelling reference, a position code and the crew's shorthand, two deployments
  standing at a height the schedule did not set; a download repeated after a card error; a process
  historian carrying a counter that wraps, an hour counter changed mid-survey, aeration air metered on two
  meters, a feed channel at a quarter of an hour against an event log to the minute, six spells with no
  rows at all and twelve more where the interface held the last value it had. Each comes from the survey's
  own equipment and paperwork and none is placed to be caught.
- **Layer 2:** field_method, own:residual-measured-in-the-source-s-own-absence. The methodological choice that
  flips the call is where the residual term of the energetic subtraction is measured: the position's own
  running record, or the minutes inside the plant's weekly process stops.
- **Decisive step:** determine the residual sound at each position from the measurements made while the plant's
  rotating equipment was down, and subtract that from the measured total, rather than correcting the total by
  a statistical background of a record the plant was in.
- **Not semantics:** every field read correctly leaves the trap standing. The plan's own words define the
  specific level as the measured level less the residual sound; the meter columns are what they say they are;
  the dB(A) unit, the time base, the drift and the exclusions can all be handled exactly and the easy route
  still subtracts a background that contains the plant, because nothing in any field or definition says that
  the plant never stops.
- **Not rule application:** the judge's caveat is that plan 3.1 and 7.1 "give most of the recipe, so the step
  is close to rule application". They do give the recipe for everything except the one term that decides the
  answer. 3.1 says the specific level is the measured level less the residual sound and defines the residual
  as the sound from sources other than the plant; 7.1 says the slot takes the dwellings in order of that
  level. Executing every filed clause end to end yields the measured level, the valid minute counts, the
  energetic arithmetic and the ranking rule, and leaves the residual term unmeasured: no clause says over
  which minutes of a six-week record the sound from sources other than the plant is to be taken, and no
  clause says the plant ever stops. **The step no filed clause performs is choosing the plant-off window as
  the residual's record**, which means reading the process log for the intervals where the blowers, the
  centrifuges and the gas engine are all down, deciding that those 240 minutes a position are the only
  record of the quantity 3.1 names, and accepting that they sit outside the assessment period 3.2 fixes for
  the measured level. A solver who executes the plan and never opens the equipment log has every figure but
  that one, and files a wrong slate; the five rungs above are what the record's own statistics give instead.
- **Analyst test:** a strong analyst who maps every deployment, applies the drift, excludes exactly the plan's
  minutes and does the energetic arithmetic correctly still takes the background from the running record and
  files NSL03, NSL04, NSL06, NSL11 and NSL12.
- **Estimand:** "the specific sound level of the effluent plant at a monitoring position is the position's
  measured sound level over the assessment period less the residual sound, in dB LAeq", filed at clause 3.1
  of `noise_management_plan.pdf`, with the population at 2.1, the assessment period at 3.2 and the slot at
  7.1.
- **No decoy:** nothing is planted and no document argues a position. The complaints register records
  complaints and no levels, the effluent file is unrelated monitoring, the plan names no background method,
  no memo or draft carries a slate and no stakeholder quotes a figure.
- **No certified replay:** no certified, published or closed figure is shipped for a method to reproduce. The
  residual is measured, not recovered: the plant's event log and the meter records are both primary, and the
  method follows from the fact that the two cannot be separated in a record that holds both.
- **Lesson:** a quantity defined as a difference has to be measured where the difference is observable, and the
  windows that make it observable come from the operating record of the thing being measured, not from the
  statistics of the measurement. No build on file teaches it: the nearest, task263, inverts mixtures of many
  sources by mass balance, and task186 judges a change against a shared cycle.
