# task115: Delahunt Tea's March 2027 Big Deals offer to Tullan Grocers

## Tags

**Domain:** Marketing & Consumer Research, pricing and promotion (a tea brand's offer into a symbol group's leaflet event, forecast from the group's scan data).
**Analytical objective:** Forecasting & Predictive Modeling.

## 1. Final Recommendation

**Offer Tullan Save 40% on Delahunt Original 160s for the March 2027 Big Deals cycle.**

It is forecast at 2.7 times base over the three weeks against Tullan's floor of 2.5, the cheapest of the four deals that clears. Not sitting March out, which Delahunt's own Save 20% and Save 25% deals point to, because none of them took the 160s down to Tullan's own-brand price. Not half price, which is the only deal clearing if the switch is read one for one off own brand's lost packs, because the deal line sells twice what own brand loses and half price costs about twice as much to fund. Not Save a third or €2.40 off, both 1.7, which stay above own brand in every store.

## 2. Critical Components

1. Where another brand's 160s deal price is at or below the store's own-brand 160s price, that deal line sells extra packs equal to **0.7** of the store's own-brand base, twice the packs own brand loses
2. Save 40% takes the 160s to **€4.19** in the stores that sell them at €6.99, below own brand's **€4.39** there, and leaves them above own brand everywhere else (**€3.89** against **€3.79**, **€3.59** against **€3.39**)
3. Base is **4,444** packs a week over the participating scanning stores
4. Save 40% forecasts **2.7** times base, Save a third and €2.40 off **1.7**, half price **4.4**

## 3. Step-by-Step Solution

1. Took each participating store's base per the brief, section 3 (w/c 29 June to 21 September 2026, weeks with a file and off deal, Appendix B stores left out): 4,444 packs a week.
2. Measured the deal lift in TullanInsight_tea_store_week_2024W41-2026W40.csv against each line's 13 weeks before its cycle, in store-weeks whose deal price stayed above the own-brand line of the same bag count: Save 40% alone lifts a line to 1.7 times base.
3. In the cycles where a deal price reached the own-brand line of the same bag count, read the deal line's own units above that lift in exactly those store-weeks: 0.7 of the store's own-brand base, while own brand lost about a third of its normal sales.
4. Tested each March deal store by store at current prices, held to 5 April 2027 per the brief: only Save 40% (€4.19 against €4.39) and half price (every store) reach own brand.
5. Forecast each store at base times the lift for the deal's depth, plus 0.7 of its own-brand base where the deal reaches own brand: Save a third 1.7, Save 40% 2.7, half price 4.4, €2.40 off 1.7.
6. Placed stores in their March 2027 regions from tullan_members_jan2026.xlsx, the 6 July 2026 changes in tullan_area_structure_from_6_July_2026.docx and member_news_2026.docx (0147 trading as 0388, 0119 now Swords Rivervalley), without the May 2027 moves.
7. Checked the 2026 debit notes against the scan feed, the deal terms and the manual returns: €217 over-billed on BD26-04 and €4,670 on BD26-13.
8. Recommendation: offer Save 40% at 2.7 times base, about 36,000 packs.
