Should PROBE-M become mandatory at every en-route planner position from 1 April 2027, on the share of STCA activations its use prevented in the 2026 trial against the one in five the acceptance criteria require? Set out the call and that share in probe_decision_2027.docx with a chart the executive team can read at a glance. In probe_trial_figures.xlsx give each sector's flight hours and its STCA activations per 1,000 flight hours, counted as the safety performance report counts them, for each month of the trial in New Zealand time, and the flights each sector handled over the trial. The share is wanted to the nearest five per cent, hours to the nearest hour, rates to one decimal and flights as whole numbers.
