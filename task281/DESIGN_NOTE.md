# task281 design note

Architecture 1, drawn 2026-10-24. Wave 6, Descriptive lane. Seed DA31's world, decision and quantity are kept (relief positions funded for the next fiscal year and placed across eight round-the-clock units of four titles, sized on the overtime they remove); the seed's mechanism rows are decoy-era design and are set aside, and the world is re-set from a city to a state government at the guard's axis bans (below). The State of Merrimont's FY2028 budget funds 60 overtime-relief positions for its round-the-clock units: three correctional facilities, the two State Police communications centers, the forensic hospital's security nursing service and two highway maintenance garages that run around the clock. The Department of Budget and Management's workforce analysis desk places them under the State's relief coverage standard, which sizes a unit's relief complement on its backfill need in the year planned for, met in full in every week but four; the Secretary of Budget and Management signs the placement into the FY2028 allowance.

## Tried and rejected

- Seed DA31 as filed (positions scored on the overtime cost they avoid and placed by greedy marginal value, `proto1.py` to `proto3.py`): the greedy cut fell in near-ties (0.0 to 0.5 per cent between the last position placed and the next) on every seed, so any convention moved positions between units; the call moved to each unit's need under a coverage standard, a quantile of its own weekly distribution, so no unit's answer turns on another's.
- The relief staff's own seasonal leave (relief officers taking leave in the same weeks the unit's staff do) as the conjunction's second half: with and without it the placement was identical (`proto1.py`, `proto2.py`), so it was dropped for the first-year availability and the position control's vacancies.
- A re-world to pharmacoepidemiology (the share of a region's patients still treated, read from the reverse waiting-time distribution of their dispensings rather than one defined daily dose a day, `wtd1.py`): under real dose spread the mixture estimate missed the truth by 2 to 8 per cent, no better than the percentile duration rules the same literature publishes, so the field's estimator could not carry a determinate call.
