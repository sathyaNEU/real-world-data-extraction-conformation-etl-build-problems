Which programme on the safety steering committee's list should Mount Bracken fund for 2027 against the rise in vehicle interaction incidents? The committee paper, vi_programme_paper_2027.docx, carries the call, the mine's vehicle interaction incidents in July to October 2026 and in the same months of 2025, and a chart of the monthly count from January 2025. Beside it, vi_workings_2026.xlsx gives, for each month from July to October 2026, the light-vehicle trips into the pits and how many of them were made by vehicles whose collision avoidance tag the gate did not detect, and, for each pit ramp and each of those months, the times haul trucks went over the speed limit in force there and how many of those times peaked more than 5 km/h over it. Every count is a whole number.
