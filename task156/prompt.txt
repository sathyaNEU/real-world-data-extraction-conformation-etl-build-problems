Can the Ngaio Road subdivision connect to the Riverbank Road pump station in 2027 and keep the station within its consented wet weather overflows, or does it have to wait for the storage upgrade? The workbook, ps03_ngaio_road_connection_2027.xlsx, should give the station's average wet weather overflows a year over the consent's replay without the subdivision, with it, and with it after the upgrade; the catchment's residential water use per dwelling per day in each quarter from January 2024 to September 2026; and each other pump station's wet weather overflows and spill hours in 2024 and in 2025, with the network's emergency overflows in each year. The report to council, council_report_ngaio_road_2027.docx, should make the recommendation on those figures, and replay_overflows_2004-2023.png should chart each replay year's overflows with and without the subdivision against the limit. Overflows a year and spill hours are whole numbers, water use to the nearest litre.
