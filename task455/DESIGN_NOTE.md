- **Pairing:** Survey Research & Official Statistics / census-register-administrative-data-quality / ETL, the client's pattern census-register-administrative-data-quality/2 (net and gross classification error differ). The Wendmoor Valley Recovery Foundation holds Wendmoor County's share of the opioid settlement. It funded phase one of the county health department's Overdose Reduction Initiative (2024 to 2026) and releases phase two when its trustees accept the department's certification that the county's residents' opioid overdose deaths in 2026 were at least 20 per cent below their 2021 to 2023 average. The department conforms the state death file, the medical examiner's review of 2025 and the population estimates into the certification and a yearly bridge. In official statistics this is register quality: the death certificates are an administrative register whose classification error is measured against a gold standard (the medical examiner's full review of one year), and each year's count is corrected for the gross error in both directions.
- **Why not another task type:** no other ETL-serving task type holds a cause-of-death register (payment claims is task458's, data integration task456's, emissions inventories task452's and task459's); the register-quality type is the field's own for vital registration.
- **Shape:** 03 (a bridge between two totals: for each year, the deaths certified as opioid overdoses, the opioid deaths the certificates missed, the stimulant deaths they listed with an incidental opioid, and the corrected count). Arithmetic: the call (1), the corrected 2026 count and the 2021 to 2023 average and the change (3), the bridge's four items for each of the four years the target reads (16), then the asks (the remaining years of the bridge and an age-adjusted rate series): about 40 criteria.
- **Strategy:** composition_mix (G6 decisive, G12 and G16 behind it). The certificates miss about one opioid overdose death in eight (certified as an unspecified drug) and list an opioid in about a quarter of stimulant overdose deaths where the medical examiner finds the opioid incidental. Stimulant deaths roughly doubled from 2021 to 2026 while other drug deaths fell and opioid deaths fell by more than a fifth. The 2025 review measures each true class's chance of each certificate category. Any multiplicative correction (a net ratio, the review's predictive values) leaves the percent change where the certificates put it; the standard two-by-two correction with one false-listing rate over all non-opioid drug deaths keeps the false listings level as the pool that produces them grows; only the false listings scaled to each year's stimulant deaths, solved from each year's certificate categories, meet the target.
- **World:** an unnamed state's Wendmoor County (invented), about 420,000 residents, around 380 drug overdose deaths a year at the baseline. The pack carries the state vital records death file extract for county residents and deaths occurring in the county, 2019 to 2026, with original and amended records and multiple-cause ICD-10 codes (the spine); the medical examiner's 2025 review; the initiative's grant agreement and outcome measure; the county's population estimates by age; the 2000 U.S. standard population; a data dictionary, a manifest and distractors. Forcing fact: phase one's grant period closed with 2026 and the trustees meet on 17 June 2027.
- **Forum and forcing event:** trustees_or_governors (the foundation's trustees accept the certification), period_close (the close of phase one). Organisation foundation_or_funder; role statistical_office_head (the department's epidemiology and vital statistics manager). Personas from `guard.py names --geo "United States" --seed 455`: Tammie Mcneil, Jesse Mcdonald, Leah Jackson.
- **Data-native sentence:** the false listings come from stimulant overdose deaths in which fentanyl was found but did not cause death, a class that roughly doubled while opioid deaths fell, so the certificates carry a growing block of non-opioid deaths into the opioid count, and only an error rate tied to the stimulant deaths that produce them removes it.
