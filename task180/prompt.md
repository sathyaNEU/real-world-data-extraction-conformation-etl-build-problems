Three signals came out of the third-quarter screen of our spontaneous reports: falls with tasorexant (Somnevo), acute kidney injury with corinurad (Uralexa) and acute pancreatitis with velaglutide (Gravera). The medicines safety committee meets on Thursday 22 October and has room to open one formal assessment. SOP-PV-14 sets out what counts as each event and who counts as a new user. Use the registry extracts to tell me which signal the registries support, that is, where starting the medicine raises the risk of the event in the month after the start.

`signal_refinement_note.docx` goes to the committee. It names the signal and, for that medicine, gives the rate ratio for the month after the start with its 95 per cent interval, the admissions for the event in that month among people who started it in 2025, and how many of those admissions the medicine caused.

`new_users_by_year.csv` has one row per medicine and calendar year, 2022 to 2025, with the number of new users.
