From April 2027 to March 2028 the mobile substation is ours for three windows, so the capital programme carries three internal inspections and no more. The board settles on 28 October which transformers get them. Who qualifies is set by our fleet DGA standard, ES-DGA-02, and Barry's oil results run to the September sampling round. The call is three transformers, in the order we take them.

The board paper is `inspection_slate_2027-28.docx`. It opens with the three, each with its substation, condition code, severity score to two decimals and the gas behind it. Then come the two tables the board asked for last time. The first lists every transformer with an in-oil tap changer, its tap operations since its last tap-changer service and whether it is past its service interval. The second lists every transformer whose oil was wetter than 30 per cent relative saturation at its latest sample, with the figure to one decimal.

For the engineers there is `dga_fleet_review.csv`, one row per transformer: its code, its severity, whether it is on the slate, its tap operations since service where it has an in-oil tap changer, and its relative saturation at its latest sample.
