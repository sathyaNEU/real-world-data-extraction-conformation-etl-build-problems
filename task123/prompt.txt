Where in the complex, if anywhere, does an apartment riser need re-phasing or a larger rising main before the approved chargers and cooktop conversions are connected in 2027? The workbook riser_capacity_2027.xlsx names those risers, each with its most heavily loaded phase's demand, existing and added, as a share of its rising main's per-phase rating, and the undesignated riser nearest the line with its share; for every riser it also gives its apartments' electricity use in July 2026, the number of charger requests its apartments' owners have lodged, and the share of its apartments' time in the week of 13 to 19 July with the supply voltage outside the statutory limits. riser_phase_loading_2027.svg shows one bar per riser for that share of rating, with the standard's limit drawn across. Rating shares are whole per cents, electricity use is in MWh to two decimals and the voltage shares are per cents to two decimals.
