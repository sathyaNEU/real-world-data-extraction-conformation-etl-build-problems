Which of the union's member comuni, if any, qualify for its 2027 peak-month services co-financing? Set out in august_2026_guest_nights.xlsx each member comune's August 2026 guest nights and nights per resident, the qualifying comuni and the comune closest to the line, and add each member comune's guest nights for January to April 2026, the net bed-place occupancy of each type of establishment on the regional register in October 2025, and the tourist tax the member comuni were due in each quarter of 2025 in euros; peak_cofinancing_2027_note.pdf gives the assembly the decision with a bar chart of every comune's nights per resident against the qualifying line. Guest nights are to the nearest 100, nights per resident to one decimal, occupancy to one decimal of a per cent and tax to the nearest 100 euros.
